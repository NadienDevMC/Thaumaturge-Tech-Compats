> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. The machine is Avaritia's Neutronium Compressor; this mod only makes it faster and more generous.

## What it is

The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_neutron_compressor), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_neutron_compressor) and [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_neutron_compressor) replace the Densest Neutronium Compressor. They use Avaritia's compressor recipes, including those for singularities (see the Singularities page of the Avaritia chapter).

## What it does

Every tick the compressor runs its normal work **speed times** (2, 4 or 8 by default). Whenever it consumes materials or adds to its output during a step, it adds extra copies of the recipe result, so the output is multiplied by the production multiplier (1, 2 or 4 by default), with the same materials.

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Speed multiplier (per tier) | times the Densest compressor works each tick, 1 to 64 |
| Production multiplier (per tier) | multiplier of the items given per finished recipe, 1 to 64 |

## Step by step

1. Place the compressor and open it with right click.
2. Choose the recipe and feed the materials as you would in Avaritia's compressor.
3. Collect the output from the output slot.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving the output slot full: extra copies are capped by the room left in the slot.
- Expecting the multiplier to save materials: it multiplies the result, the cost per recipe is the same.
