> [!NOTE]
> Needs **Avaritia** (through the Avaritia Essentia Smelteries). They can be switched off in the device config. If off, they behave exactly like the Advanced Alchemical Furnace.

## What it is

The Advanced Alchemical Furnace breaks items into essentia but waits between items and wastes part of the heat, perditio and aqua it burns. These furnaces keep the same walls but use an Avaritia smeltery as their heart, so they work faster and waste less.

| Furnace | Pause between items | Gives back (heat, perditio, aqua) |
|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_alchemical_furnace) | divided by 2 | 25% |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_alchemical_furnace) | divided by 4 | 50% |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_alchemical_furnace) | divided by 10 | 90% |

They draw and release essentia exactly like the Advanced Alchemical Furnace. Jade shows the speed and the savings.

## How to get it

You do not craft the furnace directly: you wake it with **Salis Mundus** over a smeltery.

1. Build the structure of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace: a three by three base with two layers.
2. Put the matching Avaritia smeltery at its centre: [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_smelter), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_smelter) or [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_smelter).
3. Sprinkle Salis Mundus over it. The structure comes to life as the Alchemical Furnace of that tier.

Research **Avaritia Alchemical Furnaces** first. Since each smeltery needs the one before it, each furnace needs the one before it too.

## Common mistakes

- Using a smeltery of the wrong tier at the centre.
- Breaking a part of the structure: the furnace turns back into its smeltery.
- Forgetting that the structure is the one of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace, not a new shape.

## Notes

- Its values are adjusted in the mod's devices configuration.
