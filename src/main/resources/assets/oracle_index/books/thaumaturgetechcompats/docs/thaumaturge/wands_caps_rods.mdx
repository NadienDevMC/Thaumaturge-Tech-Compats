## What it is

A **wand** is always one **rod** and two **caps**. The rod holds the vis, in six pools, one per primal aspect. The caps focus the vis as it is spent and decide how much each spell or craft really costs.

| Part | Effect (from the Thaumonomicon) |
|---|---|
| Wooden rod | holds 25 vis of each primal |
| Greatwood Rod | holds 50 of each primal |
| Silverwood Rod | holds 100 of each primal, the most of any rod that fits in a wand |
| Elemental rods (Blazing, Icy, Obsidian, Quartz, Reed, Bone) | hold 75 of each primal and slowly refill their own primal, up to a tenth of the store |
| Iron Cap | wastes a tenth of the vis it channels |
| Copper Cap | costs a tenth more, except for ordo and perditio |
| Gold Cap | no waste |
| Charged Silver Cap | aer, terra, ignis and aqua cost a little less |
| Charged Thaumium Cap | a tenth less for every aspect |
| Charged Void Metal Cap | four fifths of the normal cost |

- **Staves**: two rods around a primal charm. They hold far more vis (125 per primal for greatwood, 175 for elemental rods, 250 for silverwood) but cannot sit in the Arcane Workbench.
- **Sceptres**: three caps, a rod and a primal charm. They hold half again as much vis as the same wand and pay a tenth less for everything.
- **Foci** are crystals socketed into a wand to cast spells. You grow a blank focus in the crucible and imprint its pattern in the **Focal Manipulator**. Each pattern adds **complexity**, and complexity sets the vis cost and the cooldown of the focus. The **Focus Pouch** carries your spare foci.

## Step by step

1. Complete Discovering Auromancy. Your first wand (iron caps and a wooden rod) is the only one you can make on a normal crafting table.
2. Fill it: hold it up to condense the aura around you, hold right-click on an aura node to tap it, kill creatures (they drop motes of primal energy) or harvest vis-bearing plants.
3. Research better rods and caps. Silver, Thaumium and Void Metal caps are made inert on the Arcane Workbench and then charged on the Infusion Altar.
4. Assemble the better wand on the Arcane Workbench, which costs vis.
5. Make a blank focus in the crucible, imprint it on the Focal Manipulator and socket it into the wand.

## Good to know

- While you hold a wand, an indicator shows its vis; the tooltip lists each pool.
- A Recharge Pedestal refills vis items from the aura and, for wands, from nodes within 8 blocks.
