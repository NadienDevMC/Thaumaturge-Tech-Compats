> [!NOTE]
> Edible items can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, need a restart). All the originals come from vanilla Minecraft and Thaumaturge, so no other mod is needed.

## What it is

An **edible item** is a copy of a tool, weapon, ingot, gem, door or the Goggles of Revealing that you can **eat**. It is a food item only: it is not a working tool, weapon or door. Eating it gives status effects that depend on the kind of item and on its tier. They have their own creative tab, "Edible Items", and are named "Edible Diamond Pickaxe", "Edible Oak Door" and so on.

## How to get it

Craft the original item together with **sugar**, with no particular layout (a shapeless recipe):

> original item + [](@minecraft:sugar) = edible copy

Both the item and the sugar are used up. For example, a Diamond Pickaxe and a Sugar give an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_diamond_pickaxe).

## How to eat it

1. Hold the edible item and right click and hold, like any food. It can **always be eaten**, even with a full hunger bar.
2. It restores a small amount of food: 1 hunger point at the lowest tier, growing by 1 per tier, and saturation 0.3 growing by 0.2 per tier.
3. It gives its status effects, 30 seconds at the lowest tier and 15 seconds more per tier.

The effect level also grows with the tier (see the Effects and Tiers page).

## Stack sizes

| Kind | Stack |
| --- | --- |
| Tools, weapons, goggles | 16 |
| Ingots, gems, doors | 64 |

## Step by step

1. Check the effect table for the item you want to eat.
2. Craft it with a sugar at a crafting table.
3. Eat it when you want the effect (for example Haste before mining, with a Pickaxe).

## Common mistakes

- Trying to mine with an edible pickaxe: it is food, not a tool.
- Crafting the original away without meaning to: the recipe consumes the original item.
- Expecting enchanted or damaged tools to keep their enchantments: the edible copy is a new simple item.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
