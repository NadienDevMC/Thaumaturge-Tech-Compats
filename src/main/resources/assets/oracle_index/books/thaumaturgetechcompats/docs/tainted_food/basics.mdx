> [!NOTE]
> Vanilla foods need no other mod. The **Ultimate Stew** and **Cosmic Meatballs** versions need **Avaritia**. Tainted food can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, needs a restart). Settings: the tainted food config.

## What it is

The flux that spoils a field can also be coaxed into a loaf of bread. A **tainted food** is a copy of an ordinary food that fills **more hunger and more saturation** than the original and keeps its effects, but whoever eats it is struck by **Flux Taint** for a while. The names follow the pattern "Tainted Bread", "Tainted Apple" and so on.

## How to get it

Tainted foods are brewed in a **crucible**:

1. Research "Tainted Food" in the Thaumonomicon.
2. Fill a crucible with enough **Vitium** (the amount depends on the food, see the next page).
3. Throw the original food into the crucible. It is the **catalyst** and turns into the tainted food.

For example, a crucible with 6 Vitium and a Bread gives a Tainted Bread.

## What eating it does

- Hunger and saturation of the original, multiplied by 1.5 each.
- The effects of the original food still apply.
- Flux Taint for 10 seconds (200 ticks), level one, every time (chance 1.0). The effect, duration, level and chance are all configurable.

## Step by step

1. Fill the crucible with the Vitium you need (dissolve items that carry the Vitium aspect).
2. Add the original food as the catalyst.
3. Collect the tainted food and eat it when you can afford the Flux Taint.

## Common mistakes

- Dropping the food in before the crucible holds enough Vitium: it needs the full amount of the recipe.
- Using a food that is not on the list: only the foods of the next page have a tainted version.
- Eating them as a regular meal: the Flux Taint comes with every bite.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's tainted food configuration.
