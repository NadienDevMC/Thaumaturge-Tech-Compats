## What it is

The Infinity Mnemonic Matrix is a Mnemonic Matrix of Infinity: placed against a Thaumatorium it lets it remember more formulas at the same time. It stacks with ordinary matrices and loses no formula.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Simultaneous formulas added (default) | 10 |

## How to use it

1. Research Mnemonic Matrices of Avaritia.
2. Infuse it over the matrix of the tier before, with the Runic Matrix of its tier at the center of the altar.
3. Place it against the Thaumatorium like an ordinary Mnemonic Matrix.
4. Look at it with Jade to see how many formulas it adds.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Mnemonic Matrices of Avaritia
> Each matrix is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it:
> - The Crystal Matrix one starts from an ordinary mnemonic matrix.
> - The Neutronium one starts from the Crystal Matrix one.
> - The Infinity one starts from the Neutronium one.
> They are placed against a thaumatorium exactly like the ordinary ones, and they stack with them. By default they allow 4, 6 and 10 formulas at the same time.
