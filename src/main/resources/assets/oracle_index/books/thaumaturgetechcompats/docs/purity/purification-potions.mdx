> [!NOTE]
> Each potion can be switched off in the purity section of the compatibilities config. The Biome Purification Potion needs the Purification Splash Potion. Both are brewed in a crucible and need the research of the **Purity** tab.

## Purification Splash Potion

### What it is

Thrown at the ground, the [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:purification_potion) shatters into a clean mist. Every infected creature standing in it returns to its natural self, health, temper and all: pets, livestock and monsters alike. It also drives the flux sickness out of the blood before it can finish a creature off.

| Setting (config) | Default |
|---|---|
| Radius | 4 blocks |
| Cure chance | 100% |
| Health restored | 4 |
| Cures pets / peaceful mobs / monsters / players | all on |
| Removes the flux taint effect | on |
| Blacklist of entity types | empty |

### How to get it

Crucible: Ordo 40, Victus 40, Aqua 30, Lux 30, with an **Ethereal Bloom** as the catalyst. Research: *Observe a taint creature* first, then *Purification Splash Potion*.

## Biome Purification Potion

### What it is

The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:biome_purification_potion) does in one breath the work of an Ethereal Bloom. Thrown at the ground, it bursts into a pale green mist: the tainted biome returns to what the land was; fibres, crust, soil and growths wither away; flux goo and gas wash out; and taint seeds caught in the mist are destroyed. It also draws flux out of the aura of the chunks it touches.

| Setting (config) | Default |
|---|---|
| Radius / vertical radius | 12 / 8 blocks |
| Flux drained per chunk touched | 15 |
| Block clean chance | 100% |
| Destroys taint seeds | yes (prime taint seeds: no) |
| Use cooldown | 40 ticks |

### How to get it

Crucible: Ordo 80, Victus 60, Aqua 60, Lux 60, Auram 50, Praecantatio 40, Vitreus 40, Herba 30, with a **Purification Splash Potion** as the catalyst.

## Step by step

1. Research the potion and fill a crucible with the aspects.
2. Drop the catalyst in and collect the potion.
3. Throw it at the ground where the creatures or the tainted land are.

## Common mistakes

- Throwing it far from the target: the radius is only 4 blocks for the creature potion.
- Expecting the Biome Potion to destroy prime taint seeds: that is off by default.
- Forgetting the catalyst: the Biome Potion needs a finished Purification Splash Potion.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
