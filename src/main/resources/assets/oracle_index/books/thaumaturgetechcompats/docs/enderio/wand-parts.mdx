> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Ender IO**. The wand parts (and the whole Ender IO integration) can be switched off in the compatibilities config; on by default, game restart needed. Numbers are in the Ender IO wand parts config.

## What it is

Ten materials, ten rods and ten caps for wands of Thaumaturge. A wand is a rod plus a cap, and **any rod fits any cap**. The rod sets the vis capacity; the cap sets the cost of every spell (and each cap makes its own special aspects cheaper still).

| Material | Rod capacity | Cap cost modifier | Special aspects (cheaper) | Special modifier |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Redstone Alloy | 40 | 1.0 | potentia | 0.9 |
| Conductive Alloy | 55 | 0.98 | potentia, motus | 0.88 |
| Pulsating Alloy | 70 | 0.96 | motus, alienis | 0.86 |
| Energetic Alloy | 85 | 0.94 | potentia, lux | 0.84 |
| Dark Steel | 100 | 0.92 | tenebrae, praemunio | 0.82 |
| Soularium | 115 | 0.9 | spiritus, mortuus | 0.8 |
| Pulsating Crystal (glows) | 130 | 0.88 | motus, vitreus | 0.78 |
| Vibrant Alloy (glows) | 145 | 0.86 | lux, potentia, motus | 0.76 |
| Vibrant Crystal (glows) | 160 | 0.84 | lux, vitreus | 0.74 |
| End Steel (glows) | 175 | 0.8 | alienis, tenebrae, praemunio | 0.7 |

For reference: Thaumaturge's wooden rod holds 25, greatwood 50, silverwood 100; its thaumium cap is 0.9 and its void cap 0.8.

## How to get it

Research **Ender IO Wands** in the Tech category (needs the Greatwood Rod and Thaumium Cap). The ingredient of each material is its Ender IO ingot (or the crystal for the crystal materials).

- **Redstone, Conductive and Pulsating Alloy**: arcane workbench. Rod: three ingots on a diagonal (40 vis). Cap: five ingots in a cap shape, three on top and two below (60 vis).
- **All the others**: infusion altar. The catalyst is the **rod (or cap) of the material before it** in the table, plus four ingots (four crystals for the crystal materials). Aspects scale with the step: praecantatio, potentia and motus for rods; praecantatio, metallum and potentia for caps.

## Step by step

1. Craft the first three rods and caps at the arcane workbench.
2. Infuse the next material over the previous part, one by one.
3. Assemble a rod and a cap in the wand assembly of Thaumaturge.
4. Craft cost of an assembled wand = rod craft cost x cap craft cost (rod 4 to 13, cap 3 to 12).

## Common mistakes

- Skipping a material: each infusion needs the previous part as catalyst.
- Expecting different rods and caps not to fit: any rod fits any cap.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Ender IO wand parts configuration.
