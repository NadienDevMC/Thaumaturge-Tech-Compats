> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of **Avaritia**. The research is **Material Studies: Avaritia**.

## What it is

Plates of crystal matrix, neutronium and infinity are the base of three new golem materials in the Golem Press, and an ingredient of many other things in this mod (smelteries, stabilizers, wand caps and rods, foci).

| Material | Golem traits |
|---|---|
| Crystal Matrix | light, fireproof, blastproof |
| Neutronium | heavy, armored, fireproof, blastproof |
| Infinity | armored, brutal, fireproof, blastproof, self-repairing |

## How to get it

Plates are crafted in a normal crafting grid, three plates per craft:

| Plate | Recipe |
|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_plate) | three Crystal Matrix Ingots in a row |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_plate) | three Neutron Ingots in a row, with one Crystal Matrix Plate below the middle |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_plate) | three Infinity Ingots in a row, with one Neutronium Plate below the middle |

## Step by step

1. Finish the research **Material Studies: Avaritia**.
2. Craft the plates.
3. Open the Golem Press (see the Golem Press ponder scene) and supply it with Machina essentia through the ports below the press.
4. Pick the material: the plate of that material and a **Complex Arcane Mechanism** are the components of the golem.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping a tier: neutronium plates need a crystal matrix plate and infinity plates need a neutronium plate.
- Forgetting the Complex Arcane Mechanism when pressing the golem.
- Forgetting the Machina essentia supply of the Golem Press.
