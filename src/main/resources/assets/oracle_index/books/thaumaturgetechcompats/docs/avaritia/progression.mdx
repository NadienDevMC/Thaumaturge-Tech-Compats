> [!NOTE]
> This chapter needs the **Avaritia** mod. Its ingots (Crystal Matrix, Neutron and Infinity) are the raw material of everything below.

## What it is

Thaumaturge Tech Compats lets you pour the three Avaritia metals into Thaumaturge's infusion magic. They form a ladder of three tiers, and every upgrade of the ladder is built from the one before it:

| Tier | Ingot | Plate | Runic Matrix it needs | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crystal Matrix | Crystal Matrix Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_plate) | plain Runic Matrix | Solar |
| Neutronium | Neutron Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_plate) | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_infusion_matrix) | Nuclear |
| Infinity | Infinity Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_plate) | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_infusion_matrix) | Cosmic |

The Infinity tier also asks for an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) in almost every recipe.

## The golden rule

Everything of these tiers that is made by infusion can only be infused on an altar whose **center is the Runic Matrix of its own tier** (stones and matrices themselves use the tier before). That matrix brings its own dangerous instability to the craft. See the Matrices chapter for how to build that altar safely. The rule can be switched off per tier in the infusion config (the tier rule settings).

## Step by step

1. Craft the plates (see Plates and Golems) and an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) (see Terminus, Pearl and Akashic Records).
2. Build the plain altar, then infuse the Crystal Matrix Runic Matrix, then the Neutronium one, then the Infinity one.
3. For each tier, put the matching Runic Matrix at the center of the altar and infuse the upgrades of that tier.
4. Wake pillars with the Salis Mundus variants if you want a faster, cheaper altar.

## Common mistakes

- Infusing an upgrade on the wrong center matrix: the altar refuses the recipe and the tooltip of the item tells you which Runic Matrix it needs.
- Forgetting that each tier is a catalyst for the next: you cannot skip Crystal Matrix.
- Underestimating the instability: Neutronium and Infinity altars can explode, pour flux and teleport you.
