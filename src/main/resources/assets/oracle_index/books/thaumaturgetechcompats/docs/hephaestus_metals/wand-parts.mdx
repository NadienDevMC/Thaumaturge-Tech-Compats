> [!NOTE]
> Needs only Thaumaturge (the metals come from this mod). The research is **Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson**. Any rod fits any cap.

## What it is

Each of the four metals makes a wand **rod** (stores vis) and a wand **cap** (makes spells cheaper). The stronger the metal, the more vis the rod holds and the cheaper each spell is with the cap. The cap makes the spells of its own two aspects cheaper still.

| Metal | Rod vis capacity | Rod craft cost | Cap spell cost multiplier | Cap multiplier for its aspects | Cap aspects |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rifting | 150 | 10 | 0.78 | 0.68 | Vacuos, Alienis |
| Crimson | 250 | 14 | 0.70 | 0.60 | Perditio, Humanus |
| Fibric | 400 | 18 | 0.60 | 0.50 | Fabrico, Vitium |
| Salis | 800 | 24 | 0.45 | 0.35 | Praecantatio, Potentia |

The rods and caps glow (Crimson excepted).

## How to get them

Each part is made in two steps: shape the **inert** part, then wake it.

1. **Inert rod**: arcane workbench, three ingots of the metal in a column (40 vis).
2. **Inert cap**: arcane workbench, five nuggets of the metal in a row of three over a row of two (90 vis).
3. **Wake the part**: infusion altar with the inert part as the catalyst, four **Salis Mundus** and the aspects of the metal (potentia and auram plus the two aspects of the metal, 18 each for Rifting, 22 Crimson, 26 Fibric, 32 Salis).

| Metal | Instability (rod / cap) |
|---|---|
| Rifting | 8 / 8 |
| Crimson | 8 |
| Fibric | 4 |
| Salis | 4 |

## Step by step

1. Make the ingots and nuggets (see the metals page).
2. Craft the inert rod and cap at an arcane workbench.
3. Infuse each one with four Salis Mundus and the aspects.
4. Assemble the wand with the rod and the cap at an arcane workbench like any wand.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to use the inert parts as real parts: they are only the catalyst for the infusion.
- Forgetting the Salis Mundus (four per infusion).
- Expecting the rod to set the spell cost: the cap sets it.
