> [!NOTE]
> The Aura Converters, and each tier on its own, can be switched off in the compatibilities config. The figures live in the Aura Converter config.

## What it is

An Aura Converter drains vis from the aura of its chunk and turns it into **FE**, which it hands to every neighbouring block that accepts energy. It never takes energy in. It is a generator that eats the aura.

| Converter | FE per vis | Output per tick (max) | Buffer |
|---|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_aura_converter) | 250,000 | 250,000 FE | 10,000,000 FE |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_aura_converter) | 1,000,000 | 1,000,000 FE | 100,000,000 FE |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_aura_converter) | 560,000,000 | 500,000,000 FE | 10,000,000,000 FE |

Limits that protect the aura (all configurable):

- Every vis it converts leaves **0.1 flux** in the aura.
- It never drains the aura below **25% of its base** value; at that point it shows *No vis*.
- All the converters of a chunk share a limit of **1 vis per tick** together (*Chunk limit*). A converter that makes N FE per vis converts up to N FE per tick.
- A **redstone signal** stops the conversion (the stored energy can still be taken out).

## How to get it

- **Thaumium**: arcane workbench, top row Thaumium Plate, Redstone Dust and Thaumium Plate; middle row Copper Ingot, Vis Resonator and Copper Ingot; bottom row Thaumium Plate, Simple Arcane Mechanism and Thaumium Plate (crystals ignis 2, ordo 1; 50 vis). Research: *Aura Converter*.
- **Alchemical Brass**: infusion over a Thaumium Aura Converter with four brass plates, a Simple Arcane Mechanism and a Vis Resonator.
- **Void Metal**: infusion over an Alchemical Brass Aura Converter with four void metal plates, a Complex Arcane Mechanism and two Vis Resonators.

## Step by step

1. Place the converter in a chunk with aura and put blocks that accept FE against it (batteries, machines, cables).
2. Right-click it to read the stored energy, the aura of the chunk, its state and its rate. The states are *Converting*, *Paused*, *Buffer full*, *No vis*, *Chunk limit* and *Disabled*.
3. Do not put several converters in one chunk and expect their output to add up: they share the chunk limit.

## Common mistakes

- Leaving a redstone signal on it.
- Draining a chunk with a low aura: the converter stops at a quarter of the base aura.
- Ignoring the flux: heavy use pollutes the chunk.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Aura Converter configuration.
