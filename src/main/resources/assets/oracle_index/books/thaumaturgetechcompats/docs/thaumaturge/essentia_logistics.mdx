## What it is

Once you need a lot of essentia, you stop melting items by hand and build a small factory:

| Block or item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Essentia Smeltery | burns fuel like a furnace and breaks items down into raw essentia slurry; about 20% of the time some essentia becomes flux |
| Arcane Alembic | placed on top of a smeltery, distills the slurry into separate essentia; stack several for more storage |
| Arcane Bellows | on the side of a smeltery, makes it hotter and faster |
| Warded Jar | stores essentia almost forever; fill it from an alembic, a phial or a tube |
| Label | sticks to a jar with essentia and locks it to that type |
| Brass Lid Brace | lets only tubes put essentia in or take it out of a jar |
| Essentia Tube | carries essentia between jars, alembics and devices |
| Essentia Valve | a tube you can close by hand or with redstone |
| Essentia Filter Tube | only lets through the essentia it is marked with |
| Restricted Essentia Tube | halves the suction passing through |
| One-Way Essentia Tube | accepts suction from one direction only |
| Essentia Buffer | holds 8 essentia of any types; bellows raise its suction |
| Essentia Resonator | shows the essentia and suction of a tube or device |
| Essentia Transfusers | move essentia through the air between tubes and jars up to 16 blocks away |

## Step by step

1. Research Essentia Smelting and build an Essentia Smeltery. Fuel it with coal, or alumentum for faster distillation.
2. Put one or more Arcane Alembics on top of it and feed items to the smeltery.
3. Take the essentia out of the alembics with a jar, a phial or tubes.
4. Research Essentia Tubes and connect the alembics to warded jars. Labelled jars only pull their own type.
5. Add valves and filters: a tube moves only one kind of essentia at a time, the one with the strongest suction, so an unplanned network clogs. Gas venting from a tube means two suctions are fighting.

## Good to know

- Click a tube with a wand to connect or disconnect it from its neighbours, or to rotate a valve.
- Shift-click a jar or alembic with an empty hand to empty it. To remove a label, shift-click the side that has the label.
- A label crafted with a phial of essentia is marked with that aspect and locks an empty jar to it.
- The Thaumium and Void Metal smelteries are later, better tiers of the Essentia Smeltery.

## In Thaumaturge Tech Compats

- Bigger jars: see Jars > Jars of Avaritia.
- Faster smelteries: see Devices > Avaritia Essentia Smelteries.
- Flux Jars and Rift Jars store flux instead of essentia (see Jars > Flux Jars and Rift Jar).
