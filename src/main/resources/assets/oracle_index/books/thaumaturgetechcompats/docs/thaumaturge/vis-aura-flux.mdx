## What it is

- **The aura** is a field of magical energy over the world. Each chunk has its own aura, which fills up to a natural capacity and refills over time. Aura nodes are knots of concentrated energy inside it (see Nodes).
- **Vis** is the pure, formless energy of the aura. Wands draw it, the Arcane Workbench spends it, and many devices run on it. The Thaumometer shows the vis of the aura you stand in.
- **Flux** is "dirty" vis. It appears when essentia spills into the air (the purple puffs you see when a crucible or jar leaks), when infusions go wrong and when devices waste magic. Flux takes up room in the aura, stops vis from refilling and can grow past the natural limit. It shows as a dark bar on the Thaumometer.
- **Physical flux**: flux can also condense into **Flux Goo** and **Flux Gas**. Touching them gives Vis Exhaust, which makes magical work cost more.
- **Taint** feeds on flux and spreads wherever it finds enough of it. A node whose chunk is full of flux can turn tainted.
- **Flux rifts**: if flux builds high enough, it tears a rift in reality. Rifts feed on flux and grow, become more unstable over time and release bursts of chaotic energy. The bigger the rift, the worse the burst.
- **Warp**: researching forbidden magic and crafting dark items warps your mind. Some warp is temporary and fades; permanent warp never goes away. Warp shows up as lapses, pain and hallucinations, some of them frighteningly real.

## Step by step

1. Look through the Thaumometer to read the vis and flux of your chunk.
2. Avoid making flux: do not let essentia spill, keep infusions stable and empty crucibles you are done with.
3. Clean physical flux by hand, or build a **Flux Scrubber** (it removes goo and gas within 16 blocks, powered by aer vis from a vis relay or an energized node).
4. Clean flux in the aura with a **Flux Condenser** and its lattice: fed with plenty of essentia (any kind except flux essentia), it turns aura flux into flux essentia that you drain from the bottom.
5. Close a rift by throwing a **Causality Collapser** at it. A calmer rift releases a calmer burst when it collapses.
6. Watch your warp with the **Sanity Checker**. **Purifying Bath Salts** thrown into water give temporary relief, and **Sanity Soap** removes all temporary warp and has a small chance to remove some non-permanent warp.

## Good to know

- A **Vis Battery** stores 10 vis: it absorbs vis when the aura is above 95% and gives it back when it drops below 75%.
- Silverwood trees slowly refill the aura around them.

## In Thaumaturge Tech Compats

- **Flux as power**: the [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:flux_jar) pulls flux out of the aura and stores it, and machines of this mod can run on it. The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:rift_jar) works with flux rifts (see Node Power, Flux Jars and Rift Jars, and Magical Power Sources).
- **Dimensional Flux network**: storage cores, transmission links, chargers, shield generators and terraformers (see the Nodes and Flux chapter).
- **Aura into energy**: the [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_aura_converter) drains vis from the aura and turns it into FE (see Aura Converters).
- **Against warp and taint**: purification and warp potions (see the Purity chapter).
- **Higher instability**: the Avaritia runic matrices bring their own instability effects (see Instability Effects).
