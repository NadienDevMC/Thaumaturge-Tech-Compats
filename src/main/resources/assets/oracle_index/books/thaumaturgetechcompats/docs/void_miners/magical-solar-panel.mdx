> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Void Miners Remastered**. The solar panel, and each tier on its own, can be switched off in the compatibilities config; on by default, game restart needed. Numbers are in the Void Miners magical config.

## What it is

A Void Miners solar panel that makes FE without sun or open sky: it generates only while it can pay units of power to a magical source nearby. When the payment fails it stops until the next try.

| Tier | Controller | FE per tick | Energy stored | Units per payment | Source radius |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_solar_panel) | 1,000 | 25,000,000 | 1 | 8 |
| Alchemical Brass | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_solar_panel) | 2,500 | 100,000,000 | 2 | 12 |
| Void Metal | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_solar_panel) | 6,000 | 500,000,000 | 4 | 16 |

The generation figure is before the efficiency modifiers of the structure and does not depend on the sun or the weather.

## How to get it

Research the Magical Solar Panel entry of the tier. Infusion altar, each over the tier before:

| Tier | Catalyst | Components | Aspects (instability) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | Void Miners Rubetine Solar Panel | 3 thaumium plates, simple mechanism, 2 jarred nodes | lux 12, potentia 10, metallum 8, alkimia 6 (2) |
| Alchemical Brass | Thaumium Solar Panel Controller | 3 brass plates, simple mechanism, 2 jarred nodes | lux 18, potentia 16, praecantatio 12, machina 10 (4) |
| Void Metal | Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller | 3 void metal plates, complex mechanism, 2 jarred nodes, void seed | lux 24, potentia 24, vacuos 16, praecantatio 16 (6) |

## Step by step

1. Craft the solar frames and modifiers.
2. Build the solar structure of the tier around the controller.
3. Put a power source (jarred node, Flux Jar, Rift Jar, world node or flux rift, never a hungry node) within the tier radius.
4. Connect an FE consumer or cable to the panel as with any Void Miners solar panel. Every 20 ticks it pays its units and generates.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting it to need sunlight: it does not.
- Forgetting the power source: without it, no energy.
- Mixing frames of different tiers.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Void Miners magical configuration.
