## What it is

The **Crucible** is a magical cauldron for alchemy. It renders items down into their essentia, and when you throw in a **catalyst** that essentia turns the catalyst into something new. Its first recipe is **Nitor**, a floating, everlasting light made from glowstone dust.

## Step by step

1. Complete Discovering Alchemy, then sprinkle Salis Mundus on a cauldron to turn it into a Crucible.
2. Place it over a heat source: fire, lava, nitor or something similar.
3. Fill it with water and wait until it boils.
4. Add ingredients that contain the essentia the recipe needs: drop them in or click the top of the crucible with them. The crucible melts them down into their aspects.
5. Throw in the catalyst (for nitor, glowstone dust). If the crucible holds enough of the right essentia, the catalyst absorbs it and turns into the result.

## Good to know

- You do not need the exact mix: there only has to be enough of each essentia the recipe asks for.
- Add more essentia and more catalysts to craft several items at once.
- Each craft uses up some water; refill it by hand or with pipes.
- Leftover essentia dissolves over time, and the purple puffs you see are that essentia turning into flux (see Vis, Aura and Flux). Do not leave a full crucible sitting around.
- Shift-right-click with an empty hand empties the crucible.
- Dipping a quartz shard into the crucible grows a vis crystal, at the cost of about twice the essentia the crystal holds.
- Crucible recipes include alumentum (a fuel that burns three times longer than coal), alchemical brass, thaumium and void metal ingots, ore clusters, tallow, leather, the blank lesser focus and the golem control seals.
- The **Thaumatorium** automates the crucible: built with Salis Mundus, it needs heat but no water, draws essentia through tubes and makes the chosen formula every time.
