## What it is

The everyday tools and items of a thaumaturge:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Salis Mundus | magic dust that wakes structures: bookshelf into Thaumonomicon, crafting table into Arcane Workbench, cauldron into Crucible, and the Infusion Altar, Golem Press and Thaumatorium |
| Thaumonomicon | the research book |
| Thaumometer | scans things for aspects and shows the aura, flux and nodes |
| Goggles of Revealing | reveal hidden things, show the vis or essentia inside objects and lower the vis cost of some tasks |
| Scribing Tools | ink for research; needed to build and use the Research Table |
| Glass Phial | carries a small amount of essentia |
| Nitor | an everlasting floating light and a heat source for the crucible |
| Alumentum | fuel that burns three times longer than coal |
| Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal | the three alchemical metals, the base of most devices |
| Primordial Pearl | pure primal power; often found broken into nodules or motes |
| Sanity Checker | shows your warp |
| Purifying Bath Salts and Sanity Soap | relief from warp |
| Causality Collapser | closes flux rifts |
| Focus Pouch | carries spare foci |

Thaumaturge also adds magical cloth and armor (the cloth that lowers vis costs, Fortress armor, Traveller's Boots, Void robes), charms and rings, and decorative blocks such as tallow candles, arcane stone and the Greatwood and Silverwood trees.

## Step by step

1. Make Salis Mundus as the dream described, then the Thaumonomicon and the Arcane Workbench.
2. Craft the Thaumometer and Scribing Tools first: research needs them.
3. Complete Discovering Artifice for the Goggles of Revealing.
4. Learn Nitor and Alumentum in the crucible for light and fuel.
5. Look for amber and cinnabar (smelted into quicksilver) underground, vis crystal clusters, and the plants: Greatwood, Silverwood, Cinderpearl, Shimmerleaf, Vishroom and Mana Pods.
