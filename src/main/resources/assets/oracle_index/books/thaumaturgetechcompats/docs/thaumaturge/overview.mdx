## What it is

**Thaumaturge** (by leclowndu93150) is the magic mod this addon is built on. In Thaumaturge every block, item and creature is made of **aspects**, magic flows through the world as **vis** in the aura of each chunk, careless magic leaves **flux** behind, and most magical items are made with three methods: the **Arcane Workbench**, the **Crucible** and the **Infusion Altar**.

Everything you learn is recorded in the **Thaumonomicon**, the research book. Its research is split into seven tabs:

| Tab | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Thaumaturgy | first steps, aspects, research, aura and nodes, flux, flux rifts, warp, ores, plants and primordial pearls |
| Alchemy | the crucible, alumentum, alchemical metals, essentia smelting, warded jars, tubes, the Thaumatorium, bath salts and sanity soap |
| Artifice | magical devices: bellows, lamps, mirrors, the levitator, the hungry chest, the flux scrubber, turrets and the arcane bore |
| Infusion | the infusion altar, its stabilization and boosting stones, infusion enchanting, magical armor and charms |
| Auromancy | wands and their caps and rods, staves, sceptres, foci, node tapping, node jars, stabilizers and transducers, vis batteries and relays |
| Golemancy | clockwork minds, the Golem Press, golem materials and control seals |
| Eldritch | the late game: the outer lands, primordial nodes and void equipment |

## Step by step

1. **The dream.** At some point you wake up from a strange dream and write it down. It tells you to grind three vis crystals of different types with redstone in a bowl, using flint. The glowing result is **Salis Mundus**, the magic dust that starts everything.
2. **The Thaumonomicon.** Sprinkle Salis Mundus on a bookshelf: it turns into the Thaumonomicon.
3. **The Arcane Workbench.** Sprinkle Salis Mundus on a crafting table to turn it into an Arcane Workbench (see Arcane Workbench).
4. **The Thaumometer.** Craft it on the Arcane Workbench and scan blocks, items and creatures by holding right-click on them. Every scan gives research points of the aspects the thing contains, and the first time you discover an aspect you get two extra points.
5. **The Research Table.** Place two wooden tables side by side and click one with Scribing Tools. In the table you combine aspects into compound aspects, and you solve research notes.
6. **Research.** Open the Thaumonomicon to see what you can research next. Bigger entries list theories: clicking one spends paper and ink and gives you a research note, which you solve on the Research Table by linking the aspects around a hexagon grid (each placed aspect costs one research point). Simpler entries just cost aspects straight from your pool.
7. **The other tabs.** The entries Discovering Alchemy, Discovering Artifice, Discovering Infusion, Discovering Auromancy, Discovering Golemancy and the Eldritch one open the other tabs.

## Good to know

- Hold **Shift** over an item to see its aspects, once you have studied it.
- Objects and creatures can usually only be scanned once.
- Erasing an aspect from a research note does not give the point back (the Research Expertise entry later returns it about one time in four).

## In Thaumaturge Tech Compats

This addon uses the same research system: almost every block and item here is unlocked by an entry of the Thaumonomicon and is made on one of the three crafting methods of Thaumaturge (see Getting Started). The pages of this chapter explain each base mechanic and end with the chapters of this book that extend it.

Thaumaturge tiers its metals as **Thaumium**, **Alchemical Brass** and **Void Metal**, and this addon follows that same order in every tech compat. With Avaritia installed it adds three more tiers above them: Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity (see The Avaritia Progression).
