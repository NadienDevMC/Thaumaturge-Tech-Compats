Items are also listed in the creative tab "Tainted Food". The Farmer's Delight versions are on the Farmer's Delight chapter.

## Step by step

1. Look up the food in the table and fill the crucible with that Vitium.
2. Drop the original food as the catalyst.
3. Collect the tainted version.

## Common mistakes

- Using the wrong catalyst: it must be the exact food of the table (for example Steak, not raw beef).
- Forgetting that the Avaritia dishes need 64 Vitium.
