> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Mekanism** mod.

## What it is

The Native Osmium Cluster is a Thaumaturge-style ore cluster for a Mekanism metal: a raw chunk of metal that gives two ingots when smelted.

## How to get it

Crucible: raw osmium or raw uranium as the catalyst with 5 Metallum and 5 Ordo (research Metal Purification). Mekanism machines also make it (see the steps below).

## How to use it

1. Get it in a crucible (research Metal Purification: raw metal as the catalyst with 5 Metallum and 5 Ordo) or through the Mekanism routes (Enrichment Chamber from crystals, and the optional chamber routes).
2. Smelt it (200 ticks) or blast it (100 ticks) for two ingots.
3. With Create, mill or crush it; with Oritech, use the Pulverizer or the Grinder for byproducts.
