> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod for the Crystal Matrix, Neutron and Infinity ingots.

## What it is

The three plates are the building material of the whole progression. They are an ingredient of wand caps, wand rods, improved foci, pillar stones, workbench chargers, mnemonic matrices, smelteries and node devices. Each plate is also the base of a golem of its own material in the Golem Press (together with a Complex Arcane Mechanism).

## How to get them

All three are plain crafting table recipes, three plates per craft.

| Plate | Recipe |
| --- | --- |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_plate) | 3 Crystal Matrix Ingots in a row |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_plate) | 3 Neutron Ingots in the top row, 1 Crystal Matrix Plate in the middle of the row below |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_plate) | 3 Infinity Ingots in the top row, 1 Neutronium Plate in the middle of the row below |

So you cannot make Infinity plates without having made the two lower kinds first.

## Golem materials

| Golem | Traits (from the research) |
| --- | --- |
| Crystal Matrix | light, fireproof and blastproof |
| Neutronium | heavy, armored, fireproof and blastproof |
| Infinity | the sturdiest of all: brutal, armored, fireproof, blastproof and self-repairing |

## Step by step

1. Finish the research "Material Studies: Avaritia" (the plates have no research lock for crafting, but the golem material is part of that entry).
2. Craft Crystal Matrix Plates, then Neutronium Plates, then Infinity Plates.
3. In the Golem Press, combine a plate with a Complex Arcane Mechanism to get a golem of that material.

## Common mistakes

- Using three plates of the lower tier instead of three ingots: the recipe wants ingots in the top row and only **one** plate below.
- Expecting the plate to come out of a smeltery: they are crafting recipes only.
