## What it is

The Akashic Records is a record of every thought ever thought. Using it floods your mind with every aspect there is, hundreds of points of each. It is also an ingredient of the Cheater's Thaumonomicon.

## How to use it

1. Research Primordial Pearl, then Extremely Primordial Pearl, then Akashic Records (Avaritia tab). They cost warp: keep a way to cure it.
2. Infuse it on an altar with the Curio of Knowledge as the central item and four Infinity Ingots on the pedestals (see the recipe above).
3. Hold it and use it (right click) to receive every aspect.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Akashic Records
> The Akashic Records are complete. Opening them floods the mind with every aspect there is, hundreds of points of each, and then a little more.
