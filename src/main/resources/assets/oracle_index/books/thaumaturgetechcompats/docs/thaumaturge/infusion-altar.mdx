## What it is

**Infusion** forces essentia and items into a central item to give it new properties. It is the strongest crafting method of Thaumaturge and the one most recipes of this addon use. It needs:

- the **Runic Matrix**, floating at the top centre;
- the **Arcane Pedestal** under it, which holds the item you infuse;
- **pillars**: eight Arcane Stone blocks, two stacked at each of the four corners, that Salis Mundus turns into Infusion Pillars;
- more **pedestals** around the altar for the other ingredients;
- **essentia** in warded jars (or similar containers) nearby.

## Step by step

1. Complete the Infusion research and craft the Runic Matrix.
2. Build the structure: an Arcane Pedestal in the centre, two Arcane Stone blocks stacked at each corner around it, and the Runic Matrix two blocks above the pedestal.
3. Sprinkle Salis Mundus on it: the stones form the pillars.
4. Place pedestals around the altar, as symmetrical as you can, and put the ingredients on them. Put the main item on the central pedestal.
5. Put jars with the needed essentia close to the altar.
6. Activate the Runic Matrix with a casting tool such as a wand, then click it again to start. First it drains the essentia from the jars, then the essence of each ingredient on the pedestals, and then the result appears on the central pedestal.

## Instability

Forcing so much energy into one item is dangerous. Items can be knocked off their pedestals, flux can appear, and worse things can happen. The longer a craft stalls (missing essentia, dropped items), the more likely trouble becomes.

- Keep the altar symmetrical: pedestals and items balanced with the ones on the opposite side.
- Place occult decorations (candles, skulls and similar) symmetrically around it. Many copies of the same decoration help less and less.
- Build **Stabilizers** and connect the pedestals to them with **Redstone Inlay** (up to 15 blocks). A discharge that hits a protected pedestal travels to the Stabilizer instead and is released as flux. Pedestals carry the charge too and show runes when connected.
- **Infusion Speed Stones** under a pillar make it faster but a little more expensive; **Infusion Cost Stones** make it cheaper but a little slower. They do not need to be balanced.

## Good to know

- Infusion Enchantment and the Ancient and Eldritch altars are later research.
- Essentia Mirrors let an infusion draw essentia from far away, at the cost of flux.
