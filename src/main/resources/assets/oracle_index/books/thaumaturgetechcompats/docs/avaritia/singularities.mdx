> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. The singularities use Avaritia's own singularity item and system; this mod only adds new kinds.

## What it is

Avaritia singularities are dense lumps of a single material. Tech Compats adds seven new kinds, each tied to one ingot:

| Singularity | Material it is made of | Where the material comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | Thaumium Ingot | Thaumaturge |
| Void Metal | Void Metal Ingot | Thaumaturge |
| Alchemical Brass | Alchemical Brass Ingot | Thaumaturge |
| Rifting | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:rifting_ingot) | crucible with a Rift Jar and a trapped rift |
| Fibric | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:fibric_ingot) | altar, 4 Taint Fibres, Void Metal Ingot as catalyst |
| Salis | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_ingot) | altar, 4 Salis Mundus, Void Metal Ingot as catalyst |
| Crimson | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:crimson_ingot) | altar, 2 Crimson Blades, Void Metal Ingot as catalyst |

They are a single Avaritia item that changes with the kind, and all of them appear in this mod's creative tab. The kinds are defined in the mod's data, each with a name, the ingredient and two colors for its look.

## How to get them

1. Obtain the ingot of the kind you want (table above).
2. Use Avaritia's machinery for singularities (the Neutronium Compressor and its tiered versions, see the Avaritia Machines chapter) with that ingot as the ingredient, exactly as with the singularities of Avaritia.
3. The Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal ones need the matching ingots only; no other conditions.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for a dedicated item: the singularity is always the Avaritia item, only its name and colors change.
- Expecting different compression amounts or times: they are the Avaritia defaults, because this mod only supplies the ingredient and colors.
