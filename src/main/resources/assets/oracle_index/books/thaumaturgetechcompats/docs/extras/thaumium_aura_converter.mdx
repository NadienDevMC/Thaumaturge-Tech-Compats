## What it is

The Thaumium Aura Converter drains vis from the aura of its chunk and turns it into **FE**, which it hands to every neighbouring block that accepts energy. It never takes energy in. Every vis it converts leaves 0.1 flux in the aura, it never drains the aura below 25% of its base value, and all the converters of a chunk share a limit of 1 vis per tick.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| FE per vis | 250,000 |
| Maximum output per tick | 250,000 FE |
| Energy buffer | 10,000,000 FE |

## How to use it

1. Make it (the Thaumium one at the Arcane Workbench, research Aura Converter; the others infused over the one before).
2. Place it in a chunk with aura and put blocks that accept FE against it (batteries, machines, cables).
3. Right click it to read the stored energy, the aura of the chunk, its state and its rate.
4. A redstone signal stops the conversion (the stored energy can still be taken out).
