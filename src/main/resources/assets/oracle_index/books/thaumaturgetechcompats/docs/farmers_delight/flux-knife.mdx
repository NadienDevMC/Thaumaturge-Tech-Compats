> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Farmer's Delight** mod. The Flux Knife can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, needs a restart). Settings: the flux knife section of the tainted Farmer's Delight config.

## What it is

The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:flux_knife) is a Farmer's Delight knife that bites into the world itself. It has two special abilities:

- **Rift opening**: sneak and use it on a block to open a **flux rift** beside it.
- **Flux cutting**: food cut with it on a Cutting Board turns into flux that is added to the air.

Stats: durability 700, damage +3.5, attack speed -1.8, mining speed 8.0, enchantability 22.

## How to get it

Research "Flux Knife". Infuse it on an altar from a **Void Metal Knife** (the catalyst), with two Flux (Vitium) Crystals, Tainted Goo and a Bottle of Taint on the pedestals. Aspects: Vitium 40, Vacuos 24, Instrumentum 16, Alienis 12; instability 6.

## Opening a rift

| Rift setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Enabled | true |
| Require sneaking | true (it stays a normal knife on other blocks) |
| Initial size | 5 (Thaumaturge caps rifts at 100) |
| Cost mode | drain flux: takes the flux of that chunk; the other modes are none and drain vis |
| Cost factor | 1.0: a rift costs size squared divided by 3 |
| Durability cost | 10 |
| Cooldown (ticks) | 200 (10 seconds) |

1. Sneak and use the knife on a block.
2. The rift opens beside it, sized to the flux taken from the aura of that chunk.
3. If the chunk does not hold enough, nothing opens and you see "The aura does not hold enough to open a rift here."
4. The blade wears and the knife needs a pause before the next rift.

A size 5 rift costs about 8.3 flux (5 squared over 3). Use it to make a rift for a Rift Jar to work on.

## Cutting flux

On a Cutting Board, each cut adds flux to the aura: a base of 0.5 plus 0.25 per point of nutrition of the cut item, at most 6 per cut. By default the cut still gives its normal result (flux and item mode); the flux only mode removes the food results. By default only food makes flux (food only setting).

## Step by step

1. Craft the Void Metal Knife, then infuse the Flux Knife.
2. Pick a chunk with flux in the aura and sneak-use the knife on a block.
3. Optionally put a Rift Jar next to the rift to turn it into power.

## Common mistakes

- Using it in a clean chunk: it needs enough flux to size the rift.
- Spamming it: each rift wears 10 durability and the cooldown is 10 seconds.
- Cutting food next to your base: it adds flux where you cut.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Farmer's Delight tainted food configuration.
