> [!NOTE]
> The essences can be switched off in the compatibilities config (game restart). The research is **Tech Compats Essences**.

## What it is

Four essences that are not among the primal ones: **Sol**, **Terminus**, **Nuclear** and **Cosmic**. They do not cling to just any material. Items built from the resources that carry them inherit them according to what they are made of, whether the material is vanilla, from Thaumaturge or from another mod.

| Essence | Where it is found |
|---|---|
| Sol (power of the sun) | glowstone, blaze rods and powder, magma cream, daylight detectors, sea lanterns, sunflowers, torchflowers, honey and amber, a little gold, Solar Flux cells and panels, Botania blaze and sunny quartz, Ender IO photovoltaic parts |
| Terminus (the end of everything) | the late game of Avaritia: infinity ingots, tools and armor, the Endest Pearl, singularities, the higher Neutronium Collectors and Compressors |
| Nuclear (radiation and fission; made of potentia and perditio) | ancient debris, netherite, a little TNT and end crystals, uranium, plutonium, polonium and thorium of Mekanism and Oritech, their pellets and reactor rods, the neutronium of Avaritia |
| Cosmic (the stars and the infinite; made of alienis and auram) | nether stars, eyes of ender, ender pearls, dragon eggs and breath, shulker shells, elytra, chorus fruit, echo shards, amethyst, Crystal Matrix and Infinity ingots, the Endest Pearl, the Void Seed |

For example, the Void Metal Ingot, brewed around a Void Seed, carries Cosmic, and a Neutronium Gear carries the Nuclear of its ingots. The same goes for machines and tools of every other mod installed.

## How it matters

- The Brass and Void tiers of the Avaritia Neutron Collector and Compressor ask for Nuclear and Cosmic.
- Terminus appears in the infusion recipes of the Avaritia tiers of this mod (smelteries, stabilizers, wand parts and so on).
- The essences can be read as aspects on items with the usual Thaumaturge tools.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
