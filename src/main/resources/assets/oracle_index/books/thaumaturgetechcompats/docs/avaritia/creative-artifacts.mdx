> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod (Neutron and Infinity Ingots).

## What it is

Two items that were never meant to be built by mortal hands, forged anyway on an infusion altar:

- **Creative Node Placer** (a Thaumaturge item): plants whole nodes in the aura.
- **Cheater's Thaumonomicon** (a Thaumonomicon that already knows every secret).

Their power is the same as the creative-world versions, so use them with care.

## How to get them

Research "Creative Artifacts" (Avaritia category). It needs the Akashic Records and the Thaumaturge Nodes research, and costs 3 warp.

| Result | Catalyst | Components | Aspects | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Creative Node Placer | Node Jar | 2 Infinity Plates, 2 Neutronium Plates, 2 Neutron Ingots, 2 Primordial Pearls | Auram 128, Alienis 96, Praecantatio 96, Vinculum 64, Terminus 48 | 12 |
| Cheater's Thaumonomicon | Thaumonomicon | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:akashic_record), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl), 2 Infinity Plates, 2 Infinity Ingots | Cognitio 128, Praecantatio 96, Alienis 96, Desiderium 64, Terminus 64 | 12 |

## Step by step

1. Complete the Akashic Records research, then the Creative Artifacts research.
2. Make the Infinity Plates and Neutronium Plates (see Plates and Golems).
3. Place the catalyst (Node Jar or Thaumonomicon) in the altar and the components on the pedestals.
4. Provide the listed aspects through nodes and jars, and infuse.

## Common mistakes

- Running the altar with too little stability: with these aspect amounts the altar needs a lot of essentia and a calm structure.
- Treating them as a cheap shortcut: each one eats an Akashic Records, Infinity materials and warp.
