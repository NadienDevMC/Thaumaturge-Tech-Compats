## What it is

The Alchemical Brass Knife is a Farmer's Delight knife in every way: it cuts on the Cutting Board and counts as a knife for any recipe that asks for one. It is sturdier, sharper and easier to enchant than the iron knife.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Durability | 800 |
| Attack damage (base 0.5 plus) | +3.0 |
| Attack speed | -1.8 |
| Mining speed | 7.5 |
| Enchantability | 20 |
| Flux Taint on hit | none |

## How to use it

1. Research the knife of its tier and craft it at the Arcane Workbench (a plate over the knife of the tier before; a plate over a stick for Thaumium).
2. Use it on the Cutting Board of Farmer's Delight like any knife.
3. Enchant it: its high enchantability makes good enchantments easier.
4. The Void Metal Knife is also the base of the Flux Knife.
