## What it is

The Biome Purification Potion does in one breath the work of an Ethereal Bloom: thrown at the ground, the tainted biome returns to what the land was, taint blocks wither away, flux goo and gas wash out, and taint seeds caught in the mist are destroyed. It also draws flux out of the aura of the chunks it touches.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Radius | 12 blocks (8 up and down) |
| Flux drained per chunk touched | 15 |
| Rest between uses | 40 ticks |

## How to use it

1. Research it in the Purity tab.
2. Brew it in a crucible with a Purification Splash Potion as the catalyst.
3. Throw it at the ground in the tainted land. Prime taint seeds are not destroyed by default.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Biome Purification Potion
> The brew is ready. Thrown at the ground, it bursts into a pale green mist that does in one breath the work of an ethereal bloom: the tainted biome returns to what the land was, fibres, crust, soil and growths wither away, flux goo and gas wash out, and any taint seed caught in the mist is destroyed.
> It also draws flux out of the aura of the chunks it touches.
> 
> **Biome Purification Potion**
> 
> It is brewed in the crucible from Ordo, Victus, Aqua, Lux, Auram, Praecantatio, Vitreus and Herba, with a Purification Splash Potion as the catalyst. The area, the flux it draws and what it cleans are set in the configuration.
