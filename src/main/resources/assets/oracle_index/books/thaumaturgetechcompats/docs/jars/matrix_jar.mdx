> [!NOTE]
> The Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity jars need the **Avaritia** mod.

## What it is

The Crystal Matrix Jar is a bigger version of the Warded Jar of Thaumaturge. It works like any warded jar: labels and braces fit it, and the essentia and the aspect filter are kept when you break and pick it up.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Essentia capacity | 500 |
| Fire resistant | no |

## How to use it

1. Research Jars of Avaritia.
2. Infuse it over the jar of the tier before (a plain Warded Jar for Crystal Matrix), with the Runic Matrix of its tier at the center of the altar.
3. Use it exactly like a normal warded jar: fill it with essentia tubes and label it if you want.

- []($jars/neutronium_jar)

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Jars of Avaritia
> Each jar is made on an infusion altar, starting from a plain warded jar:
> - The Crystal Matrix Jar is surrounded by crystal matrix ingots.
> - The Neutronium Jar needs a Crystal Matrix Jar and neutron ingots.
> - The Infinity Jar needs a Neutronium Jar, infinity ingots and an Extremely Primordial Pearl.
> They work like any other warded jar, only larger. Labels and braces fit them as usual, and essentia is kept when the jar is picked up.
