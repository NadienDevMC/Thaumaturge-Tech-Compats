---
title: Hephaestus Tool Modifiers
---

> [!NOTE]
> Needs **Hephaestus Tools**. The modifiers show up in the Arsenal Table. Maximum level of each: 12.

## What they are

Four extra upgrades for Hephaestus tools. You apply one in the **Arsenal Table** (Modify tab): each level consumes **one ingot** of its material and needs a free upgrade slot on the tool. You can add up to level 12. At level 12 the tool **never loses durability**.

| Modifier | Ingot consumed per level | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Unbreakable | Thaumium Ingot | Chance to avoid durability loss that grows with every level (it acts like Unbreaking of level 2 x level squared) |
| Vis Repair | Brass Ingot | The tool mends itself over time, much faster while the aura of the chunk has vis to give |
| Void Teleport | Void Metal Ingot | Mined blocks and the drops of slain mobs go straight into your inventory: chance level / 8 per drop, always from level 8 |
| Ender Teleport | Thaumaturge Fabric | Use the tool (right click) to teleport forward like an enderman; hitting a mob may also blink you away |

### Vis Repair details

Every 2 seconds, for each tool in your inventory that is damaged, it repairs up to 5 durability points per level, paying 0.02 vis per point from the aura of the chunk where you stand. If the aura cannot pay, it repairs only a slow amount (level / 6 points, at least 1) instead.

### Ender Teleport details

- Range: 6 + 2 per level blocks (it stops short of walls).
- Cooldown: 60 - 4 per level ticks, never less than 10.
- When you hit a mob, each hit has 3% chance per level to blink you away to a random nearby spot (radius 3 + half the level).

## Step by step

1. Build the tool (for instance from the metals of the previous pages).
2. Open the Arsenal Table and go to the Modify tab.
3. Pick the modifier and insert its ingots. One ingot gives one level; repeat until you reach the level you want (maximum 12).

## Common mistakes

- No free upgrade slot on the tool: the modifier will not apply.
- Expecting Void Teleport to collect everything below level 8: before that, only part of the drops go to the inventory.
- Using Vis Repair in a chunk with no vis: it repairs slowly.
