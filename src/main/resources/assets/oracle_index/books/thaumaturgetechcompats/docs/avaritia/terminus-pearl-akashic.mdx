> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod (the Akashic Records use Infinity Ingots).

## What it is

- **Terminus** is a new aspect, "the end of all things". It is a compound of Desiderium and Alienis. Almost every recipe of this chapter asks for a few points of it, and Avaritia items such as the Infinity Ingot carry it as an aspect.
- The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) is several primordial pearls folded into one. It is **not spent** when used as a catalyst, so it is an everlasting catalyst, and it is an ingredient of the Infinity tier of nearly every upgrade.
- The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:akashic_record) is a record of every thought ever thought. Using it floods your mind with every aspect there is, hundreds of points of each.

The Tech Compats also add three more aspects: **Sol** (Lux + Victus), **Nuclear** (Potentia + Perditio) and **Cosmic** (Alienis + Auram). They can be put on base items with the Tech Compats essences feature of the compatibilities config (on by default, needs a restart).

## How to get them

Research order in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia category): Primordial Pearl, then Extremely Primordial Pearl, then Akashic Records. Both need one Eldritch observation and one Eldritch theory, and they give 2 and 3 warp while you research them.

| Item | Altar recipe | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extremely Primordial Pearl | catalyst Primordial Pearl, 7 Primordial Pearls; Alienis 64, Praecantatio 64, Aqua 64, Ignis 64, Terra 64, Aer 64, Ordo 64, Perditio 64, Terminus 32 | 12 |
| Akashic Records | catalyst Curio of Knowledge, 4 Infinity Ingots; Cognitio 64, Praecantatio 64, Terminus 32 | 12 |

## Step by step

1. Finish the research entries in order and keep your warp in check.
2. Build an infusion altar with plenty of Aqua, Ignis, Terra, Aer, Ordo and Perditio in the surrounding nodes and jars.
3. Put a Primordial Pearl as the catalyst and seven more around the altar, then infuse. The result is one Extremely Primordial Pearl.
4. For the Akashic Records, place the Curio of Knowledge as the catalyst and four Infinity Ingots on the pedestals.
5. Right click the Akashic Records to receive every aspect.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to use the Extremely Primordial Pearl as a one-use ingredient: as a **catalyst** it survives, but when a recipe lists it as an ingredient (Infinity jars, stones...) it is consumed.
- Skipping the Terminus aspect: it is part of the recipe, so your altar needs a source for it (Desiderium and Alienis combine into it).
- Doing these without a way to cure warp: both researches are warp-heavy.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
