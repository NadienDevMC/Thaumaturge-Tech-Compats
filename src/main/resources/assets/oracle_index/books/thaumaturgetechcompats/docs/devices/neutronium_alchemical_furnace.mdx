> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of the **Avaritia** mod.

## What it is

The Neutronium Alchemical Furnace is the Advanced Alchemical Furnace with the Neutronium Essentia Smeltery as its heart: it breaks items into essentia like the advanced one, but waits less between items and gives back part of the heat, Perditio and Aqua it burns.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Pause between items | divided by 4 |
| Heat, Perditio and Aqua given back | 50% |

## How to get it

### Salis Mundus on a structure

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | built in the world, woken with Salis Mundus |
| Research | **Avaritia Alchemical Furnaces** (Avaritia tab) |

## How to use it

1. Research Avaritia Alchemical Furnaces.
2. Build the structure of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace (a three by three base with two layers).
3. Put the matching Neutronium Essentia Smeltery at its center.
4. Sprinkle Salis Mundus over it: the structure comes to life as this furnace.
5. Use it like the Advanced Alchemical Furnace; Jade shows its speed and savings. Breaking a part turns it back into its smeltery.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Avaritia Alchemical Furnaces
> The structure is exactly that of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace, a three by three base with two layers, but the essentia smeltery at its centre is the matching Avaritia smeltery: Crystal Matrix, Neutronium or Infinity. Salis mundus sprinkled over it brings it to life, and since every smeltery needs the one before it, every furnace needs the one before it too.
> - Crystal Matrix: waits half as long between items and gives back 25% of the heat, perditio and aqua it burns.
> - Neutronium: waits a quarter as long and gives back 50%.
> - Infinity: waits a tenth as long and gives back 90%.
> If a part is broken the furnace turns back into its smeltery. They draw and release essentia exactly like the Advanced Alchemical Furnace.
