Thaumaturge Tech Compats connects **Thaumaturge** with other tech and magic mods and adds tiered machines, jars, matrices, tools and materials on top of it.

## How to use this book

1. Press the Oracle Index key (default **H**) and pick **Thaumaturge Tech Compats** in the mod list at the top left.
2. The **guides** section explains each feature step by step: what it is, how to get it, what to place, what it needs and the common mistakes. Guides are grouped in chapters.
3. Inside every chapter, after its guides, each block and item of that chapter has its own page: what it is, the numbers it uses, its recipe drawn as a grid (crafting table, arcane workbench or the layout of the infusion altar) with a table of vis, crystals, essentia, instability and research, and step by step how to use it. The **content** section has the same item pages, grouped by chapter.
4. Hold **ALT** over an item tooltip to open its page, when it has one.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The book lists everything the mod can add, but many chapters belong to features that only exist when another mod is installed (Create, Oritech, Mekanism, Botania, Void Miners, Ender IO, Farmer's Delight, Avaritia, Hephaestus Tools, Solar Generation). Each guide starts with a note saying which mod it needs. If that mod is not installed, the feature and its items are simply not there.

## General rules of the mod

- Almost every recipe is an **infusion altar**, **arcane workbench** or **crucible** recipe of Thaumaturge, so most things need their research in the Thaumonomicon first.
- Tiers follow a fixed order: Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal for the tech compats, and Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity for the Avaritia content. Each tier is built from the one before it.
- Machines that use magical power take it from a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or a flux rift near them. A node that is hungry is never a valid source.
- Values such as speeds, capacities and costs are in the configuration files of the mod, and every compat can be switched off in the compat toggles.
