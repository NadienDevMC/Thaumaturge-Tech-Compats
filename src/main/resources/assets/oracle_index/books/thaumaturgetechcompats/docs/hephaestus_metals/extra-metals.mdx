> [!NOTE]
> The metals themselves only need Thaumaturge. Melting and casting need **Hephaestus Forge** (and its Hephaestus smeltery feature), and making tools from them needs **Hephaestus Tools**. The research is **Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson**. Their aspects come from the item aspects of this mod, a feature of the compatibilities config.

## What they are

Four metals the old alchemists never named. Each has an **ingot**, a **nugget**, a **block** (9 ingots) and a **native cluster**, a bucket of its molten form for the Hephaestus smeltery, and a wand rod and cap. As Hephaestus tool materials they rank, best to worst: **Salis, Fibric, Crimson, Rifting**.

## How to obtain each ingot

| Ingot | Method | Aspects | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:rifting_ingot) | Crucible, catalyst a Void Metal Ingot | Praecantatio 20, Sol 20 | A **Rift Jar holding a trapped rift** must stand next to the crucible; without it nothing happens |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:fibric_ingot) | Infusion, catalyst a Void Metal Ingot, four Taint Fibres | Fabrico 26, Vitium 26 | instability 4 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_ingot) | Infusion, catalyst a Void Metal Ingot, four Salis Mundus | Praecantatio 32, Potentia 32 | instability 4 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:crimson_ingot) | Infusion, catalyst a Void Metal Ingot, two Crimson Blades | Perditio 22, Humanus 22 | instability 8 |

Nuggets, ingots and blocks convert in a normal crafting grid: nine nuggets make an ingot, one ingot makes nine nuggets, nine ingots make a block, a block makes nine ingots.

## Hephaestus smeltery

Melt the ingots, nuggets, blocks and tool parts in the Hephaestus Forge smeltery into molten fluids, and cast them again on a casting table or basin. The heat the smeltery needs: Rifting 1600, Crimson 1700, Fibric 1800, Salis 2000. The metals also melt from existing Hephaestus Tools parts made of them.

## Tool material stats

| Metal | Head durability | Mining speed | Attack damage | Enchantability | Binding durability |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rifting | 8,500 | 6 | 2.5 | 14 | 100 |
| Crimson | 12,500 | 7 | 3 | 15 | 160 |
| Fibric | 45,500 | 8 | 3.5 | 18 | 240 |
| Salis | 75,900 | 11 | 5 | 25 | 550 |

Fibric and Salis are epic rarity. Tool parts of these metals appear in the creative tab; they can be cast from the molten metal.

## Step by step

1. Complete the research.
2. Make the ingot of your choice (see the table).
3. Melt it in the smeltery (optional) and cast tool parts, or craft wand parts (see the wand page).
4. Build the Hephaestus tool and add modifiers (see the modifiers page).

## Common mistakes

- Making a Rifting Ingot without a Rift Jar that holds a trapped rift next to the crucible. The message reads: the void metal needs a Rift Jar with a trapped rift next to the crucible.
- Using the nuggets as a catalyst: the recipes ask for Void Metal Ingots as catalyst.
- Looking for a recipe or world generation for the native clusters: none is defined here.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
