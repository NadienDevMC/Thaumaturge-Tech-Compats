title: Magical Conduits

> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Ender IO**. The conduits (and the whole Ender IO integration) can be switched off in the compatibilities config; on by default, game restart needed. Numbers are in the Ender IO conduits config.

## What it is

Nine conduits that go beyond the vibrant ones of Ender IO: **Energy**, **Item** and **Fluid**, each in Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal. They are Ender IO conduits (they use the normal conduit item and the conduit bundle), so they connect, filter and are configured exactly like Ender IO conduits, only faster.

| Tier | Energy (RF per tick) | Item (items per cycle, ticks per cycle) | Fluid (mB per tick) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vibrant (Ender IO) | 192,000 | 64 per cycle, 10 ticks | 8,000 |
| Thaumium | 750,000 | 64, every 5 ticks | 32,000 |
| Alchemical Brass | 3,000,000 | 128, every 4 ticks | 128,000 |
| Void Metal | 12,000,000 | 256, every 2 ticks | 512,000 |

Item conduits are tuned with their transfer rate and ticks per cycle (1 to 20, lower is faster); energy and fluid conduits only have a transfer rate.

## How to get it

Research **Magical Conduits** in the Tech category. Arcane workbench, shaped: top row a plate of the tier, the extra material of the kind and a plate; middle row a Conduit Binder (Ender IO), the conduit of the previous tier and a Conduit Binder; bottom row a plate, the bottom item and a plate. The extra material is Vibrant Alloy Ingot for energy, Pulsating Alloy Ingot for items, Vibrant Crystal for fluids; the bottom item is a Thaumium plate (Thaumium), simple mechanism (Alchemical Brass) or void seed (Void Metal). Each conduit page shows the grid. One craft gives **8 conduits**.

| Tier | C (previous conduit) | Vis | Crystals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | the vibrant conduit of the same kind (Ender IO) | 40 | kind crystal x2, ordo x1 |
| Alchemical Brass | the Thaumium conduit of the same kind | 80 | kind crystal x3, ordo x2 |
| Void Metal | the Alchemical Brass conduit of the same kind | 120 | kind crystal x4, ordo x3 |

Kind crystal: ignis for energy, aer for items, aqua for fluids.

## Step by step

1. Craft or find a Vibrant conduit of the kind you want.
2. Craft the Thaumium conduit from it, then climb the tiers.
3. Place them like any Ender IO conduit; use the Ender IO conduit tools to connect and set modes.

## Common mistakes

- Starting from a basic conduit: the first tier needs the vibrant one.
- Mixing kinds: the catalyst must be of the same kind (energy with energy).
- Forgetting the Conduit Binders (2 per craft).

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Ender IO conduits configuration.
