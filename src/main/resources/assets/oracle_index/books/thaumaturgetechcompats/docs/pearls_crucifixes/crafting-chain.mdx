> [!NOTE]
> The Blaze Cube, Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity tiers need the **Avaritia** mod. The first pearl needs an Avaritia Endest Pearl.

## What it is

Pearls and crucifixes are all infused on an altar in a **chain**: each one needs the one before it as the catalyst and four units of its own material. The research entries are "Planetary Endest Pearl" and "The Great Orbits" (pearls) and "Star Crucifixes" (crucifixes), in the Avaritia category.

The pearl uses the previous pearl as catalyst and the crucifix uses the previous crucifix. The Creative tier has no recipe. The Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity ones are infused on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of their own tier.

## Aspects

The recipes use the new essences of this addon: Sol, Nuclear and Cosmic.

- Pearls: copper asks for Motus 4, Alienis 4, Praecantatio 2; Void Metal asks for Motus 28, Alienis 28, Praecantatio 14, Potentia 14, Auram 14, Vacuos 14; Blaze Cube adds Sol and Cosmic; Neutronium adds Cosmic and Nuclear (Motus 37, Alienis 37, others 18 each).
- Crucifixes: copper asks for Vinculum 8, Praemunio 8, Spiritus 4. Infinity asks for Vinculum 64, Cosmic 48, Mortuus 48, Ordo 48, Alienis 40, Nuclear 32, Terminus 24. The Neutronium and Infinity crucifixes also need Nuclear, and Infinity needs Cosmic too.

## Step by step

1. Research "Planetary Endest Pearl" and/or "Star Crucifixes".
2. Craft the Copper one: an altar with an Endest Pearl (or a Totem of Undying) as the catalyst and four copper ingots.
3. Infuse each following tier with the previous item as catalyst. The previous item is consumed as the catalyst, so you need a full chain of crafts, one tier after the other.
4. From Blaze Cube on, make the Salis Mundus variants first (see the Avaritia chapter).
5. For Crystal Matrix and above, switch the altar center to the matching Runic Matrix.

## Common mistakes

- Selling or storing the previous tier: it is the catalyst of the next.
- Forgetting the extra Salis Mundus: the amounts grow 2, 4, 6, 8.
- Trying to craft the Creative tier: it is creative-tab only.
