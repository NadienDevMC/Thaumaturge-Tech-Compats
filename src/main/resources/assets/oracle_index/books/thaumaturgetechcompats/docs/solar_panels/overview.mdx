> [!NOTE]
> This chapter needs the **Solar Generation** mod: every panel is built from its panels, cells and cores. Some panels also need Create, All The Ores, Hephaestus Forge or Mekanism (see the table). Everything can be switched off in the compatibilities config, and so can each panel on its own. These panels are not the Void Miners solar controllers.

## What it is

Eight solar panels that behave like Solar Generation panels (they use its battery and its sun curve) but with much bigger numbers. They are an upgrade line that starts from the **Alchemical Solar Panel**, made in a crucible from an Advanced Solar Panel, and branches into several metals.

All toggles are on by default and need a restart. A panel whose mod is missing is simply not registered.

## The upgrade tree

1. **Alchemical Solar Panel**: drop an Advanced Solar Panel of Solar Generation into a crucible filled with Sol and Machina.
2. From the Alchemical panel, the arcane workbench makes the **Brass**, **Brass Andesite**, **Thaumium Zinc** and **Alchemical Cobalt** panels.
3. The Brass panel is the base of the **Thaumium** panel (infusion), which is the base of the **Void Metal** panel, which is the base of the **Void Uranium** panel.

The exact recipes and the numbers are on the next pages.

## Step by step

1. Research "Alchemical Solar Panel" in the Thaumonomicon.
2. Brew the Alchemical Solar Panel in a crucible.
3. Pick a branch and research its entry (each panel has one).
4. Craft or infuse the panel following the recipes page.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for the Brass Andesite or Cobalt panel without the right mod installed: it does not exist then.
- Skipping the Alchemical panel: all other panels are built on top of it.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
