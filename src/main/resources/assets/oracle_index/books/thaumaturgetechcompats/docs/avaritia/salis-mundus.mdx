> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod (Blaze Cubes and the three ingots). The Avaritia Salis Mundus variants can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, needs a restart).

## What it is

Four dusts that work **exactly like the Salis Mundus** of Thaumaturge and wake the same structures, but are made of heavier materials. They exist because the recipes of the high Planetary Endest Pearls and Star Crucifixes ask for them (see the Pearls and Crucifixes chapter).

## How to get them

Each variant is infused on an altar starting from the previous dust, with four units of its material, and gives **four dusts**.

| Dust | Catalyst | Components | Aspects | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:blaze_cube_salis_mundus) | Salis Mundus | 4 Blaze Cubes | Ignis 24, Potentia 16, Praecantatio 12, Vinculum 8 | 4 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_salis_mundus) | Blaze Cube Salis Mundus | 4 Crystal Matrix Ingots | Vitreus 24, Ordo 20, Lux 16, Praecantatio 16, Praemunio 12 | 6 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_salis_mundus) | Crystal Matrix Salis Mundus | 4 Neutron Ingots | Nuclear 24, Metallum 24, Potentia 24, Ordo 16, Perditio 8 | 8 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_salis_mundus) | Neutronium Salis Mundus | 4 Infinity Ingots | Cosmic 32, Alienis 32, Auram 24, Nuclear 16, Vacuos 16, Terminus 8 | 10 |

The Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity dusts can only be infused on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of their own tier, so they bring Solar, Nuclear and Cosmic instability.

## Step by step

1. Research "Salis Mundus Variants".
2. Make the Blaze Cube dust on any altar from a plain Salis Mundus and four Blaze Cubes.
3. Build the altar with the right Runic Matrix at the center, then infuse the next dust using the previous one as catalyst.
4. Keep the dusts for the pearls and crucifixes, or sprinkle them like normal Salis Mundus.

## Common mistakes

- Using the dust as the catalyst when you also need it as an ingredient: the catalyst is consumed like any other infusion catalyst unless a recipe says otherwise, so make extra.
- Starting from the wrong tier: each dust needs the dust of the tier right before it.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
