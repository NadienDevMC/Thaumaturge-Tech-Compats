> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod for the three ingots. The matrix, stone and altar variants each have a switch in the compatibilities config (all on by default, need a restart). Numbers: the infusion config.

## What it is

The infusion altar of Thaumaturge has three kinds of part you can upgrade with the Avaritia metals:

1. the **Runic Matrix** at the center (see Runic Matrices),
2. the **Infusion Cost Stones** and **Infusion Speed Stones** under the pillars (see Runic Stones),
3. the **pillars and pedestals** themselves (see Pillars and Pedestals).

Each part has a Crystal Matrix, a Neutronium and an Infinity tier, and you can mix tiers in one altar.

## The layout

The tiered altar is built exactly like the plain one: **four corner pillars of two stones each (eight pillar stones in all), a pedestal in the middle and a Runic Matrix of any kind** (plain or any tier). Instead of ordinary stones you place the **Infusion Pillar Stones** of a tier, and you wake them by sprinkling Salis Mundus over the structure. The stones turn into **Infusion Pillars** of the same tier, and the look of the pillars follows the stone used.
## Step by step

1. Craft the plain altar structure of Thaumaturge and test it with a normal Runic Matrix.
2. Infuse the Runic Matrix of Crystal Matrix on that altar, then use it as the center to infuse the Neutronium one, and so on.
3. Craft the Pillar Stones of the tier you want (arcane workbench, four at a time).
4. Build the eight-stone structure with a pedestal in the middle and the Runic Matrix, in the same arrangement as the plain altar, and sprinkle Salis Mundus to wake the pillars.
5. Place the stones (cost or speed) under the pillars, and infuse the tiered pedestals if you want them.
6. Always set the matching tier at the center before infusing a tiered item: each item says in its tooltip which Runic Matrix it needs.

## What the tiers change

| Part | Effect on the altar |
| --- | --- |
| Tiered pillars | lower the cost and the cycle time of the altar, and add stability for the plain Runic Matrix |
| Tiered pedestals | lower the cost and the cycle time a little for every pedestal around |
| Cost stones | lower the cost |
| Speed stones | shorten the cycle time |
| Tiered Runic Matrices | no symmetric altar needed, no stability recovery, a special instability and (Infinity) 20x essentia |

The altar never costs less than half (cost multiplier 0.5).

## Common mistakes

- Using the plain Runic Matrix for Neutronium items: they need their own tier at the center.
- Expecting the stability bonus of the pillars to help a tiered matrix: tiered matrices have no stability recovery, so that bonus only matters for the plain Runic Matrix.
- Forgetting the Salis Mundus: the structure is only a pile of stones until you wake it.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's infusion configuration.
