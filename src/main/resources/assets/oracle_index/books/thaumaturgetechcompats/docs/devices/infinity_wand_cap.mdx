> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of the **Avaritia** mod.

## What it is

The Infinity Cap is a Infinity wand part of Thaumaturge. The rod sets how much vis a wand stores and the cap sets what its spells cost; any rod fits any cap. The full Infinity wand (Infinity rod and cap) is built in Avaritia's Extreme Crafting Table and refills every primal aspect on its own.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Spell cost multiplier | 0 (spells are free) |

## How to use it

1. Research Crystal Matrix Wand, then Neutronium Wand, then Infinity Wand.
2. Make the part (see the recipe above); the Neutronium and Infinity parts are infused over the part of the tier before.
3. Assemble a rod and a cap into a wand as with any Thaumaturge wand.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Infinity Wand
> The wand is far too grand for an arcane workbench. It can only be built in an Extreme Crafting Table:
