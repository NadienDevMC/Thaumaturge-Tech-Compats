> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Farmer's Delight** mod. These recipes only exist when both the tainted Farmer's Delight foods and the base tainted foods are on in the compatibilities config, and the tainted foods they use are registered.

## What it is

Besides the crucible, six tainted dishes can be made in the normal Farmer's Delight kitchen, using **tainted ingredients** from the base Tainted Food (tainted carrots, baked potatoes and steak).

## Cooking Pot

Put the ingredients in the Cooking Pot with a **Bowl** as the container. Each recipe cooks for 200 ticks (10 seconds) and gives 1 dish and 1.0 experience.

| Result | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_beef_stew) | Tainted Steak, Tainted Carrot, Tainted Baked Potato |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_fried_rice) | rice, an egg, Tainted Carrot, onion |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_steak_and_potatoes) | Tainted Steak, Tainted Baked Potato, onion |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_vegetable_soup) | Tainted Carrot, Tainted Baked Potato, beetroot, a leafy green |

Rice, eggs, onion, beetroot and leafy greens can be any item of the matching common tag.

## Cutting Board

Place the item on a Cutting Board and cut it with any knife (any item of the knife tag, or one with the knife-dig ability, including the Thaumaturge knives):

| Item on the board | Result |
| --- | --- |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_cooked_beef) | 2 [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_beef_patty) |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_cooked_porkchop) | 2 [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:tainted_cooked_bacon) |

Cutting with the [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:flux_knife) also adds flux to the air, see its page.

## Step by step

1. Make tainted steak, carrots and baked potatoes in the crucible (Tainted Food chapter).
2. Cook them in the pot with a bowl, or cut them on the board.
3. Eat the result; remember it carries Flux Taint.

## Common mistakes

- Using normal carrots or steak in the recipes: they need the tainted versions.
- Forgetting the Bowl in the Cooking Pot.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
