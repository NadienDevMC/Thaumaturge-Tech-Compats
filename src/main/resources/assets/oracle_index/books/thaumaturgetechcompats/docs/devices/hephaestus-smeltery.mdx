> [!NOTE]
> Needs **Hephaestus Forge** (smeltery) and **Avaritia**. The Hephaestus smeltery feature can be switched off in the compatibilities config (needs a game restart).

## What it is

The Hephaestus smeltery refuses the three great metals of Avaritia. Once you finish the research **Smeltery Metals**, they melt like any other ore into their own fluids: Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity. Void metal and alchemical brass need nothing new: Hephaestus Tools already knows them.

| Molten metal | Smeltery heat needed | Melts from |
|---|---|---|
| Crystal Matrix | 1100 | ingots, plates, blocks |
| Neutronium | 1500 | ingots, plates, blocks, nuggets |
| Infinity | 2000 | ingots, plates, blocks, nuggets |

Each has a bucket ([](@thaumaturgetechcompats:molten_crystal_matrix_bucket), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:molten_neutronium_bucket), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:molten_infinity_bucket)).

## Step by step

1. Finish the research.
2. Build a smeltery hot enough for the metal (the higher the metal, the hotter the smeltery).
3. Melt the ingots, plates or blocks. The fluid fills the smeltery tank.
4. Cast it again: ingot cast or plate cast on a casting table, nugget cast (Neutronium and Infinity only), or a casting basin for a block.

## Common mistakes

- Using a smeltery that is not hot enough: Infinity needs a heat of 2000.
- Looking for a Crystal Matrix nugget: it does not exist; only Neutronium and Infinity have one.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
