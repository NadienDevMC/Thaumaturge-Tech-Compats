title: Infusion, Arcane Workbench and Crucible

Most recipes of this mod use one of three Thaumaturge crafting methods:

- **Arcane workbench**: a crafting grid that also needs vis (from a wand or a workbench charger) and sometimes crystals.
- **Crucible**: a cauldron of water that you heat, fill with aspects and drop a catalyst into.
- **Infusion altar**: a Runic Matrix at the centre, a central pedestal with the starting item, pedestals around it with the other ingredients, eight pillar stones, and the essentia the recipe asks for.

Tiered recipes (Crystal Matrix, Neutronium, Infinity; Thaumium, Alchemical Brass, Void Metal) always start from the item of the tier before. Higher Avaritia tiers need an altar whose centre is the Runic Matrix of their own tier, and each one brings its own instability: **solar** (fires, lightning), **nuclear** (radiation, flux, meltdown) and **cosmic** (flux tears, warp, summons, teleports).
