> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Oritech**. In the compatibilities config you can switch off the whole Oritech integration, all the addons, or each addon tier on its own; all default to on. Values live in the Oritech config and need a game restart. A higher tier is only available if the lower tiers of the same addon are enabled.

## What it is

Nine addon blocks for Oritech machines: Speed, Efficiency and Capacitor, each in Brass, Thaumium and Void Metal. They work like Oritech's own addons (placed against the machine) but are stronger, and every tier beats the one before.

## How to get it

Research **Brass Addons**, then **Thaumium Addons**, then **Void Metal Addons** (Tech category). All are crafted at the arcane workbench in the same shape: top and bottom rows a plate of the tier, a mechanism and a plate; middle row the side item of the addon kind, the center addon and the side item again. Each addon page shows its grid.

| Tier | Plate | Mechanism | Center (C) | Vis | Crystals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brass | brass plate | simple | Oritech addon of the same kind | 100 | 2 |
| Thaumium | thaumium plate | complex | the Brass addon of the same kind | 250 | 4 |
| Void Metal | void metal plate | complex | the Thaumium addon of the same kind | 500 | 6 |

| Addon | Center for Brass | Side item S (Brass / Thaumium / Void) | Crystal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speed | Oritech machine speed addon | magnetic coil / overcharged crystal / Heisenberg compensator | aer |
| Efficiency | Oritech machine efficiency addon | electrum ingot (all tiers) | ordo (brass) |
| Capacitor | Oritech machine capacitor addon | energite ingot (all tiers) | ignis |

(For the speed addon the side item changes with the tier; the Thaumium and Void recipes of efficiency and capacitor keep the same side item.)

## Step by step

1. Craft the Oritech addon you want to upgrade and the Brass version around it.
2. Place the addon against the machine like any other Oritech addon.
3. Climb tiers by crafting the next one around the previous.

## Numbers (Oritech config)

| Setting | Brass | Thaumium | Void Metal | Oritech own |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Speed addon: processing time multiplier (lower is faster) | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0.1 | 0.5 |
| Speed addon: energy multiplier | 1.1 | 1.0 | 0.9 | 1.2 |
| Efficiency addon: energy multiplier (lower is cheaper) | 0.6 | 0.4 | 0.2 | 0.8 |
| Capacitor addon: energy capacity added | 10,000,000 | 100,000,000 | 1,000,000,000 | 2,000,000 |
| Capacitor addon: insert rate added | 10,000 | 100,000 | 1,000,000 | 2,000 |

## Common mistakes

- Trying to craft a Thaumium addon without the Brass one of the same kind: it is the center ingredient.
- Disabling the Brass toggle: that also disables Thaumium and Void Metal of that addon.
- Editing the numbers and not restarting: they are read at startup.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Each addon tier can also be turned off on its own.
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Oritech configuration.
