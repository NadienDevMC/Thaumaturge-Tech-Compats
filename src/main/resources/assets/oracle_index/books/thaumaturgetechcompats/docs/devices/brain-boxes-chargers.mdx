## Mnemonic Matrices

### What it is

A Mnemonic Matrix placed against a Thaumatorium lets it remember more formulas at the same time. The ordinary one adds 2. These add more, stack with the ordinary ones and lose no formula. Odd configured values are rounded up to the next even number.

| Matrix | Simultaneous formulas added (default) |
|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_brain_box) | 4 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_brain_box) | 6 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_brain_box) | 10 |

### How to get it

Infusion altar, each built on the one before it (catalyst: an ordinary Mnemonic Matrix, then the Crystal Matrix one, then the Neutronium one). Components: four plates of the tier plus a mind part (Mind Clockwork for Crystal Matrix, Biothaumic Mind for Neutronium, an Extremely Primordial Pearl for Infinity). Place it against the Thaumatorium like any ordinary matrix. Jade shows the formulas it adds.

## Workbench Chargers

### What it is

A Workbench Charger sits on top of an arcane workbench and pulls vis from the aura of the chunks around it. These tiers reach farther and charge less vis for each craft.

| Charger | Radius (chunks, default) | Vis of each craft charged | Discount |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thaumaturge charger | 1 | 100% | 0% |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_workbench_charger) | 2 | 70% | 30% |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_workbench_charger) | 3 | 40% | 60% |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_workbench_charger) | 4 | 10% | 90% |

### How to get it

Infusion altar from the charger of the tier before (the ordinary Arcane Workbench Charger for Crystal Matrix). Components: four plates of the tier, a Vis Resonator and ingots of the tier (two; the Infinity one also needs an Extremely Primordial Pearl).

## Step by step

1. Craft the matrix or charger on an infusion altar whose centre is the Runic Matrix of the tier asked by the research.
2. Place the matrix against the Thaumatorium, or the charger on top of the arcane workbench.
3. Check the numbers with Jade.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting the matrix to replace the ordinary one: it stacks with it.
- Placing the charger where the chunks have no vis: it can only use the aura around it.

## Notes

- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Brain Box and charger configuration.
