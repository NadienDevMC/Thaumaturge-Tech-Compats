## What it is

Thaumaturge golems are magical servants with a **clockwork mind**. They do nothing until you give them tasks with **Control Seals**, and you tune both with the **Golemancer's Bell**.

A golem is made of up to five parts: a **material** (wood, clay, iron, brass, thaumium or void metal), a **head**, **arms**, **legs** and an optional **addon**. The parts decide its traits, health, armor and damage.

## Step by step

1. Complete Discovering Golemancy and Clockwork Minds.
2. Build the **Golem Press** (it is a structure woken with Salis Mundus).
3. In the press, choose material, head, arms, legs and addon. Have the listed parts in your inventory and supply **Machina** essentia through the ports under the press.
4. Place the golem. Without tasks it goes back to the spot where you placed it.
5. Make **seals**: craft a Blank Seal on the Arcane Workbench and use it as the catalyst of a seal recipe in the crucible. Place seals on any block face. They become intangible and are only visible while you hold a golem, a seal or the bell.
6. Click a seal with the bell to set its area, filters, priority (from -5 to +5) and the traits a golem needs. Shift-right-click with the bell picks the seal up.

## Good to know

- Seal types include Collect, Empty, Fill, Guard, Harvest, Lumber, Provide, Stock, Store, Use, Break and Butcher; several have an advanced version with more filters.
- Dye a golem or a seal to pair them: colored seals only give tasks to golems of the same color (or uncolored ones). Picking the golem up clears its color.
- A redstone signal can switch a seal off.
- Sneak and right-click a golem to pick it up.
- With Provide seals, sneak-right-click with the bell to request items from golems within 32 blocks.
