## What it is

The Infinity Plate is a building material of the whole Avaritia progression of this mod: wand caps and rods, improved foci, pillar stones, workbench chargers, mnemonic matrices, smelteries and node devices use it. It is also the material of a Infinity golem in the Golem Press.

## How to use it

1. Craft it at a normal crafting table (three per craft, see the recipe). The Neutronium plate needs a Crystal Matrix plate and the Infinity plate a Neutronium plate, so make them in order.
2. Use it as an ingredient in the recipes listed below.
3. For a golem: finish Material Studies: Avaritia, feed the Golem Press with Machina essentia and press the plate together with a Complex Arcane Mechanism.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Material Studies: Avaritia
> Three ingots pressed in a crafting grid make three plates. Neutronium plates also need a Crystal Matrix Plate, and infinity plates a Neutronium Plate.
> - Crystal Matrix: light, fireproof and blastproof.
> - Neutronium: heavy, armored, fireproof and blastproof.
> - Infinity: the sturdiest of all, brutal, armored, fireproof, blastproof and self-repairing.
> Each plate is the base of a golem in the Golem Press, together with a Complex Arcane Mechanism.
> The plates are also ingredients for the wand caps and rods and for the improved foci of this chapter.
