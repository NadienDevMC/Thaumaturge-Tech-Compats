> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod only for the Blaze Cube, Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity tiers (their ingots and Salis Mundus). Each tier has its own switch in the compatibilities config. Settings: the Star Crucifix config.

## What it is

A cross that waits in your hand until something dares to strike you. Then **starlight chains** leave it, close around the creature, lift it, and drag it underground, where it dies and drops its loot on the surface. The same happens if you point it at a creature and use it.

## How to use it

1. Hold the crucifix in **either hand**.
2. It triggers by itself when a creature strikes you, or you can **aim at a creature within its range** and use it (right click).
3. The chains need at least 20 ticks to close. The creature rises, hangs in the air for the **bind time** of the tier, and is dragged underground. Its loot appears on the surface.
4. Only **one binding can be active for each player** at a time.
5. The blow that triggered it still lands.

## When it fails

A creature with more maximum health than the crucifix allows, and every boss if the crucifix cannot kill bosses, **resists**: the chains strain, the creature roars, the chains snap one by one, and the crucifix **cracks and explodes**, throwing you away, stunned. The crucifix is lost.

Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity and Creative crucifixes kill bosses: bosses are bound and dragged down like anything else, the Wither and the Ender Dragon included, without the crucifix failing. Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity and Creative are also **unbreakable**. Every other crucifix has durability, and **every activation wears one point, even when the creature resists**.

The Creative crucifix has no recipe: it binds anything, kills bosses and is unbreakable, and only exists in the creative tab.

## Reading the tooltip

It shows "Binds creatures of up to X health" or "Binds creatures of any health", the range in blocks, the durability left or "Unbreakable", and "Kills bosses" or "Cannot bind bosses".

## Common mistakes

- Using a low tier on a big creature: if its maximum health is above the limit, the crucifix explodes.
- Trusting a non-boss-killing crucifix against a boss: it will resist and break.
- Forgetting that a failed use still costs durability.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Star Crucifix configuration.
