> [!NOTE]
> These blocks use materials of **Avaritia** (crystal matrix, neutron and infinity ingots). Avaritia is required to craft them.

## What it is

The Essentia Smeltery turns items into essentia slurry. The Thaumaturge smelteries stop at void metal; this mod adds three better ones: [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_smelter), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_smelter) and [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_smelter). They work exactly like the other smelteries and use the same parts around them: alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows. What changes is how much essentia is lost and how fast slurry is handed over.

| Smeltery | Efficiency | Hands over slurry every | Vis cost | Ignis crystals |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Crystal Matrix | 97% | 8 ticks | 1250 | 4 |
| Neutronium | 98.5% | 5 ticks | 2000 | 5 |
| Infinity | 100% (nothing lost) | 2 ticks | 3000 | 6 |

Look at a smeltery with Jade to read its efficiency and smelting interval.

## How to get it

Research **Avaritia Smelteries** in the Thaumonomicon. Each one is crafted at the arcane workbench with the same shape, using the smeltery of the tier before it in the middle:

1. Row 1: ingot, previous smeltery, ingot.
2. Row 2: plate, **Advanced Alchemical Construct**, plate.
3. Row 3: three plates.

| Tier | Ingot | Plate | Previous smeltery (middle) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crystal Matrix | Crystal Matrix Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_plate) | Void Metal Essentia Smeltery |
| Neutronium | Neutron Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_plate) | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_smelter) |
| Infinity | Infinity Ingot | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_plate) | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_smelter) |

Plates are made from ingots in a normal crafting grid (see the Golems page).

## Step by step

1. Complete the research and gather the plates and ingots of your tier.
2. Put the vis and the ignis crystals in the arcane workbench (the recipe needs the vis cost and crystals of the table above).
3. Craft the smeltery with the previous tier in the centre.
4. Place it and connect the alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows the way you would for any other Essentia Smeltery.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to skip a tier: every smeltery needs the one before it, so you must build Crystal Matrix before Neutronium and Neutronium before Infinity.
- Forgetting the Advanced Alchemical Construct.
- Expecting new machine parts: the surrounding parts are the same as for the other smelteries.
