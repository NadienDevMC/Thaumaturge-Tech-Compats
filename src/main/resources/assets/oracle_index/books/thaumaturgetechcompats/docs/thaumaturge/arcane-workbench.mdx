## What it is

The **Arcane Workbench** is a crafting table for magical recipes. It works like a normal crafting table (and can be used as one), but arcane recipes also cost **vis**, which the bench draws from the aura of its chunk, and many of them ask for **vis crystals** of the primal aspects.

## Step by step

1. Sprinkle Salis Mundus on a crafting table to turn it into an Arcane Workbench.
2. Learn the recipe in the Thaumonomicon: arcane recipes stay locked until you have the research.
3. Put the ingredients in the grid and the crystals the recipe asks for in their slots.
4. Slot a wand beside the grid. Its caps discount the vis cost, and a charged wand can pay for the crystals instead: two vis of the matching primal per crystal, with no crystal spent.
5. Take the result. The vis is drawn from the aura of the chunk.

## Good to know

- Without a wand the bench still works, but the crystals are burned as catalysts and the aura is charged a quarter more.
- Staves do not fit in the bench; use a wand or a sceptre.
- The **Workbench Charger**, placed on top of the bench (or of the Focal Manipulator), lets it draw vis from its own chunk and the 8 around it, splitting the cost between them.
- A Vis Relay nearby can also pay crafting costs from an energized node.
- The vis crystals themselves grow in the crucible (see Crucible) or are mined from crystal clusters.

## In Thaumaturge Tech Compats

Many first-tier recipes of this mod are arcane workbench recipes. Higher Workbench Chargers such as the [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_workbench_charger) reach more chunks (see Mnemonic Matrices and Workbench Chargers).
