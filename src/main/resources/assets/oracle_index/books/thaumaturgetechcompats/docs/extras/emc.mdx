---
title: EMC (Equivalent Exchange)
---

> [!NOTE]
> Needs **Equivox** (the EMC mod used by this integration). It can be switched off in the compatibilities config. Recipes apply when data packs reload; base values need a game restart. The research is **Equivalent Exchange**.

## What it is

The integration teaches EMC the worth of Thaumaturge things, so the philosopher's stone can weigh thaumium, alchemical brass, void metal and everything forged from them.

## What gets EMC

- Everything made on the **arcane workbench**, in the **crucible** and on the **infusion altar** gets EMC from the items it is made of, like the vanilla table does with dusts and ingots. The aspects, the vis and the instability of the recipe add nothing.
- Materials that no recipe produces have a value of their own:

| Material | Worth |
|---|---|
| Thaumium Ingot | more than iron, less than gold |
| Alchemical Brass Ingot | more than gold, less than diamond |
| Void Metal Ingot | more than diamond, less than netherite |
| Amber, quicksilver, salis mundus, void seeds, other raw materials | what they would be in the vanilla table |

- Creative items, unbreakable pearls and crucifixes, wands and jars that hold a charge or contents carry **no EMC**.

## Step by step

1. Install Equivox and finish the research.
2. Open the Equivox transmutation interface: the items of this mod appear with their value.
3. To change which recipes or items count, use the buttons *Edit Thaumaturge Tech Compats recipes* and *Edit Thaumaturge Tech Compats exclusions* in the EMC configuration screens.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting EMC for items that hold contents or charge (wands, jars).
- Expecting the vis or the aspects of a recipe to add value.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
