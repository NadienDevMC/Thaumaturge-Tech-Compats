## What it is

The Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Frame is the structure block of the Alchemical Brass magical miner or solar panel. Every tier uses its own frames.

## How to use it

1. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench around the matching frame of the tier before (the base frame of Void Miners for Thaumium), with plates and obsidian.
2. Build the multiblock of its tier with these frames, Void Miners glass panels and modifier slots, around the controller.
3. Do not mix frames of different tiers in one structure.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Alchemical Brass Solar Panel
> The Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Thaumium Solar Panel Controller, alchemical brass plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes.
> Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from alchemical brass plates, obsidian and the matching Thaumium frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.
