## What it is

## Reading the tooltip

Hovering a pearl shows the planets, the damage and its rhythm, the duration and orbit radius, the cooldown, the durability left (or "Unbreakable"), the boss cap or "Kills bosses", and "Disabled" if the tier was turned off.

## Step by step

1. Pick the tier that matches the fight: low tiers are cheap consumables, high ones are for bosses.
2. Check the durability: Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond and Emerald have 3 to 16 throws.
3. For a boss, use a tier that kills bosses (Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity or Creative); the others hit it for at most their boss cap per planet hit.
4. Wait for the cooldown before throwing again.

## Common mistakes

- Thinking a higher pearl is also unbreakable: only Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity and Creative are.
- Comparing damage without the planets: total damage depends on planets times damage over the duration.
