> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Ender IO**. The grinding balls (and the whole Ender IO integration) can be switched off in the compatibilities config; on by default, game restart needed.

## What it is

Three grinding balls for the grinding ball slot of the Ender IO SAG Mill. Each beats the one before: more output, more bonus output, less power use and longer life.

| Ball | Output x | Bonus output x | Power use x | Durability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:brass_grinding_ball) | 1.15 | 1.3 | 0.85 | 60,000 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_grinding_ball) | 1.45 | 1.8 | 0.8 | 110,000 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_grinding_ball) | 1.5 | 2.3 | 0.65 | 140,000 |

## How to get it

Normal crafting table, no research: a plus shape of five ingots (brass, thaumium or void metal) gives **24 balls**.

The ingots go in the top, left, centre, right and bottom slots; the item page of each ball shows the grid.

## Step by step

1. Craft the balls from five ingots of the metal.
2. Open the SAG Mill of Ender IO and put the ball in the grinding ball slot.
3. Feed the mill as usual. The ball changes output, bonus output and power use according to the table.

## Common mistakes

- Expecting the balls to work in a machine that is not the SAG Mill.
- Mixing metals: the recipe needs all five ingots of the same metal.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
