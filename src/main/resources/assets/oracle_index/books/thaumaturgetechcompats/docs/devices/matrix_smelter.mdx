## What it is

The Crystal Matrix Essentia Smeltery is an Essentia Smeltery above the Void Metal one: it turns items into essentia slurry, loses less essentia and hands the slurry over faster. It uses the same parts around it: alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Efficiency | 97% |
| Hands over slurry every | 8 ticks |

## How to use it

1. Research Avaritia Smelteries and craft it at the Arcane Workbench with the smeltery of the tier before in the middle.
2. Place it and connect the alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows as with any Essentia Smeltery.
3. Look at it with Jade to read its efficiency and smelting interval.
4. It is also the heart of the Crystal Matrix Alchemical Furnace.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Avaritia Smelteries
> Each smeltery is built on the one before it, with five plates and two ingots of its own material and an advanced alchemical construct:
> - Crystal Matrix: 97% efficiency, hands slurry over every 8 ticks.
> - Neutronium: 98.5% efficiency, every 5 ticks.
> - Infinity: perfect efficiency, so no essentia is ever lost, every 2 ticks.
> They work exactly like the other smelteries, with the same alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows.
