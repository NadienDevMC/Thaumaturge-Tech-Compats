> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Mekanism**. The energized tools, and each tier on its own, can be switched off in the compatibilities config; all on by default, game restart needed. Numbers are in the Mekanism tools config.

## What it is

Pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and sword in three tiers, 15 tools: **Energized Thaumium**, **Energized Alchemical Brass** and **Energized Void Metal**. They run on Mekanism energy instead of durability: they never lose durability, and every action spends energy. A charge bar below the item shows what is left.

With no energy the tool mines like bare hands (speed 1, no drops that need a tool), and its attack damage is removed. A tooltip says "Out of energy: it does nothing until it is charged".

## How to get it

Research **Energized Thaumium Tools**, then **Energized Alchemical Brass Tools**, then **Energized Void Metal Tools**. Each tool is infused at the altar one by one:

| Tier | Catalyst | Components | Aspects | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | the normal thaumium tool | 2 energy tablets, reinforced alloy, advanced control circuit, 2 thaumium plates | machina 15, potentia 15, metallum 10, instrumentum 10 | 1 |
| Alchemical Brass | a normal iron tool | 2 energy tablets, infused alloy, basic control circuit, 2 brass plates, **the energized thaumium tool of the same kind** | machina 30, potentia 30, metallum 20, instrumentum 20 | 3 |
| Void Metal | the normal void metal tool | 2 energy tablets, atomic alloy, ultimate control circuit, 2 void metal plates, **the energized brass tool of the same kind** | machina 50, potentia 50, vacuos 30, instrumentum 30, praecantatio 20 | 5 |

The previous energized tool is consumed.

## Step by step

1. Infuse the tool.
2. Charge it in a Mekanism energy cube, a charge pad, the charging slot of a Mekanism machine, or with any other energy source.
3. Use it as a normal tool. Each block mined, each hit, and each strip/flatten/till spends energy.
4. Recharge when the bar empties.

| Tier | Capacity | Per block | Per hit | Per use (strip/flatten/till) | Mining speed | Attack damage bonus | Enchantability |

| Thaumium | 250,000 | 600 | 1,000 | 500 | 6.0 | 2.0 | 14 |
| Alchemical Brass | 1,000,000 | 500 | 800 | 400 | 8.0 | 3.0 | 18 |
| Void Metal | 4,000,000 | 400 | 600 | 300 | 10.0 | 3.5 | 22 |

All values can be edited. The capacity needs a restart for the charge bar. Creative mode players spend no energy.

## Common mistakes

- Using the tool with an empty charge bar: it does nothing.
- Trying to craft Brass without the energized Thaumium tool of the same kind in the components.
- Looking for durability: these tools never lose any, only energy matters.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Mekanism tools configuration.
