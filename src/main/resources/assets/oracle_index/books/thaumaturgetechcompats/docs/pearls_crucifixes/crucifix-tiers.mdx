## What it is

Max health is in health points (2 per heart). The Creative crucifix has no recipe.

## Step by step

1. Pick a tier whose max health is higher than the creature you want to remove.
2. Keep it in a hand, or aim at the target within the range and use it.
3. For bosses use Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity or Creative.
4. Watch the durability: every activation wears one point, resisted or not.

## Common mistakes

- Carrying a Thaumium crucifix (500 health) against creatures with more health: it will explode.
- Mixing up range and health: range is how far you can aim, health is how big the creature may be.

## Notes

- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Star Crucifix configuration.
