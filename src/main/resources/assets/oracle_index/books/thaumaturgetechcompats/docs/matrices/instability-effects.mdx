> [!WARNING]
> These effects can kill you, explode your base and teleport you away. Every number is a default and can be changed per matrix in the infusion config.

## What it is

While a tiered Runic Matrix runs an infusion, its stability drops. Once it goes below zero, events fire with a chance each cycle. Every 25 points of stability below zero add one more level of **intensity**, and most amounts are multiplied by it. Events reach the radius of the matrix (6, 8 or 12 blocks). Each effect can be switched off in the config.

## Solar (Crystal Matrix)

Every craft is **twice as unstable by day** (day multiplier 2; the config can also require open sky).

| Event | Chance | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fires around the altar | 0.6 | 4 fire attempts, times intensity |
| Lightning | 0.25 | strikes near the altar |
| Burn | 0.7 | 4 seconds on fire and 2 damage, times intensity |

## Nuclear (Neutronium)

| Event | Chance | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radiation | 0.8 | 2 damage and 10 seconds of weakness, hunger and wither, times intensity |
| Meltdown | 0.08 | explosion of power 3, times intensity up to three times; breaking blocks can be turned off |
| Contamination | 0.5 | pours 8 flux into the aura, times intensity |

## Cosmic (Infinity)

| Event | Chance | Detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flux tear | 0.7 | pours 15 flux, times intensity |
| Warp | 0.6 | 2 warp, times intensity; 0.2 of it is the slow-fading kind instead of temporary |
| Summon | 0.4 | 3 creatures (endermite, vex, silverfish by default; configurable), times intensity up to three times |
| Teleport | 0.5 | throws creatures up to 16 blocks away |

## How to stay alive

1. Keep the instability of the recipe low: pick recipes with low instability for your first crafts. The stability bonus of the pillars only helps the plain Runic Matrix, so with a tiered matrix the recipe itself decides what happens.
2. Stay far outside the radius (12 blocks for Infinity) while the altar works.
3. Bring a way to cure warp and burns, and keep flux jars nearby for nuclear and cosmic altars.
4. If you do not want the destruction, turn off the block breaking of the meltdown, or all the effects of the matrix, in the infusion config.

## Common mistakes

- Standing next to a Nuclear altar: radiation hurts and weakens everything alive.
- Not preparing for flux: Nuclear contamination and Cosmic tears both pour flux into the aura.
- Forgetting that Solar is worst at day, so do Crystal Matrix crafts at night.

## Notes

- Its values are adjusted in the mod's infusion configuration.
