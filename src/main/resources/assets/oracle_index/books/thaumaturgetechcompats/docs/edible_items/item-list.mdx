> [!NOTE]
> Everything here is crafted as the original item plus a Sugar. The creative tab "Edible Items" shows them all.

## What it is

95 edible items in total, in four groups.

## Tool sets

Each set has a pickaxe, a sword, a spear, an axe, a hoe and a shovel (6 items per set):

| Set | Tier | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wooden | 0 | 6 |
| Golden | 0 | 6 |
| Stone | 0 | 6 |
| Copper | 1 | 6 |
| Iron | 1 | 6 |
| Diamond | 2 | 6 |
| Netherite | 3 | 6 |
| Thaumium (Thaumaturge) | 2 | 6, such as [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_thaumium_sword) |
| Void Metal (Thaumaturge) | 3 | 6, such as [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_void_axe) |
| Elemental (Thaumaturge) | 2 | 6 |

Plus the Mace ([](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_mace), tier 2, a weapon) and the Goggles of Revealing ([](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_goggles_revealing), tier 0).

## Gems

Diamond, Emerald, Amethyst Shard, Lapis Lazuli and Quartz, all tier 0 (for example [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_emerald)).

## Doors

All the vanilla wooden doors (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, bamboo, crimson, warped), the iron door and the eight copper doors (plain, exposed, weathered, oxidized and the waxed versions of each), all tier 0, for example [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:edible_oak_door).

## Step by step

1. Find your item in the groups above.
2. Craft it with a Sugar.
3. Look at the Effects and Tiers page for what it gives.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for edible items of other mods: only vanilla and Thaumaturge items are covered.
- Counting the netherite pick as the best for every purpose: the best effect for you depends on the kind (Haste for pickaxes, Absorption for ingots).
