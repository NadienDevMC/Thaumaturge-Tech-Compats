> [!NOTE]
> The pearls need the **Avaritia** mod; the Blaze Cube, Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity tiers too.

## What it is

The Creative Star Crucifix waits in your hand until something strikes you: then starlight chains close around the creature, lift it and drag it underground, where it dies and drops its loot on the surface. You can also aim it at a creature and use it. A creature with more maximum health than it allows (or a boss, unless it kills bosses) resists, and the crucifix cracks and explodes.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Maximum health it binds | any |
| Range | 48 blocks |
| Bind time | 1 seconds |
| Durability | unbreakable |
| Kills bosses | yes |

## How to get it

It has no recipe: it only exists in the creative tab.

## How to use it

1. Research Star Crucifixes, then infuse it with the crucifix of the tier before as the central item (a Totem of Undying for Copper). From Blaze Cube on it also asks for Salis Mundus variants. The Creative crucifix has no recipe.
2. Hold it in either hand: it triggers by itself when a creature strikes you, or aim at a creature within range and use it.
3. The creature rises, hangs for the bind time and is dragged underground. Only one binding per player can be active.
4. Every activation wears one point of durability, even when the creature resists, unless it is unbreakable.
