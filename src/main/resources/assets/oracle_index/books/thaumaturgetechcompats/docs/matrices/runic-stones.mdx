> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. The stone variants can be switched off in the compatibilities config. Numbers: the stones sections of the infusion config, one per tier and kind (cost or speed).

## What it is

The plain Infusion Cost Stone and Infusion Speed Stone help the altar but ask something back. The Avaritia versions ask for less and give more. They go **under the four pillars** of the altar, like the plain stones. The values below are added for **every stone** under a pillar.

| Stone | Cost change | Cycle time change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| plain Cost Stone | -0.02 | +1 |
| plain Speed Stone | +0.01 | -1 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_infusion_cost_stone) | -0.03 | 0 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_infusion_cost_stone) | -0.05 | 0 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_infusion_cost_stone) | -0.125 | 0 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_infusion_speed_stone) | 0 | -2 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_infusion_speed_stone) | 0 | -3 |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_infusion_speed_stone) | -0.01 | -4 |

A negative cost change makes the altar cheaper; a negative cycle time change makes it quicker. The altar **never costs less than half**. The stability modifier of these stones is 0 and tiered matrices ignore it anyway.

## How to get them

Each stone is infused on an altar from the stone before it, with four ingots of its metal (and an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) for Infinity), on an altar whose Runic Matrix is the **tier before** it.

| Stone | Catalyst | Altar center | Aspects | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crystal Matrix Cost | plain Infusion Cost Stone | plain Runic Matrix | Ordo 16, Praecantatio 12, Terminus 2 | 2 |
| Neutronium Cost | Crystal Matrix Cost | Crystal Matrix matrix | Ordo 24, Praecantatio 20, Terminus 4 | 4 |
| Infinity Cost | Neutronium Cost | Neutronium matrix | Ordo 40, Praecantatio 32, Terminus 12 | 6 |
| Crystal Matrix Speed | plain Infusion Speed Stone | plain Runic Matrix | Motus 16, Praecantatio 12, Terminus 2 | 2 |
| Neutronium Speed | Crystal Matrix Speed | Crystal Matrix matrix | Motus 24, Praecantatio 20, Terminus 4 | 4 |
| Infinity Speed | Neutronium Speed | Neutronium matrix | Motus 40, Praecantatio 32, Terminus 12 | 6 |

## Step by step

1. Research "Runic Stones of Avaritia".
2. Build the altar with the matrix of the tier before.
3. Infuse the stone with the previous one as catalyst and four ingots on the pedestals.
4. Put the stones under the four pillars of your working altar.

## Common mistakes

- Using the stone of the same tier at the center: stones and matrices follow the tier **before** them, while all other tiered items need their own tier.
- Expecting more than half off: the altar's cost multiplier never goes below 0.5.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's infusion configuration.
