> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Farmer's Delight** mod. The tainted Farmer's Delight foods can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, need a restart). Settings: the tainted food and food effect sections of the tainted Farmer's Delight config.

## What it is

Fourteen Farmer's Delight dishes with a tainted version. Like the base tainted foods, they fill **more hunger and saturation** (x1.5 by default), keep the effects of the original and strike whoever eats them with **Flux Taint** (10 seconds, level one, by default).

## How to get them

The simplest way is the **crucible**, with the original Farmer's Delight dish as the catalyst and enough Vitium. Research "Tainted Farmer's Feasts".

Some of them can also be made from tainted ingredients in the Cooking Pot or on the Cutting Board (see the next page). The stews and soups come in a bowl.

## Step by step

1. Fill a crucible with the Vitium of the dish.
2. Drop the original dish into the crucible as the catalyst.
3. Collect the tainted dish.

## Common mistakes

- Having the base tainted food switch off: the tainted ingredient recipes of the next page need it, though the crucible recipes work with this chapter's toggle only.
- Removing a dish from the foods list and expecting its recipe to remain: removing the id removes the food and its recipe.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Farmer's Delight tainted food configuration.
