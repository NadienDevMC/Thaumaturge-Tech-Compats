> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of **Avaritia**. The Infinity items also need an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) or Infinity ingots.

## Wand rods and caps

Any rod fits any cap. Spell cost is set by the cap, vis storage by the rod.

| Part | Effect | How to get it |
|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_wand_rod) | Channels vis without losing any; a wand with a Crystal Matrix rod and cap holds 1000 vis and spells cost nothing | Arcane workbench: three Crystal Matrix Ingots in a column |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_wand_cap) | Spells cost nothing | Arcane workbench: three Crystal Matrix Ingots and two Crystal Matrix Plates |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_wand_rod) | Holds 5000 vis | Infusion: two Neutron Ingots and two Neutronium Plates, catalyst Crystal Matrix Rod |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_wand_cap) | Cuts every spell cost by half | Infusion: two Neutron Ingots and two Neutronium Plates, catalyst Crystal Matrix Cap |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_wand_cap) | Spells are free | Infusion on a Neutronium Cap with four Infinity Ingots and two Infinity Plates |

### The Infinity Wand

There is no separate Infinity rod item. The complete wand (Infinity rod and Infinity cap) is built in an Avaritia **Extreme Crafting Table** (tier 4) in a diamond-shaped pattern of Crystal Matrix Ingots, Neutron Ingots, Infinity Ingots and one Block of Infinity in the middle. It refills every primal aspect on its own (this can be turned off in the main config).

## Improved foci

All of them are infused on top of a Greater Focus (or another improved focus).

| Focus | Recipe | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_focus) | Greater Focus + two Crystal Matrix Ingots and two Crystal Matrix Plates | holds a bigger spell than any focus before it |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_focus) | Crystal Matrix Focus + two Infinity Ingots, two Infinity Plates, two Neutronium Plates | the most capable focus |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:blaze_cube_focus) | Greater Focus + two Blaze Cubes and two Crystal Matrix Plates | less deep but with many more branches and repeats, good for fire barrages |

## Scribing tools

A quill and inkwell holding much more ink, used on the research table like ordinary scribing tools.

| Tools | Ink uses | Refill |
|---|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_scribing_tools) | 300 | black dye |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_scribing_tools) | 1000, fireproof | black dye |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_scribing_tools) | 1,000,000, fireproof | restores itself while carried (can be turned off in the scribing config) |

Infuse each from the previous tools (ordinary scribing tools for Crystal Matrix) with four ingots of its material; the Infinity ones also take an Extremely Primordial Pearl.

## Common mistakes

- Mixing up rod and cap: the rod stores vis, the cap lowers cost.
- Trying to infuse without the previous tier as catalyst.
- Trying to build the Infinity Wand at an arcane workbench: it needs the Extreme Crafting Table.
