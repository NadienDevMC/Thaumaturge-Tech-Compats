> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod: the first pearl is made from an Avaritia Endest Pearl. Every tier has its own switch in the compatibilities config (on by default, needs a restart); turning off a tier also turns off the tiers above it. Settings: the Planetary Pearl config.

## What it is

The Endest Pearl of Avaritia opens a gaping void wherever it lands. The Planetary Endest Pearls instead summon **planets** named after those of the sky: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Ceres, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, Haumea and Eris (12 at most). The planets orbit what the pearl hits, each on a different orbit with its own speed and slant, and hurt it again and again until they fade.

## How to use it

1. **Throw it** with right click, like an ender pearl.
2. If it **hits a creature**, the planets orbit that creature.
3. If it **hits a block**, the planets orbit the closest creature within 6 blocks. If there is none, they orbit the point of impact and hurt whatever enters their reach (1.2 blocks).
4. Wait for the planets to fade. The pearl then recharges: each tier has its own cooldown.

Safety and rules (defaults):

- The planets **never hurt the thrower**, nor tamed animals of the thrower. They do hurt other players. All three can be changed in the config.
- Every planet hit ignores the short invulnerability of the victim, so many planets hit together.
- Planet hits knock back only 10% of normal.
- A dimension holds at most 256 planets at once; a throw is cut short if the limit is reached.
- **Bosses** only take a limited amount of damage from each planet (the "boss cap" of the tier), except for the tiers that kill bosses.

## Tiers that kill bosses

The Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity and Creative pearls kill bosses: their planets ignore armor, resistance and the shield of what they hit, can finish the Wither and the Ender Dragon, and finish a creature that survives a hit that should have been lethal. They are also **unbreakable**: they never wear out, only the cooldown holds them back. All other pearls have durability: every throw wears one point and the pearl breaks with the last one.

The Creative pearl has no recipe: it is only in the creative tab.

## Common mistakes

- Throwing it at air: it needs to hit a block or creature.
- Expecting the lowest pearls to kill a boss: they hit it for at most 0.5 to 8 per planet hit.
- Standing in a crowd of other players while hurting players is on: their planets can hurt you.
- Turning off a middle tier in the compats file and wondering why the higher ones vanish: a tier needs every tier below it enabled.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Planetary Pearl configuration.
