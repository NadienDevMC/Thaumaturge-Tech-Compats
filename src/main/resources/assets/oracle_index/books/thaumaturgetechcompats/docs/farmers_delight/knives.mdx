> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Farmer's Delight** mod. The tainted knives can be switched off in the compatibilities config (on by default, need a restart). Stats: the knives section of the tainted Farmer's Delight config.

## What it is

Three knives that are Farmer's Delight knives in every way: they cut on the Cutting Board and count as a knife for any recipe that asks for one. They are sturdier, sharper and easier to enchant than the iron knife, and the best one sometimes leaves **Flux Taint** on what it hits.

| Knife | Durability | Damage (base 0.5 plus) | Attack speed | Mining speed | Enchantability | Taint on hit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Iron Knife (Farmer's Delight) | 375 | +2.0 | -2.0 | 6.0 | 14 | - |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_knife) | 450 | +2.5 | -1.9 | 6.5 | 16 | - |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_knife) | 800 | +3.0 | -1.8 | 7.5 | 20 | - |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_knife) | 1300 | +4.0 | -1.7 | 9.0 | 24 | 35% chance, 5 seconds |

Higher attack speed values are faster. The Thaumium knife uses the iron-tool block rules (cannot mine what iron cannot), the Brass and Void ones the diamond-tool rules. The taint chance and duration can be changed for every knife, and 0 duration removes the effect.

## How to get them

Arcane workbench, plate over the item below, with the vis shown:

| Knife | Recipe | Vis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | Thaumium Plate over a Stick | 20 |
| Alchemical Brass | Alchemical Brass Plate over a Thaumium Knife | 35 |
| Void Metal | Void Metal Plate over an Alchemical Brass Knife | 50 |

Each has its own research: "Thaumium Knife", "Alchemical Brass Knife" and "Void Metal Knife".

## Step by step

1. Research the knife you want.
2. Craft the Thaumium Knife first (it is the base of the chain), then upgrade.
3. Use it on the Cutting Board of Farmer's Delight like any knife.
4. Enchant it: the high enchantability makes good enchantments easier.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to craft a Void Metal Knife without first crafting the Alchemical Brass Knife: it needs it as an ingredient.
- Expecting the Thaumium or Brass knife to taint things: only the Void Metal Knife does by default.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Farmer's Delight tainted food configuration.
