## What it is

The Neutronium Rod is a Neutronium wand part of Thaumaturge. The rod sets how much vis a wand stores and the cap sets what its spells cost; any rod fits any cap. The full Infinity wand (Infinity rod and cap) is built in Avaritia's Extreme Crafting Table and refills every primal aspect on its own.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Vis capacity of the rod | 5,000 |

## How to use it

1. Research Crystal Matrix Wand, then Neutronium Wand, then Infinity Wand.
2. Make the part (see the recipe above); the Neutronium and Infinity parts are infused over the part of the tier before.
3. Assemble a rod and a cap into a wand as with any Thaumaturge wand.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Neutronium Wand
> Both parts are infused with two neutron ingots and two neutronium plates. The Neutronium Cap uses a Crystal Matrix Cap as its catalyst, and the Neutronium Rod uses a Crystal Matrix Rod.
> Assembled into a wand, they hold 5000 vis and cut the cost of every spell by half.
