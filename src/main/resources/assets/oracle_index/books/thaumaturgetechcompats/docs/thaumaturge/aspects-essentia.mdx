## What it is

Every item, block and creature is made of **aspects**: magical elements in varying amounts. A stone may hold only Terra, while a tree or a creature holds several. Thaumaturge has 36 aspects in two kinds:

- **Primal aspects**, the six basic building blocks: **Aer** (air), **Terra** (earth), **Ignis** (fire), **Aqua** (water), **Ordo** (order) and **Perditio** (entropy).
- **Compound aspects**, each made from two other aspects. The simplest come from two primals, like **Victus** (life) = Terra + Aqua; others are built from layers of simpler compounds.

The compound aspects are Alienis, Alkimia, Auram, Aversio, Bestia, Cognitio, Desiderium, Exanimis, Fabrico, Gelum, Herba, Humanus, Instrumentum, Lux, Machina, Metallum, Mortuus, Motus, Permutatio, Potentia, Praecantatio, Praemunio, Sensus, Spiritus, Tenebrae, Vacuos, Victus, Vinculum, Vitium, Vitreus and Volatus.

**Essentia** is an aspect that has been separated from its object and purified: a magical liquid that is both a crafting ingredient and something you can store, pipe and pour.

## Step by step

1. **Scan things.** Point the Thaumometer at a block, item or creature and hold right-click. When it finishes it shows the aspects inside and adds research points of those aspects to your pool. You can only scan something whose aspects you already know (or whose two parts you know).
2. **Read aspects.** Once an item is studied, hold Shift over it to see its aspects and their amounts.
3. **Combine aspects.** On the Research Table, drag two aspects into the runic circles and press the button to try to combine them into a compound aspect. The two aspects are spent even if the combination is wrong.
4. **Get essentia.** Throw items into a heated Crucible to melt them into essentia (see Crucible), or melt them in an Essentia Smeltery and distill them in Arcane Alembics (see Essentia Logistics).
5. **Hold essentia.** Store it in Warded Jars or Glass Phials, or turn it back into a solid crystal with the Essentia Crystallizer.

## Good to know

- An item with no aspects cannot be scanned or melted into essentia.
- Essentia that spills into the open air becomes flux (see Vis, Aura and Flux).

## In Thaumaturge Tech Compats

- **New aspects**: Terminus, Sol, Nuclear and Cosmic, carried by the materials of the mods this addon supports (see Terminus, the Pearl and the Akashic Records, and Tech Compats Essences).
- **Aspects for other mods**: items of Botania get aspects so you can scan them and melt them down (see Aspects of Botania Items).
