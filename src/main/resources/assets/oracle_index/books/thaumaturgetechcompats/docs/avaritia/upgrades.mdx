> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. Each tier is infused on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of that tier (Crystal Matrix: Solar, Neutronium: Nuclear, Infinity: Cosmic).

## What it is

Each Thaumaturge tool or machine below has a Crystal Matrix, a Neutronium and an Infinity version. Every version is made from the previous one on an infusion altar.

## How they are made

- **Scribing Tools**: altar, previous tools as catalyst, 4 ingots of the tier (plus an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) for Infinity). Crystal Matrix starts from ordinary scribing tools.
- **Foci**: altar on top of a Greater Focus. Crystal Matrix Focus: 2 Crystal Matrix Ingots and 2 Crystal Matrix Plates. Infinity Focus: on a Crystal Matrix Focus with 2 Infinity Ingots, 2 Infinity Plates and 2 Neutronium Plates. Blaze Cube Focus: 2 Blaze Cubes and 2 Crystal Matrix Plates.
- **Workbench Chargers**: altar, 4 plates of the tier, a Vis Resonator and 2 ingots, on the charger of the tier before (the Crystal Matrix one starts from an ordinary workbench charger).
- **Mnemonic Matrices**: altar, 4 plates and a Mind Clockwork on the previous matrix.
- **Node Stabilizers**: altar around the previous stabilizer (Advanced Node Stabilizer for Crystal Matrix) with ingots and plates of the tier alternating with Nitor and Alumentum.
- **Node Transducers**: arcane workbench, around the previous transducer, with four plates, two ingots of the tier, a comparator and a Nitor.
- **Smelteries**: arcane workbench, 5 plates and 2 ingots of the tier, the previous smeltery (Void Metal one for Crystal Matrix) and an Advanced Alchemical Construct.
- **Alchemical Furnaces**: build the Advanced Alchemical Furnace structure with the matching smeltery as its heart and sprinkle Salis Mundus; breaking a part turns it back into the smeltery.

## Step by step

1. Research the entry of the upgrade (Avaritia category).
2. Build the altar with the Runic Matrix of the tier in the center.
3. Place the previous item as catalyst, the ingredients on pedestals, and infuse.

## Common mistakes

- Putting the Crystal Matrix item on a plain altar for Neutronium: each tier needs its own center matrix.
- Forgetting the Salis Mundus on the furnaces, which are brought to life like multiblocks.
