> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of **Avaritia**. Three switches in the compatibilities config: Runic Matrix variants, Infusion Cost/Speed Stone variants and Infusion Altar pillar/pedestal variants. The exact values are in the infusion config.

## What it is

Most things in this mod are infused on an infusion altar. The altar itself has Avaritia tiers: Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity. Every tier is built on the one before it.

## Runic Matrices (the heart of the altar)

The Runic Matrix keeps an infusion in order. The Avaritia matrices never ask for a symmetric altar and their stability does not recover; what goes wrong is decided by the instability of the craft, and it is never gentle.

| Matrix | Instability |
|---|---|
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_infusion_matrix) | Solar: burns, sets fires, calls lightning; every craft is more unstable by day |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_infusion_matrix) | Nuclear: radiation, flux poured into the aura, a meltdown that can explode |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_infusion_matrix) | Cosmic: counts every essentia as twenty; tears open flux, gives warp, summons creatures, throws everything nearby elsewhere |

Each one is infused with ingots of its material (the Infinity one also with an [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl)), **on an altar whose centre is the tier before it**.

## Stones, pillars and pedestals

| Part | Effect | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| Infusion Cost Stone / Speed Stone (Crystal Matrix, Neutronium, Infinity) | Go under the four pillars; lower the cost or the cycle time of the altar. It never costs less than half | Infusion from the plain stone, four ingots; Infinity also a Pearl; needs the Runic Matrix of the tier before |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_pillar_stone) and its tiers | Eight stones make the pillars; also lower cost and cycle time | Arcane workbench, four per craft, ingots + Eldritch Stone Tiles (or the tier before); the Crystal Matrix recipe needs 200 vis |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_infusion_pedestal) and its tiers | Where the ingredients go; lower cost and cycle time | Infusion from the previous pedestal (Eldritch Pedestal first) on an altar with the Runic Matrix of its own tier |

Build the altar like the plain one: eight stones, a pedestal and a Runic Matrix of any kind. Sprinkle **Salis Mundus** over the stones to raise the pillars; their look follows the stone used.

## Salis Mundus variants

Dusts infused on an altar from the previous dust, four units of the material each, giving four dusts: Blaze Cube, then [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_salis_mundus), then Neutronium, then Infinity. They work exactly like Salis Mundus; the Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity ones need the Runic Matrix of their own tier on the altar. Planetary pearls and star crucifixes of those levels ask for them.

## Common mistakes

- Infusing a tier part on an altar without the previous tier's matrix at the centre.
- Forgetting the instability: a Neutronium or Infinity matrix is dangerous. Prepare for fire, radiation or cosmic effects.
- Expecting a lower altar cost than half: it is capped.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's infusion configuration.
