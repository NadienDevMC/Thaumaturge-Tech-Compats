## What it is

The Clarity Potion is a drinkable potion that removes all your temporary warp in one drink (by default).

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Removes | all temporary warp |
| Side effect (default) | Nausea for 100 ticks |
| Rest between uses | 20 ticks |

## How to get it

### Crucible

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | Crucible with water, over heat |
| Item to drop in (catalyst) | Sanity Soap |
| Essentia in the crucible | Cognitio 60, Ordo 50, Spiritus 40, Aqua 40, Victus 30, Alienis 20 |
| Research | **Clarity Potion** (Purity tab) |
| Makes | 1 |

## How to use it

1. Research it in the Purity tab.
2. Brew it in a crucible with a Sanity Soap as the catalyst.
3. Drink it: the temporary warp is gone and you get the glass bottle back. It does not touch permanent warp.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Clarity Potion
> The draught is clear as spring water and tastes of nothing. Drunk, it takes the temporary warp out of the mind at once.
> It does not touch the warp that has settled for good, and the drinker may feel a brief unease afterwards.
> 
> **Clarity Potion**
> 
> It is brewed in the crucible from Cognitio, Ordo, Spiritus, Aqua, Victus and Alienis, with a Sanity Soap as the catalyst. How much it removes and its side effect are set in the configuration.
