## What it is

The Discharge Cannon is a weapon that concentrates its stored Dimensional Flux into one high impact shot.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensional Flux stored | 5,000,000 |
| Cost | 1,000,000 DF per shot |
| Attack damage (base 0.5 plus) | 30 |
| Range | 64 blocks |
| Rest between uses | 30 ticks |

## How to use it

1. Infuse it with a Complex Arcane Mechanism as the central item.
2. Keep it charged with an Environmental Charger.
3. Hold right click for 15 ticks to charge it and release to fire.
4. The shot hits what it meets and also half as hard the creatures within 3 blocks of the impact.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Discharge Cannon
> The Discharge Cannon is made on an infusion altar from a complex mechanism, four void metal plates, two void seeds and two vis resonators.
> It is a weapon that concentrates the stored energy to fire high impact projectiles. Hold right click to charge it and release to fire: the shot crosses the air up to its range, hurts what it hits hard and the creatures around the impact a little, and uses a fixed amount of Dimensional Flux. Without charge it will not fire, and it needs a short rest between shots.
