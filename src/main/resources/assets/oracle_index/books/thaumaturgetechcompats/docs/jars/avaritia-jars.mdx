> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. Capacities are set in the jars config.

## What it is

Three bigger versions of the Warded Jar of Thaumaturge. They work like any other warded jar: labels and braces fit them, and the essentia and the aspect filter are kept when you break and pick the jar up.

| Jar | Capacity | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warded Jar (Thaumaturge) | 250 | the base |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_jar) | 500 | |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_jar) | 1000 | fire resistant |
| [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_jar) | 2500 | fire resistant |

Each jar (Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity) has its own capacity in the jars config.

## How to get them

Each jar is infused on an altar starting from the one before it, with the Runic Matrix of its own tier at the center.

| Jar | Catalyst | Components | Aspects | Instability |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Crystal Matrix | plain Warded Jar | 4 Crystal Matrix Ingots | Vitreus 24, Praemunio 16, Potentia 16, Terminus 4 | 3 |
| Neutronium | Crystal Matrix Jar | 4 Neutron Ingots | Metallum 32, Praemunio 32, Vitreus 16, Terminus 8 | 6 |
| Infinity | Neutronium Jar | 4 Infinity Ingots, [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:extremely_primordial_pearl) | Alienis 32, Praemunio 48, Vacuos 32, Terminus 24 | 9 |

## Step by step

1. Research "Jars of Avaritia".
2. Build an altar with the Runic Matrix of the tier at its center (Crystal Matrix: Solar, Neutronium: Nuclear, Infinity: Cosmic).
3. Place the previous jar as the catalyst and the ingots on the pedestals.
4. Provide the aspects and infuse. Use the jar exactly like a normal one.

## Common mistakes

- Skipping a tier: the Infinity jar needs a Neutronium jar, which needs a Crystal Matrix jar.
- Forgetting the new limits: these jars hold from 2 to 10 times what a Warded Jar holds, so plan for the larger amounts.

## Notes

- Its values are adjusted in the mod's jars configuration.
