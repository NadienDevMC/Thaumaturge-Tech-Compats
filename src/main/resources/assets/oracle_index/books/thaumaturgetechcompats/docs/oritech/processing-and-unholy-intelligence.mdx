> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Oritech**. In the compatibilities config you can switch off the processing recipes (or only the pulverizer, the grinder or the bonus) and the Unholy Intelligence; all on by default. Recipes apply when data packs reload.

## What it is

### Pulverizer and Grinder recipes

The Oritech Pulverizer and Grinder (the grinder only exists in some Oritech versions) process Thaumaturge clusters and ores.

| Input (yields shown with the bonus toggle off) | Pulverizer | Grinder (plus extra) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Iron cluster | 2 raw iron | 2 raw iron + 1 raw nickel |
| Copper cluster | 2 raw copper | 2 raw copper + 1 raw gold |
| Gold cluster | 2 raw gold | 2 raw gold + 1 raw copper |
| Cinnabar cluster / ore | 2 raw cinnabar | 2 raw cinnabar + 2 quicksilver nuggets |
| Quartz cluster / ore | 2 quartz | 3 quartz |
| Amber ore | 2 amber | 3 amber |
| Uranium cluster | 2 raw uranium | 3 raw uranium + 1 small plutonium dust |
| Osmium cluster (Mekanism) | 2 raw osmium (150 ticks) | 2 raw osmium + 1 raw platinum |
| Lead / Tin clusters (Mekanism) | 2 raw lead / tin | 2 raw lead / tin + 1 raw tin / lead |

Pulverizer time is 100 ticks (150 for osmium); grinder time is 40 ticks. With the processing bonus on (default) every recipe gives **one extra** raw material; with it off the yield matches Oritech ores.

### Unholy Intelligence

An alternative way to get Oritech's logic core. Research **Unholy Intelligence** (needs Brain in a Jar and Base Artifice, Golemancy theory) and craft at the arcane workbench (100 vis, 2 ordo and 2 perditio crystals):

Top row thaumium plate, clockwork mind and thaumium plate; middle row complex mechanism, Brain in a Jar and complex mechanism; bottom row thaumium plate, Oritech Dubious Container and thaumium plate. Result: Oritech's Unholy Intelligence.

## Step by step

1. Put the cluster or ore into the Pulverizer for the cheap route (more output with bonus) or the Grinder for byproducts.
2. Power the machine with Oritech energy as usual.
3. Collect the raw materials.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for Grinder recipes when your Oritech version has no Grinder.
- Expecting Mekanism cluster recipes without Mekanism.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
