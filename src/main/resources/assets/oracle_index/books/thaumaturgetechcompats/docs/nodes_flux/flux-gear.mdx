> [!NOTE]
> The Portable Void Siphon and Discharge Cannon can each be switched off in the compatibilities config. The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:flux_knife) is a **Farmer's Delight** knife and needs that mod. These items hold Dimensional Flux (DF); recharge them in your inventory with an Environmental Charger.

## Flight Thruster

A chestplate module that holds 200,000 DF. Wear it on the chest. Hold jump in the air to thrust upward (and hold forward to push ahead too), in bursts of 50 ticks followed by a 10-tick cooldown. It uses 800 DF per second of thrust and cancels fall damage while it holds any DF. With no flux it does nothing.

Infusion recipe: a Thaumium Chestplate, three brass plates, two Complex Arcane Mechanisms, a Void Seed and two Vis Resonators.

## Portable Void Siphon

Hold right click to pull items and experience orbs within 8 blocks towards you, using 2,000 DF per second. At the same time it draws up to 0.5 aura flux per second from the chunk (2,000 DF each) into its own store, which also cleans the aura a little. It holds 2,000,000 DF.

Arcane workbench: top row brass plate, Void Seed and brass plate; a Simple Arcane Mechanism in the middle of the second row and a brass plate in the middle of the third (crystals aer 2, perditio 2, ordo 1; 80 vis).

## Discharge Cannon

Hold right click for 15 ticks to charge it, release to fire. The shot crosses up to 64 blocks, deals 30 damage to what it hits and 50% to the creatures within 3 blocks of the impact, and costs 1,000,000 DF (it holds 5,000,000). There is a 30-tick rest between shots.

Infusion recipe: a Complex Arcane Mechanism as the catalyst, four void metal plates, two Void Seeds and two Vis Resonators.

## Flux Knife

A Farmer's Delight knife that bites into the world: **sneak and use it on a block** to open a flux rift beside it, sized to the flux the knife takes from the aura of that chunk. If the chunk does not have enough, it opens nothing. Every rift wears the blade and the knife needs a pause before the next one. On the Cutting Board, food it cuts also adds flux to the air.

Infusion recipe: a Void Metal Knife, two Flux Crystals, Tainted Goo and a Bottle of Taint.

## Step by step

1. Craft the item and keep a charger running near you (thruster, siphon and cannon).
2. Check the stored flux in the tooltip (*Dimensional Flux: x / y DF*).
3. Use it as described above.

## Common mistakes

- Using the cannon or the thruster with no flux: *Not enough Dimensional Flux*.
- Holding the thruster longer than a burst: it overheats and has to cool down.
- Using the Flux Knife in a chunk with little flux: no rift opens.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's flux gear configuration.
