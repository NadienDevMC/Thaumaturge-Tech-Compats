> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Avaritia** mod. The collector itself is Avaritia's Neutron Collector; this mod only makes it faster and more generous.

## What it is

The [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_neutron_collector), [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_neutron_collector) and [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_neutron_collector) are drop-in replacements for Avaritia's Densest Neutronium Collector. They keep its inventory, its menu and its output slot.

## What it does

Every tick the machine runs its normal work **speed times** (2, 4 or 8 by default). Every time a production cycle finishes, it adds extra items to the output slot, so the total per cycle is the production multiplier (1, 2 or 4 by default).

| Setting | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Speed multiplier (per tier) | times the Densest collector works each tick, 1 to 64 |
| Production multiplier (per tier) | items produced per finished cycle, 1 to 64 |

The tiers are Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal.

## Step by step

1. Place the collector like Avaritia's one and right click it to open its menu.
2. Do as you do with the original collector: it works by itself and fills its output slot.
3. Take the output with a hopper or by hand.

## Common mistakes

- Letting the output slot fill up: the extra items are only added if there is room in the slot (stack limit), so empty it often or pipe it away.
- Expecting different outputs: the products are the same as Avaritia's collector, only more and faster.
