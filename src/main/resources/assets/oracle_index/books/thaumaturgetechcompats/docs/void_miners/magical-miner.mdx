> [!NOTE]
> Requires **Void Miners Remastered**. The miner, and each tier on its own, can be switched off in the compatibilities config; on by default, game restart needed. Numbers are in the Void Miners magical config.

## What it is

A Void Miners miner that takes **no FE at all**: it works only while it can pay a few units of power to a magical source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. It is a multiblock like the Rubetine miner: the controller plus frames, glass panels and miner modifiers.

| Tier | Controller | Mining tier (Void Miners recipes) | Speed x | Production x | Units per payment | Source radius |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_miner) | 3 | 1.5 | 1.5 | 1 | 8 |

(Mining tiers go from 1 Rubetine to 9 Ultimate and can be changed in the config.)

## How to get it

Research the entry of the tier in the Tech category (Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal Magical Miner). Infusion altar, each over the tier before:

| Tier | Catalyst | Components | Aspects (instability) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thaumium | Void Miners Rubetine Miner | 3 thaumium plates, simple mechanism, 2 Flux Jars | machina 12, terra 8, metallum 8, potentia 6 (2) |
| Alchemical Brass | Thaumium Miner Controller | 3 brass plates, simple mechanism, 2 Flux Jars | machina 18, terra 12, praecantatio 10, potentia 12 (4) |
| Void Metal | Alchemical Brass Miner Controller | 3 void metal plates, complex mechanism, 2 Flux Jars, void seed | machina 24, terra 16, vacuos 16, potentia 12 (6) |

## Step by step

1. Craft the miner frames and any modifiers (see the Frames, Upgrades and Power Sources page).
2. Build the multiblock of the tier around the controller, using its frame, Void Miners glass panels and modifier slots.
3. Place a power source within the tier radius of the controller: a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar, a world node or a flux rift. A hungry node is never valid.
4. The miner pays its units every 20 ticks (the interval of the config). While paid it mines; when payment fails it stops until the next try.
5. Take the output as you would from a Void Miners miner.

## Common mistakes

- No power source in range: the miner does nothing.
- A hungry node as the source: it is never accepted.
- Using frames of another tier in the structure.

## Notes

- It can be turned off in the mod's configuration (compatibilities file).
- Its values are adjusted in the mod's Void Miners magical configuration.
