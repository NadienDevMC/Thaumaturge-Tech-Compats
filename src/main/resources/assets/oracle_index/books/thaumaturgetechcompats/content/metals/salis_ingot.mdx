## What it is

Salis Ingot, the base of the Salis metal: nuggets, blocks, wand caps and rods and Hephaestus Tools parts are made from it.

**Made from (same family):** [Molten Salis](@thaumaturgetechcompats:molten_salis), [Salis Block](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_block), [Salis Native Cluster](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_native_cluster), [Salis Nugget](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_nugget)

**Used to make (same family):** [Molten Salis](@thaumaturgetechcompats:molten_salis), [Salis Block](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_block), [Salis Nugget](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_nugget)

## Step by step

1. Get a Void Metal Ingot from Thaumaturge and finish the research you need.
2. Fibric, Salis or Crimson: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Put the Void Metal Ingot on the central pedestal and the themed material on the pedestals around it, then supply the two aspects (Fibric: fabrico and vitium; Salis: praecantatio and potentia; Crimson: perditio and humanus).
3. Make nuggets and blocks with the crafting grid; smelt Native Clusters in a furnace or blast furnace for two ingots each.
4. Use the ingots for wand rods and caps (below) and for Hephaestus Tools materials (see the next page).

## Tips and common mistakes

- Native Clusters have no generation route in this mod: they are meant to be found in the creative tab or provided by other content; smelting them gives two ingots.
