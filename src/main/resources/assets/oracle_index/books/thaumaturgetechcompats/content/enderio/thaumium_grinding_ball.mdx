> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Ender IO** mod.

## What it is

The Thaumium Grinding Ball goes in the grinding ball slot of the Ender IO SAG Mill: more output, more bonus output, less power use and a longer life than the balls before it.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Output multiplier | 1.45 |
| Bonus output multiplier | 1.8 |
| Power use multiplier | 0.8 |
| Durability | 110,000 |

## How to use it

1. Craft 24 balls at a crafting table from five ingots of the metal in a plus shape (no research needed).
2. Open the SAG Mill of Ender IO and put the ball in the grinding ball slot.
3. Feed the mill as usual; the ball wears down with use.
