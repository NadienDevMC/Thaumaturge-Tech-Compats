## What it is

The Thaumium Miner Frame is the structure block of the Thaumium magical miner or solar panel. Every tier uses its own frames.

| --- | --- |
| Structure | 5x5 |

## How to use it

1. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench around the matching frame of the tier before (the base frame of Void Miners for Thaumium), with plates and obsidian.
2. Build the multiblock of its tier with these frames, Void Miners glass panels and modifier slots, around the controller.
3. Do not mix frames of different tiers in one structure.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Thaumium Miner
> The Thaumium Miner Controller is made on an infusion altar from a Rubetine Miner, thaumium plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.
> Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from thaumium plates, obsidian and a base frame. The structure is built out of these frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, and it takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.
