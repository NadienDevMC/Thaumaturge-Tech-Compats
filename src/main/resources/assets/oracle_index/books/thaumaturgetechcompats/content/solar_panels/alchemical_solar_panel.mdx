> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Solar Generation** mod.

## What it is

The Alchemical Solar Panel is a Solar Generation panel with much bigger numbers. Every tick it produces its generation multiplied by the sun intensity of its position, stores it in its battery and offers it to every side except the top.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Generation under full sun (FE per tick) | 89,500 |
| Transfer (FE per tick) | 50,500 |
| Capacity (FE) | 18,000,000 |

## How to get it

### Crucible

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | Crucible with water, over heat |
| Item to drop in (catalyst) | Advanced Solar Panel |
| Essentia in the crucible | Sol 20, Machina 10 |
| Research | **Alchemical Solar Panel** (Solar Panels tab) |
| Makes | 1 |

## How to use it

1. Research its entry in the Solar Panels tab and make it (the Alchemical panel in a crucible; the branch panels at the Arcane Workbench around the Alchemical panel; Thaumium, Void Metal and Void Uranium infused in a chain from the Brass panel).
2. Place it with the top free and open to the sky.
3. Put a cable, battery or machine on any other side.
4. Right click it to see the stored energy and the current production. Broken and picked up, it keeps its energy.
