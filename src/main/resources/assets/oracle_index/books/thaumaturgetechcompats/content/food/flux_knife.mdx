## What it is

The Flux Knife is a Farmer's Delight knife that bites into the world itself: sneak and use it on a block to open a flux rift beside it, sized to the flux it takes from the aura of that chunk. If the chunk does not have enough, it opens nothing. Every rift wears the blade, and the knife needs a pause before the next one.

**Made from (same family):** [Void Metal Knife](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_knife)

## How to get it

A seam in the air, and the kitchen smells of rust.

Infuse a void metal knife with two Flux Crystals, Tainted Goo and a Bottle of Taint. Sneak and use it on a block to open a flux rift beside it. The rift is sized to the flux it takes from the aura of that chunk, and if the chunk does not have enough the knife opens nothing. Every rift wears the blade and the knife needs a pause before the next one.

On the Cutting Board it is a Farmer's Delight knife in every way, except that the food it cuts also adds flux to the air, more for what feeds better. Depending on the configuration it may not leave the food at all.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Thaumium Knife: a thaumium plate over a stick. Alchemical Brass Knife: an alchemical brass plate over a Thaumium Knife. Void Metal Knife: a void metal plate over an Alchemical Brass Knife.
3. Use it as any Farmer's Delight knife: it cuts on the Cutting Board and counts as a knife for every recipe that asks for one. Each tier lasts longer, hits harder and enchants better; the Void Metal one sometimes leaves Flux Taint on what it hits.
4. Flux Knife: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item is a Void Metal Knife, with two Flux Crystals, Tainted Goo and a Bottle of Taint.
5. Sneak and use the Flux Knife on a block to open a flux rift beside it, sized to the flux it takes from the aura of that chunk (nothing opens if the chunk does not have enough). Every rift wears the blade and the knife needs a pause before the next one.
