## What it is

Inert Salis Cap, the uncharged Salis wand cap. Charge it in an infusion to get the Salis Cap.

**Used to make (same family):** [Salis Cap](@thaumaturgetechcompats:salis_wand_cap)

## Step by step

1. Craft the inert cap and the inert rod at an arcane workbench.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Use the inert part as the starting item with the Salis Mundus and ingots above.
3. Assemble a wand from any rod and any cap.
4. Salis and Rifting rods glow; Salis has the largest vis capacity of the four.
