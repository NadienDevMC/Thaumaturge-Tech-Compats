## What it is

Higher tiers of backpacks, barrels, chests, shulker boxes and stack upgrades, plus an Arcane Workbench upgrade, for Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage.

## How to get it

The Arcane Workbench Upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from a Crafting Upgrade, an Arcane Workbench, thaumium plates and a few crystals of each primal aspect.

In a backpack or storage it opens a tab identical to the one of the Crafting Upgrade, with the same crafting grid, result slot, shift click target and refill buttons, plus six slots for the primal crystals the recipes ask for, a wand slot that pays the vis like the wand of an arcane workbench, and a charger slot that takes a workbench charger and gives its vis discount and radius. It crafts every recipe of the arcane workbench I have researched, and the vis comes from the aura of the chunk I stand in.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Backpacks and storage (barrels, limited barrels, chests, shulker boxes): craft each from the one below it surrounded by eight plates of the new metal (Thaumium from Netherite, Alchemical Brass from Thaumium, Void Metal from Alchemical Brass). They keep contents and upgrades.
3. Arcane Workbench Upgrade: Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. It uses a Crafting Upgrade, an Arcane Workbench, thaumium plates and a few primal crystals. In a backpack or storage it opens a tab like the Crafting Upgrade plus six primal crystal slots, a wand slot and a charger slot; the vis comes from the aura of the chunk you stand in.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Slot counts, upgrade slots and multipliers are set in the configuration.
