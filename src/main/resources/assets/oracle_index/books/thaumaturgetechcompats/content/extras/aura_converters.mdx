## What it is

The Thaumium Aura Converter drains vis from the aura of its chunk and turns it into FE, which it gives to every neighbouring block that accepts it. It never takes energy in.

## How to get it

### Aura Converter

The resonator has been tuned to the aura of the chunk and the thaumium carries the current away.

#### Thaumium Aura Converter

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from thaumium plates, copper, redstone, a vis resonator and a simple mechanism. It drains vis from the aura of its chunk and turns every vis into 250,000 FE. It gives out up to 250,000 FE per tick and stores 10,000,000 FE. It gives the energy to every neighbouring block that accepts it and never takes any in.

Right-click it to see the stored energy, the aura of the chunk, its state and its rate. Every vis it converts leaves a little flux behind, it never drains the aura under a quarter of its base, and all the converters of a chunk share a limit on what they can drain. A redstone signal stops it. The figures can be changed in the configuration.

### Alchemical Brass Aura Converter

The brass wraps the resonator and nothing is lost on the way out.

#### Alchemical Brass Aura Converter

It is infused over a thaumium aura converter with alchemical brass plates, a simple mechanism and a vis resonator. Every vis becomes 1,000,000 FE. It gives out up to 1,000,000 FE per tick and stores 100,000,000 FE.

### Void Metal Aura Converter

The void drinks the aura and gives it back as pure power.

#### Void Metal Aura Converter

It is infused over an alchemical brass aura converter with void metal plates, a complex mechanism and two vis resonators. Every vis becomes 560,000,000 FE. It gives out up to 500,000,000 FE per tick and stores 10,000,000,000 FE. The limit of the chunk keeps it from emptying the aura on its own.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Thaumium: Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Use thaumium plates, copper, redstone, a vis resonator and a simple mechanism. Alchemical Brass and Void Metal: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Start from the previous converter, with plates of the new metal, a mechanism and vis resonators.
3. Place the converter against a block that accepts FE (a cable, a machine, a battery). It gives energy to every neighbouring block that accepts it and never takes any in.
4. It drains vis from the aura of its chunk: every vis becomes 250,000 FE (Thaumium), 1,000,000 FE (Alchemical Brass) or 560,000,000 FE (Void Metal), at up to 250,000, 1,000,000 or 500,000,000 FE per tick. Right-click it to see the stored energy, the aura of the chunk, its state and its rate.
5. Redstone signal stops it.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Every vis converted leaves a little flux behind, the converter never drains the aura under a quarter of its base, and all the converters of a chunk share a limit on what they can drain.
