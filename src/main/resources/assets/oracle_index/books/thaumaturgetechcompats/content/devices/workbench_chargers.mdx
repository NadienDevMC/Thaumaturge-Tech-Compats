## What it is

A Workbench Charger of Crystal Matrix. It sits on top of an arcane workbench and pulls vis from the aura of the chunks around it, reaching farther and charging less of the vis of every craft than the charger before it.

## How to get it

Each charger is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it:

- The Crystal Matrix one starts from an ordinary workbench charger.
- The Neutronium one starts from the Crystal Matrix one.
- The Infinity one starts from the Neutronium one.

They sit on top of the workbench like the ordinary one. By default they draw from 2, 3 and 4 chunks around it and charge only 70%, 40% and 10% of the vis of each craft.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the previous charger (an ordinary Workbench Charger for Crystal Matrix).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Place the charger on top of an arcane workbench. By default the three tiers draw vis from 2, 3 and 4 chunks around it and charge only 70%, 40% and 10% of the vis of every craft.
