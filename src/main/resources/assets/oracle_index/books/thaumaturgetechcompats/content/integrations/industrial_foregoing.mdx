## What it is

The Magical Laser Drill works like the Laser Drill of Industrial Foregoing, with the same screen painted purple, but its bar holds vis instead of power. Place it with its emitting face looking at any Laser Base, mundane or magical, inside its working area, and every operation spends 1 vis and gives a step of work to the base. The vis comes from the aura of its chunk and, if the aura cannot pay, from a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or a flux rift within reach; a hungry node never counts.

## How to get it

### Magical Laser Drill

The Magical Laser Drill is born on an infusion altar from an ordinary Laser Drill. It looks and works like the original, with the same screen painted purple, but its bar holds vis instead of power.

Place it so that its emitting face looks at any Laser Base, mundane or magical. The base must be inside the area of three blocks per side centred two blocks in front of it, and the screen shows the target it found. Every operation spends 1 vis, which is worth the 1000 power of the original, and gives one step of work to the base.

The vis is taken from the aura of its own chunk and, when the aura cannot pay, from a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or a flux rift within reach. A hungry node is never a source. Redstone, addons and the settings of the machine work as in the original. The cost, the speed, the buffer and the sources can all be changed in its configuration.

### Magical Ore Laser Base

The Magical Ore Laser Base is born on an infusion altar from an ordinary Ore Laser Base and a diamond pickaxe. It is the same machine with its screen painted purple: six lens slots, fifteen output slots and the depth that decides which ores can appear.

Lenses raise the chance of the ores of their color, and the work needs no power of its own: it advances every time a Laser Drill, mundane or magical, sends its beam into it. An efficiency addon shortens the work. Hoppers and pipes can take the output out as in the original.

The number of steps, the weight given by each lens and the base itself can be changed in its configuration.

### Magical Fluid Laser Base

The Magical Fluid Laser Base is born on an infusion altar from an ordinary Fluid Laser Base and a bucket. It is the same machine with its screen painted purple: one lens slot that chooses the fluid, the depth that decides where it can be found and a tank of 32000 mB with its bucket slots.

It needs no power of its own: it advances every time a Laser Drill, mundane or magical, sends its beam into it. An efficiency addon shortens the work, and the fluid can be pumped out of the tank as in the original.

The number of steps and the base itself can be changed in its configuration.

### Magical Infinity Drill

The Magical Infinity Drill is born on an infusion altar from an Infinity Drill. It mines a whole area at once and drops everything at my feet, just like the original, but it holds vis instead of power. A Recharge Pedestal fills it, and while its charging is on it also draws vis slowly from the aura around me.

The more vis it holds, the wider the area: nothing at first, then 3x3, 5x5, 7x7, 9x9, 11x11 and, when it is almost full, the Artifact level, a 13x13x13 cube. Every mined block costs 1 vis and the drill falls back to a smaller level as it empties. Its tank takes Liquid Death, which is spent instead of vis without lowering the level. Right click on a source of Liquid Death fills the tank.

Shift and right click steps the area down one level, wrapping back to the largest one. Right click on air turns the charging on or off. The vis cost, the levels, the speed, the tank and the rest can be changed in its configuration.

### Brass Industrial Addons

The chip has set. Speed, efficiency, processing and range: a brass addon for each, and every one of them stronger than the diamond originals.

#### Brass Addons

They are made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from three brass plates, redstone, a glass pane, the material of each addon and Liquid Death. Place them in the addon slots of a machine, or use them on the machine directly. The values can be changed in the configuration.

### Thaumium Industrial Addons

The thaumium has taken to the chip like a second circuit.

#### Thaumium Addons

Each one is made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from the brass addon of the same kind, three thaumium plates, a simple mechanism, the material of the addon, a diamond and a larger measure of Liquid Death.

### Void Metal Industrial Addons

The chips are dark, cold and impossibly quick.

#### Void Metal Addons

Each one is made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from the thaumium addon of the same kind, three void metal plates, a complex mechanism, the material of the addon, an eye of ender and a great deal of Liquid Death.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Laser drill and bases: place the Magical Laser Drill with its emitting face looking at any Laser Base (mundane or magical) inside the area of three blocks per side centred two blocks in front of it, and Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source. Every operation spends 1 vis. Put lenses and an efficiency addon in the base; take the output with hoppers or pipes.
4. Magical Infinity Drill: fill it with a Recharge Pedestal (and Liquid Death in its tank). Right click on air toggles charging; Shift and right click steps the mining area down. The more vis it holds the wider the area, up to a 13x13x13 cube.
5. Addons: craft each in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber (Brass, Thaumium, Void Metal tiers) and put it in the addon slots of a machine, or use it on the machine directly.
