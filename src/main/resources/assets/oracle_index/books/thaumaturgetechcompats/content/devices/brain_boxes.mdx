## What it is

A Mnemonic Matrix of Crystal Matrix. Placed against a thaumatorium, it lets it remember more formulas at the same time without losing any, and it stacks with the ordinary ones.

## How to get it

Each matrix is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it:

- The Crystal Matrix one starts from an ordinary mnemonic matrix.
- The Neutronium one starts from the Crystal Matrix one.
- The Infinity one starts from the Neutronium one.

They are placed against a thaumatorium exactly like the ordinary ones, and they stack with them. By default they allow 4, 6 and 10 formulas at the same time.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the previous matrix (an ordinary Mnemonic Matrix for Crystal Matrix).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Place the finished matrix against a thaumatorium, exactly like the ordinary ones. It stacks with them: by default the three tiers allow 4, 6 and 10 formulas at the same time.
