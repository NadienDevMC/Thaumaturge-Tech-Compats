> [!NOTE]
> Needs materials of the **Avaritia** mod.

## What it is

The Neutronium Workbench Charger sits on top of an Arcane Workbench and pulls vis from the aura of the chunks around it. It reaches farther than the ordinary charger and charges only part of the vis of each craft.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Vis radius | 3 chunks |
| Vis of each craft that is charged | 40% |

## How to use it

1. Research Workbench Chargers of Avaritia.
2. Infuse it over the charger of the tier before (the ordinary Arcane Workbench Charger for Crystal Matrix).
3. Place it on top of the Arcane Workbench.
4. Craft as usual: the vis comes from the chunks in its radius. Jade shows its discount and radius.
