## What it is

The edible version of Goggles of Revealing, made by crafting the original with sugar. Eating it gives a status effect that depends on the kind of item and its tier: haste for pickaxes, health boost and regeneration for weapons, speed for axes, luck for hoes, jump boost for shovels, night vision for goggles and gems, absorption for ingots and resistance for doors.

## Step by step

1. Take the original item (a diamond pickaxe, an iron ingot, an oak door...) and put it in a crafting grid with sugar.
2. Take the edible copy. It no longer works as the tool: it is food.
3. Eat it to get the effect for its class and tier.
