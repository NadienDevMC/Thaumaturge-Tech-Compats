## What it is

A power network of its own: Dimensional Flux is produced from flux rifts, moved by linked beams, stored in a multiblock core and spent by chargers and gear.

## How to get it

The Rift Extractor is made on an infusion altar from a Flux Jar, a void seed, four void metal plates and a complex arcane mechanism.

It absorbs energy directly from an open flux rift within reach, taking one point of its size every so often, and turns each point into Dimensional Flux. It stops once the rift is down to its smallest size or once it has no room left to store more. The Flux it holds is pushed into the storages and Transmission Links that touch it.

## Step by step

1. Start with a producer: a Rift Extractor next to an open flux rift. It takes one point of the rift every so often and turns it into Dimensional Flux (DF), and pushes it into the storages and links that touch it.
2. Connect distant parts with Transmission Links: craft two at an arcane workbench, use the Transmission Linker on one and then on the other to pair them, and sneak-use the linker on a link to break the pair. Flux flows both ways until both are equally full; links side by side chain flux.
3. Raise the capacity with Capacity Cells on pedestals within six blocks of the core, and keep node stabilizers within eight blocks: above half full and short of stabilizer points the core overloads, releases flux and hurts whoever is near.
4. Spend flux: an Environmental Charger gives it to every charged item, wand and vis item of every player in its radius; push flux into it with links, cores or extractors. Right-click it to read its radius and rate.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Links of different tiers pair, but the pair reaches only as far and moves only as much as the weaker one. A pair only works while both ends are loaded.
- Until the core ring is complete it neither stores nor passes on flux. Blocks of a higher tier count for the ring.
