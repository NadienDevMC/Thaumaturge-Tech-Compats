## What it is

An Infusion Cost Stone of Crystal Matrix. It goes under the four pillars of an infusion altar like the plain stone, and each tier lowers the cost of the altar more than the one before. The altar never costs less than half.

## How to get it

Each stone is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it, with four ingots of its metal and, for the Infinity ones, an Extremely Primordial Pearl:

- The Crystal Matrix stones start from the Infusion Cost Stone or the Infusion Speed Stone.
- The Neutronium stones start from the Crystal Matrix ones.
- The Infinity stones start from the Neutronium ones.

Each stone can only be infused on an altar whose Runic Matrix is the tier before it: the Crystal Matrix ones on a plain Runic Matrix, the Neutronium ones on the Crystal Matrix one and the Infinity ones on the Neutronium one.

They go under the four pillars of the altar just like the plain stones, and each tier lowers the cost or the cycle time of the altar more than the one before. The altar never costs less than half, and the exact values are set in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the plain Infusion Cost Stone or Speed Stone (or the previous tier), and the altar centre must be the Runic Matrix of the tier before it.
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Put the stones under the four pillars of your altar like the plain ones. The Cost stone lowers the cost of the altar; the Speed stone shortens its cycle. The altar never costs less than half.
