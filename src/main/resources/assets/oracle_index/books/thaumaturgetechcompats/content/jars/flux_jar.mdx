## What it is

The Flux Jar slowly pulls flux from the aura of its chunk until it is full. Machines that work on magical power can use that flux as their power source. A hungry node is never a safe source.

**Used to make (same family):** [Crystal Matrix Flux Jar](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_flux_jar)

## How to get it

The Flux Jar is made on an arcane workbench from a warded jar surrounded by four void metal plates.

It slowly pulls flux from the aura around it until it is full, and machines that work on magical power can use that flux as their source. A hungry node is never a safe source, and neither is a jar that has been broken: breaking the Flux Jar pours everything it holds back into the aura.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Surround a Warded Jar with four void metal plates. For the Avaritia tiers, infuse the previous jar with ingots as the list above shows.
3. Place the Flux Jar on the ground: it slowly pulls flux from the aura of its chunk until it is full. Machines that use magical power can use that flux as their source (see Magical Power Sources).
4. To collect the flux, use an empty bucket on the jar: you get a bucket of liquid flux.
5. Optional: put a crucible beside the jar so its leaks and overflow go into the jar instead of polluting the aura.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Do not break a Flux Jar that is full: everything it holds pours back into the aura as flux.
- If the jar is full or there is none, the leaks of a nearby crucible vanish and give temporary warp to players near it.
