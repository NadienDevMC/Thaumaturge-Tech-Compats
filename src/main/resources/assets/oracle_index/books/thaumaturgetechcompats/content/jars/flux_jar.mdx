> [!NOTE]
> The Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity jars need the **Avaritia** mod.

## What it is

The Flux Jar slowly pulls **flux** out of the aura of its chunk until it is full. Machines that work on magical power use that flux as their power source, so the jar turns a pollution problem into fuel. A crucible next to it sends its leaks and overflow into the jar instead of the aura.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Flux capacity | 100 |
| Flux pulled every 20 ticks | 0.25 |

## How to use it

1. Make it (the Flux Jar at the Arcane Workbench; the higher ones infused from the jar before, with the Runic Matrix of their tier at the center).
2. Place it in the chunk whose flux you want to use and wait: it fills whenever the aura has flux.
3. Put a machine that uses magical power next to it (Thaummotor, magical miners and panels...).
4. Right click it to read its flux. An empty bucket takes out 10 flux as a bucket of liquid flux.
5. Do not break a full jar in your base: everything it held goes back into the aura.

> It slowly pulls flux from the aura around it until it is full, and machines that work on magical power can use that flux as their source. A hungry node is never a safe source, and neither is a jar that has been broken: breaking the Flux Jar pours everything it holds back into the aura.
