## What it is

A Amber Resource Node: a bedrock node that Oritech's Deep Drill can bleed forever, giving amber without ever running dry. It also generates in the overworld like the nodes of Oritech, with a boulder of deepslate on the surface.

## How to get it

On the infusion altar, a resource node of Oritech surrounded by the ore and the product of the material becomes a node of that material:

- Cinnabar (from the Redstone node), Amber (Gold node) and Quartz (Lapis node), with the Thaumaturge ore and its drop.

Under Oritech's Deep Drill each node gives its material forever: raw cinnabar, amber or quartz. They also generate in the overworld like the nodes of Oritech, with a boulder of deepslate on the surface.

The amount per cycle and how rare each node is can be changed in the configuration, and every node can be turned off there.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the Oritech resource node of the base material (Redstone for Cinnabar, Gold for Amber, Lapis for Quartz), surrounded by the ore and the product of the material.
3. Place the node, or find them in the overworld, generated like Oritech nodes with a boulder of deepslate on the surface.
4. Put an Oritech Deep Drill over it: each node gives its material forever (raw cinnabar, amber or quartz).
