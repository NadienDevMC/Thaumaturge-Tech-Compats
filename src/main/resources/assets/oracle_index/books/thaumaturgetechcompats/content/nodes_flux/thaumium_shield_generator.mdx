## What it is

Tools and field devices powered by Dimensional Flux: the Portable Void Siphon, the Discharge Cannon, the Flight Thruster, the Shield Generator and the Arcane Terraformer.

**Used to make (same family):** [Alchemical Brass Shield Generator](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_shield_generator)

## How to get it

The Thaumium Shield Generator is made on an arcane workbench from four thaumium plates, two mirrored glass, two simple mechanisms and a vis resonator.

While it holds Dimensional Flux it raises a bubble around itself that destroys the projectiles of hostile mobs, pushes hostile mobs out and takes the damage out of explosions inside. Every tick of the bubble costs flux, and so does every projectile and every explosion it absorbs. When the flux runs out it shuts down, and it starts again once it has recovered enough. Right-click it to read its state.

## Step by step

1. Charge every item with Dimensional Flux (DF): keep an Environmental Charger running near you, or use links and storages (see the Dimensional Flux Network).
2. Shield Generator: place it and give it DF. While it holds flux it raises a bubble that destroys hostile projectiles, pushes hostile mobs out and takes the damage out of explosions inside; it shuts down when flux runs out and restarts once it recovers.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Biomes and dimensions listed in the configuration are left alone by the terraformer. Without DF, none of these devices work. Higher tiers (Alchemical Brass, Void Metal) hold more flux and reach farther, at a bigger flux cost per tick.
