The wand is far too grand for an arcane workbench. It is built in an **Extreme Crafting Table** (the 9x9 table of Avaritia), with this pattern:

```
. . . . . . C . .
. . . . . C I C .
. . . . . N I I C
. . . . N I N C .
. . . N X N . . .
. C N I N . . . .
C I I N . . . . .
. C I C . . . . .
. . C . . . . . .
```

C: Crystal Matrix Ingot, N: Neutron Ingot, I: Infinity Ingot, X: Block of Infinity. A dot is an empty slot. The result is a wand with the Infinity rod and the Infinity cap, which refills every primal aspect on its own and whose spells are free.

The Infinity Cap can also be made alone: it is infused on an altar on top of a Neutronium Cap, with four infinity ingots and two infinity plates.

## Step by step

1. Make the ingredients first: Crystal Matrix, Neutron and Infinity ingots and a Block of Infinity come from Avaritia.
2. Place the 9x9 pattern above in an Extreme Crafting Table and take the wand.
3. Hold the wand and use it like any other Thaumaturge wand: it never runs dry, so you can cast without charging it.
4. Optional: to swap only the cap on another wand, infuse an Infinity Cap (altar on top of a Neutronium Cap, four infinity ingots and two infinity plates).
