    **Requires:** Thaumaturge
</Callout>

Each Rift Metal has an **inert** and a **finished** wand rod and cap. The inert parts are crafted at the arcane workbench; the finished ones are infused from the inert ones.

- Inert Cap: nuggets of the metal in a ring pattern (three above, two at the sides). Inert Rod: three ingots on a diagonal.
- Finished Cap: infused from the inert cap with four Salis Mundus and the aspects of the metal (Rifting: vacuos, alienis, praecantatio; Fibric: fabrico, vinculum, praecantatio; Salis: potentia, auram, ordo, praecantatio; Crimson: ignis, perditio, praecantatio).
- Finished Rod: infused from the inert rod with two Salis Mundus and two ingots of the metal.

## Step by step

1. Craft the inert cap and the inert rod at an arcane workbench.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Use the inert part as the starting item with the Salis Mundus and ingots above.
3. Assemble a wand from any rod and any cap.
4. Salis and Rifting rods glow; Salis has the largest vis capacity of the four.
