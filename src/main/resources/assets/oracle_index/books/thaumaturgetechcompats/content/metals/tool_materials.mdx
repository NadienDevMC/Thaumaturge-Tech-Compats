<Callout variant="info">
    **Requires:** Hephaestus Tools (and Hephaestus Forge to cast the parts)
</Callout>

With Hephaestus Tools installed, **Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson** are tool materials: head, handle and binding stats, each with the ingot of the metal as its ingredient. Salis is the best (highest durability, speed, damage and enchantability), then Fibric, Crimson and Rifting.

With Hephaestus Forge (smeltery) you can also melt the parts and cast them from the molten metal (adze head, broad axe head, hammer head, large blade, large plate, pick head, small axe head, small blade, tool binding, tool handle, tough binding, tough handle).

The mod also adds four **upgrades** applied in the Arsenal Table, each consuming an item:

- **Unbreakable** (costs a Thaumium Ingot): a chance to avoid durability loss that grows with each level.
- **Vis Repair** (costs a Brass Ingot): the tool repairs itself in your inventory using vis from the chunk aura, or slowly without it.
- **Void Teleport** (costs a Void Metal Ingot): blocks mined and mob drops go straight into your inventory.
- **Ender Teleport** (costs a Fabric item): use the tool to teleport where you look (range and cooldown scale with level); hits can teleport you behind the target.

At **level 12** every one of them makes the tool unbreakable.

## Step by step

1. Make ingots of the metal (see Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson).
2. In the Hephaestus Tools part builder (or by casting with Hephaestus Forge) make parts with the metal as material and assemble a tool as usual.
3. Open the Arsenal Table with the tool and apply an upgrade by giving it the item it consumes; repeat to raise its level up to 12.
4. Level 12 makes the tool unbreakable whichever upgrade got you there.
