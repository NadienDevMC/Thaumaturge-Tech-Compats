## What it is

A Brass Grinding Ball for the SAG Mill of Ender IO. It gives more output and more bonus output than the ball before it, uses less power and lasts longer. The tiers go Brass, Thaumium, Void Metal.

## How to get it

It is crafted from ingots of brass. Put it in the grinding ball slot of the mill.

## Step by step

1. Craft the ball from ingots of the metal (Brass, Thaumium or Void Metal).
2. Open a SAG Mill of Ender IO and put the ball in its grinding ball slot.
3. Mill as usual: each tier gives more output and more bonus output than the one before, uses less power and lasts longer.
