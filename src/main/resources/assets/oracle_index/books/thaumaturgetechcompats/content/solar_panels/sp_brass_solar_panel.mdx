## What it is

The brass plating channels the light into the panel without losing it.

**Made from (same family):** [Alchemical Solar Panel](@solarflux:sp_alchemical)

**Used to make (same family):** [Thaumium Solar Panel](@solarflux:sp_thaumium)

## How to get it

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from photovoltaic cells, brass plates and an alchemical solar panel.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Brass: at the arcane workbench craft photovoltaic cells, brass plates and an alchemical panel. Thaumium and Void Metal: infuse over the previous panel with plates, advanced or finest photovoltaic cells and mechanisms.
3. Variants: Brass Andesite (needs Create), Thaumium Zinc (needs Create or All The Ores), Alchemical Cobalt (needs Hephaestus Forge) and Void Uranium (needs All The Ores) are crafted or infused as listed above.
4. Place the panel in the open sun like any Solar Flux panel and connect it to your energy network.

## Tips and common mistakes

- The Void Uranium panel irradiates whoever carries it when Mekanism is installed.
