## What it is

A Thaumium Range Addon for the machines of Industrial Foregoing: it widens the working range of the machine, and is stronger than the diamond original and than the tier before it.

**Made from (same family):** [Brass Range Addon](@thaumaturgetechcompats:brass_range_addon)

**Used to make (same family):** [Void Metal Range Addon](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_range_addon)

## How to get it

The thaumium has taken to the chip like a second circuit.

Each one is made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from the brass addon of the same kind, three thaumium plates, a simple mechanism, the material of the addon, a diamond and a larger measure of Liquid Death.

### Dissolution Chamber

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Brass Range Addon | 1 |
| Thaumium Plate | 3 |
| Simple Arcane Mechanism | 1 |
| Ender Pearl | 2 |
| Diamond | 1 |
| Liquid Death | 2000 mB |

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Addons: craft each in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber (Brass, Thaumium, Void Metal tiers) and put it in the addon slots of a machine, or use it on the machine directly.
