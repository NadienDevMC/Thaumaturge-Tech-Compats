## What it is

The Magical Infinity Drill mines a whole area at once and drops everything at your feet, like the Infinity Drill of Industrial Foregoing, but it holds vis instead of power. A Recharge Pedestal fills it, and while its charging is on it also draws vis slowly from the aura around you. The more vis it holds, the wider the area, and it falls back to a smaller level as it empties. Its tank takes Liquid Death, which is spent instead of vis.

## How to get it

The Magical Infinity Drill is born on an infusion altar from an Infinity Drill. It mines a whole area at once and drops everything at my feet, just like the original, but it holds vis instead of power. A Recharge Pedestal fills it, and while its charging is on it also draws vis slowly from the aura around me.

The more vis it holds, the wider the area: nothing at first, then 3x3, 5x5, 7x7, 9x9, 11x11 and, when it is almost full, the Artifact level, a 13x13x13 cube. Every mined block costs 1 vis and the drill falls back to a smaller level as it empties. Its tank takes Liquid Death, which is spent instead of vis without lowering the level. Right click on a source of Liquid Death fills the tank.

Shift and right click steps the area down one level, wrapping back to the largest one. Right click on air turns the charging on or off. The vis cost, the levels, the speed, the tank and the rest can be changed in its configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Magical Infinity Drill: fill it with a Recharge Pedestal (and Liquid Death in its tank). Right click on air toggles charging; Shift and right click steps the mining area down. The more vis it holds the wider the area, up to a 13x13x13 cube.
