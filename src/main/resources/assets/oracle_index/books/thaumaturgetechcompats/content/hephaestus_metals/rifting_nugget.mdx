## What it is

The Rifting Nugget is the small form of the metal: nine of them make an ingot. The inert wand cap of the metal is made from nuggets.

## How to use it

1. Craft an ingot at a crafting table to get nine nuggets, or cast them from the molten metal with a nugget cast.
2. Use five of them at the Arcane Workbench for the inert wand cap of the metal.
3. Nine nuggets turn back into an ingot.
