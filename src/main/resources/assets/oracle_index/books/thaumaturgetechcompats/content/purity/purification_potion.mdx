## What it is

The Purification Splash Potion is thrown at the ground and shatters into a clean mist: every infected creature standing in it returns to its natural self (pets, livestock and monsters alike), and the flux sickness is driven out of the blood.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Radius | 4 blocks |
| Cure chance | 100% |
| Health restored | 4 |

## How to get it

### Crucible

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | Crucible with water, over heat |
| Item to drop in (catalyst) | Ethereal Bloom |
| Essentia in the crucible | Ordo 40, Victus 40, Aqua 30, Lux 30 |
| Research | **Purification Splash Potion** (Purity tab) |
| Makes | 1 |

## How to use it

1. Research it in the Purity tab (observe a taint creature first).
2. Brew it in a crucible with an Ethereal Bloom as the catalyst.
3. Throw it at the ground next to the creatures you want to cure.
