## What it is

The Neutronium Cap, a wand cap that cuts the cost of every spell far more than ordinary caps. Any rod fits any cap.

## How to get it

### Crystal Matrix Wand

The Crystal Matrix Rod is crafted from three crystal matrix ingots at an arcane workbench. The Crystal Matrix Cap needs three ingots and two crystal matrix plates, the same plates used to build golems.

Assembled into a wand, they hold 1000 vis and its spells cost nothing.

### Neutronium Wand

Both parts are infused with two neutron ingots and two neutronium plates. The Neutronium Cap uses a Crystal Matrix Cap as its catalyst, and the Neutronium Rod uses a Crystal Matrix Rod.

Assembled into a wand, they hold 5000 vis and cut the cost of every spell by half.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Crystal Matrix: at an arcane workbench craft the Rod from three crystal matrix ingots and the Cap from three ingots plus two crystal matrix plates.
3. Neutronium: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Use a Crystal Matrix Rod (or Cap) as catalyst with two neutron ingots and two neutronium plates.
4. Assemble a wand from any rod and any cap: any rod fits any cap. Crystal Matrix gives 1000 vis and free spells; Neutronium holds 5000 vis and halves the cost of every spell.
5. For the top tier see the Infinity Wand page.
