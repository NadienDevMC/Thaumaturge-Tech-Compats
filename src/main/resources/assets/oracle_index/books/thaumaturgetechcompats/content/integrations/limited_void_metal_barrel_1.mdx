## What it is

Higher tiers of backpacks, barrels, chests, shulker boxes and stack upgrades, plus an Arcane Workbench upgrade, for Sophisticated Backpacks and Sophisticated Storage.

**Made from (same family):** [Limited Alchemical Brass Barrel I](@thaumaturgetechcompats:limited_alchemical_brass_barrel_1)

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Backpacks and storage (barrels, limited barrels, chests, shulker boxes): craft each from the one below it surrounded by eight plates of the new metal (Thaumium from Netherite, Alchemical Brass from Thaumium, Void Metal from Alchemical Brass). They keep contents and upgrades.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Slot counts, upgrade slots and multipliers are set in the configuration.
