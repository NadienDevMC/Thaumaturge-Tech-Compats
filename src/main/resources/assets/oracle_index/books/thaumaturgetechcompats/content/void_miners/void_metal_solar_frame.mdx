## What it is

A Void Metal Solar Panel Frame, the block the structure of a solar panel of this tier is built from, together with glass panels and modifiers.

**Made from (same family):** [Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Frame](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_solar_frame)

## How to get it

The Void Metal Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller, void metal plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes, a void seed.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from void metal plates, obsidian and the matching Alchemical Brass frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Make the frames from plates of the metal, obsidian and a base frame (or the previous frame). Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The controller starts from a Rubetine Solar Panel (Thaumium) or the previous controller, with plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes.
3. Build the structure from frames, glass panels and any solar modifier.
4. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source.
5. It generates energy only while it can pay units to the source, with no need for sun or open sky. Upgrades go in free slots and multiply with the others.
