## What it is

The Salis Ingot is one of the four extra metals of this mod. It is a Hephaestus tool material (best to worst: Salis, Fibric, Crimson, Rifting), the base of a wand rod and cap, an ingredient of the Avaritia singularity of its kind, and it melts in the Hephaestus Forge smeltery.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Smeltery heat needed | 2000 |
| Tool head durability | 75,900 |
| Mining speed | 11 |
| Attack damage as a tool material | 5 |
| Enchantability | 25 |
| Binding durability | 550 |

## How to use it

1. Research Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson.
2. Make the ingot (see the recipe above). The Rifting Ingot needs a Rift Jar **holding a trapped rift** next to the crucible, or nothing happens.
3. Nine nuggets make an ingot and nine ingots a block, and back again, at a crafting table.
4. Melt it in the Hephaestus smeltery (heat in the table above) and cast tool parts, or craft its inert wand rod and cap.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson
> Rifting Ingot: drop a Void Metal Ingot into a crucible with water and 20 praecantatio and 20 sol, with a Rift Jar next to the crucible that holds a trapped rift. Without the trapped rift nothing happens.
> Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson each have an ingot, a nugget, a block and a native cluster, and they can be melted in the Hephaestus smeltery and made into Hephaestus tools, from best to worst: Salis, Fibric, Crimson and Rifting.
> Each metal also makes a wand rod and a wand cap. First shape the inert part at an arcane workbench: the rod from three ingots and the cap from five nuggets. Then wake it with an infusion, with the inert part as catalyst, four salis mundus and the aspects of the metal. The stronger the metal, the more vis the rod holds and the cheaper every spell is with the cap.
