## What it is

The Void Metal Transmission Link moves Dimensional Flux between devices that never touch. Two links tuned to each other form a visible beam; flux flows both ways until both are equally full, and each link also takes flux from the storages next to it and gives it to those that can receive it. Links side by side pass flux along, so chains are possible.

## How to use it

1. Make two links of the same tier (the Thaumium one at the Arcane Workbench, two per craft).
2. Use the Transmission Linker on the first link, then on the second: they pair.
3. Keep the pair within range (the shorter range of the two) and both ends loaded.
4. Right click a link to read its flux, partner, distance, rate and status.
5. To break a pair, sneak and use the linker on a link.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Void Metal Transmission Link
> The Void Metal Transmission Link is made on an infusion altar from an Alchemical Brass Transmission Link, void metal plates, a complex arcane mechanism and a void seed.
> It has the greatest reach and the highest rate of all the links. It can be paired with a link of another tier, but the pair only reaches as far and moves only as much as the weaker of the two.
