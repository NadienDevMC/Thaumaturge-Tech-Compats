## What it is

The Extremely Primordial Pearl, several primordial pearls folded into one on an infusion altar with the aspect of the end of things. It is not spent when it is used as a catalyst, so it serves as an everlasting one.

## How to get it

The altar has done it. The Extremely Primordial Pearl hums with a power that is more primordial than any pearl has a right to be.

It does not seem to be spent when I work with it, so it will serve as an everlasting catalyst.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it.
3. Put several Primordial Pearls on the pedestals and provide the Terminus aspect the recipe asks for.
4. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
5. Keep the pearl: it is not spent when used as a catalyst, so one pearl serves for every infusion that asks for it.

## Tips and common mistakes

- It is required by the Infinity tier of nearly every Avaritia upgrade of this mod (jars, matrices, stones, scribing tools, stabilizers and more).
