## What it is

An energized Thaumium pickaxe that runs on Mekanism energy: it loses no durability, and every block mined, every hit and every stripped, flattened or tilled block spends some of its charge. It is charged in an energy cube, a charge pad, any Mekanism machine charging slot or any other energy source. With no energy left it does nothing a tool should do until it is charged again.

## How to get it

### Energized Thaumium Tools

The plan is simple enough. A normal thaumium tool, two energy tablets, a reinforced alloy, an advanced control circuit and two thaumium plates, all together on an infusion altar.

#### Energized Thaumium Tools

The infusion altar gives me an energized thaumium pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe or sword from the matching normal tool. They no longer lose durability. They hold energy, and every block mined, every hit on a creature and every stripped, flattened or tilled block spends some of it. They are charged in an energy cube, a charge pad, a charging slot of any Mekanism machine or with any other source of energy, and the charge bar under the item shows what is left. With no energy left they mine like bare hands, drop nothing a tool should drop and hit like a fist, until I charge them again.

They are the first of the energized tools: they hold the least energy, spend the most of it and work the slowest, and the alchemical brass ones improve on them. The capacity, the cost of every action, the mining speed and the damage can be changed in the configuration.

### Energized Alchemical Brass Tools

The plan is simple enough. A normal iron tool, the energized thaumium tool of the same kind, two energy tablets, an infused alloy, a basic control circuit and two alchemical brass plates, all together on an infusion altar.

#### Energized Alchemical Brass Tools

The infusion altar gives me an energized alchemical brass pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe or sword from the matching iron tool, and the energized thaumium tool is consumed in the process. They work like the thaumium ones: they lose no durability, they hold energy and every block mined, every hit and every stripped, flattened or tilled block spends some of it. With no energy left they are as useless as bare hands.

They hold more energy, spend less of it and work faster than the thaumium ones. The capacity, the cost of every action, the mining speed and the damage can be changed in the configuration.

### Energized Void Metal Tools

A normal void metal tool, the energized alchemical brass tool of the same kind, two energy tablets, an atomic alloy, an ultimate control circuit and two void metal plates, on the infusion altar.

#### Energized Void Metal Tools

These tools come from the normal void metal ones, and the energized alchemical brass tool is consumed in the process. They work like the brass ones, with a far larger store of energy, a lower cost for every action, a faster mining speed and a stronger blow. They also lose no durability and they too are useless once the energy is gone. They are made in the infusion altar one by one: pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and sword.

The capacity, the cost of every action, the mining speed and the damage can be changed in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: the normal thaumium tool (Thaumium); a normal iron tool plus the energized thaumium tool (Alchemical Brass); the normal void metal tool plus the energized alchemical brass tool (Void Metal). Add two energy tablets, the alloy and circuit listed above and two plates of the metal.
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item. The previous energized tool is consumed.
4. Charge the tool in an energy cube, a charge pad, a charging slot of any Mekanism machine or any other energy source; the bar under the item shows what is left.
5. Use it: it loses no durability, but every block mined, every hit and every stripped, flattened or tilled block spends energy. With no energy it does nothing a tool should do until charged again.
