## What it is

A Brass Speed Addon for the machines of Oritech: it makes the machine process in a smaller fraction of the time than Oritech's own addon, at a slightly different energy cost. Each tier does it better than the one before.

## How to get it

### Brass Machine Addons

Each addon is built at the arcane workbench around the Oritech addon of the same kind, with brass plates, simple mechanisms and a component of Oritech:

- Speed: processes in 40% of the time (Oritech's addon: 50%) and spends 110% of the energy.
- Efficiency: spends 60% of the energy (Oritech's addon: 80%).
- Capacitor: adds 10,000,000 of capacity and 10,000 of insert rate to the machine.

They are placed against the machine like any other addon. Every value can be changed in the configuration.

### Thaumium Machine Addons

Each addon is built around the brass addon of the same kind, with thaumium plates, complex mechanisms and an overcharged crystal:

- Speed: processes in 20% of the time and spends 100% of the energy.
- Efficiency: spends 40% of the energy.
- Capacitor: adds 100,000,000 of capacity and 100,000 of insert rate.

### Void Metal Machine Addons

Each addon is built around the thaumium addon of the same kind, with void metal plates, complex mechanisms and a Heisenberg compensator:

- Speed: processes in 10% of the time and spends 90% of the energy.
- Efficiency: spends 20% of the energy.
- Capacitor: adds 1,000,000,000 of capacity and 1,000,000 of insert rate.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Build each addon around the Oritech addon of the same kind (Brass), then around the Brass one (Thaumium) and around the Thaumium one (Void Metal), with the plates, mechanisms and component the list above shows.
3. Place the addon against the machine like any other Oritech addon.
4. Speed addons cut processing time (40%, 20% and 10% of the base); Efficiency addons cut energy use (60%, 40% and 20%); Capacitor addons add capacity and insert rate (10 million, 100 million and 1 billion capacity).
