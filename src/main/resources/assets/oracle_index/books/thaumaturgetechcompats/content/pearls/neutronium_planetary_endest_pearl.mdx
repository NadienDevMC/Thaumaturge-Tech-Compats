## What it is

Thrown pearls that summon planets: they orbit whatever the pearl hits and hurt it again and again. 13 tiers from Copper to Infinity, plus a Creative one.

**Made from (same family):** [Crystal Matrix Planetary Endest Pearl](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_planetary_endest_pearl)

**Used to make (same family):** [Infinity Planetary Endest Pearl](@thaumaturgetechcompats:infinity_planetary_endest_pearl)

## How to get it

The Planetary Endest Pearls are made on an infusion altar. The Copper one needs an Avaritia Endest Pearl and four copper ingots, and every following pearl needs the one before it and four units of its own material:

- Copper, Iron, Gold, Diamond, Emerald and Netherite: copper ingots, iron ingots, gold ingots, diamonds, emeralds and netherite ingots.

They are thrown with right click like an ender pearl. When one hits a creature, planets named after those of the sky, Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars and so on, orbit it on different orbits, each with its own speed and slant, and hurt it again and again until they fade. When one hits a block, the planets orbit the closest creature within reach, and if there is none they orbit the point of impact and hurt whatever enters their reach. They never hurt the thrower, or their team and tamed animals, unless the configuration says so. Bosses take only a limited amount of damage from each planet.

The Netherite pearl is the first that kills bosses: its planets ignore armor, resistance and the shield of what they hit, and it is unbreakable, so it never wears out when thrown, only held back by its cooldown. The lower pearls have durability: every throw wears one point and the pearl breaks with the last one. Copper has 3, Iron 5, Gold 8, Diamond 12 and Emerald 16 (set in the configuration).

Every pearl's planets, damage, interval, duration, orbit and cooldown are set in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Start with an Avaritia Endest Pearl and four copper ingots for the Copper pearl; every next pearl needs the one before it plus four units of its material (the list above gives them, and the higher tiers also need Salis Mundus variants).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item. The higher the pearl, the more unstable the infusion; the upper tiers use the Cosmic, Nuclear and Sol essences.
4. Throw it with right click like an ender pearl, aiming at a creature.
5. Watch the planets orbit the target and hurt it again and again until they fade. Against a block they orbit the nearest creature, or the impact point.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Planets never hurt the thrower, their team or tamed animals (unless the configuration says so).
- Netherite, Void Metal, Infinity and Creative pearls are unbreakable and kill bosses; lower ones have limited durability (Copper 3, Iron 5, Gold 8, Diamond 12, Emerald 16, Thaumium 32, Brass 64, Blaze Cube 128, Crystal Matrix 256, Neutronium 512).
