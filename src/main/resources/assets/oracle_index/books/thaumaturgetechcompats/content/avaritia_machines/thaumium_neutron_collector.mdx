## What it is

The Thaumium Neutron Collector works exactly like the Avaritia machine and uses the same recipes, only faster, and it gives more every time it finishes. Each tier is quicker and more generous than the one before.

**Used to make (same family):** [Brass Neutron Collector](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_neutron_collector)

## How to get it

Every machine is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it:

- The Thaumium Neutron Collector and Compressor need the Densest Neutronium Collector or Compressor, four thaumium plates and a Simple Arcane Mechanism.

- The Brass Neutron Collector and Compressor need the Thaumium machine, four alchemical brass plates and a Simple Arcane Mechanism.

- The Void Neutron Collector and Compressor need the Brass machine, four void metal plates and a Void Seed.

They work exactly like the machines of Avaritia and use the same recipes, only faster, and each tier also gives more every time it finishes. How much faster and how much more is set in the configuration.

Each tier can only be infused on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of its own tier, and that matrix brings its own instability to the craft:

- The Thaumium machines need the Crystal Matrix one: solar instability, with fires, lightning and burns.

- The Brass machines need the Neutronium one: nuclear instability, with radiation, flux poured into the aura and a meltdown that explodes.

- The Void machines need the Infinity one: cosmic instability, with flux torn open, warp, creatures from beyond and everything nearby thrown elsewhere.

The Brass and Void machines also ask for two new essences. Nuclear, made of potentia and perditio, is the power locked in the heart of matter. Cosmic, made of alienis and auram, is the vastness between the stars. The Brass machines ask for Nuclear, and the Void machines ask for Nuclear and Cosmic.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build the right altar for the tier: Thaumium machines need a Crystal Matrix Runic Matrix at the centre of the altar, Brass machines a Neutronium one and Void machines an Infinity one.
3. Place the previous machine (the Densest Neutronium Collector or Compressor for Thaumium, then each tier on the one before) on the central pedestal and the plates and mechanism (or Void Seed) from the list above on the pedestals.
4. Provide the essentia, including Nuclear (Brass) and Nuclear plus Cosmic (Void), and start the infusion. Expect the instability of the matrix: solar fires, nuclear radiation or cosmic warp.
5. Place the finished machine like the Avaritia one. A Collector produces neutron pellets, a Compressor makes singularities from the same recipes, only faster and giving more each cycle.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Each tier needs the previous tier's machine: you cannot skip a tier.
- Do not build the altar near things you care about before the Brass and Void tiers: nuclear and cosmic instability can explode, irradiate and teleport.
