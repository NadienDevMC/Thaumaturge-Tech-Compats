The mod registers Avaritia singularities for seven metals: **Thaumium, Alchemical Brass, Void Metal, Salis, Fibric, Crimson and Rifting**. They are Avaritia singularity definitions (one per metal, each tied to the ingot of that metal), so they appear in the Avaritia singularity system like the original ones.

Use them as you use any Avaritia singularity: they are the dense form of the metal that Avaritia machines and recipes consume.

## Step by step

1. Make sure the ingot of the metal exists (see the Rift Metals chapter for Salis, Fibric, Crimson and Rifting; Thaumium, Alchemical Brass and Void Metal come from Thaumaturge).
2. Open the Avaritia singularity recipes in JEI to see how many ingots each singularity needs.
3. Compress the ingots as Avaritia does for its own singularities (the Neutronium Compressor).
