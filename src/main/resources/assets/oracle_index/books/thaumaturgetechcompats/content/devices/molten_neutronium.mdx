## What it is

This feature adds **molten Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity** to the Hephaestus Forge smeltery, plus the molten forms of the four Rift Metals (Salis, Fibric, Crimson, Rifting). Each has melting and casting recipes: ingots, blocks and nuggets can be melted, and ingots, blocks, nuggets and plates can be cast again. Their temperatures are 1100, 1500 and 2000 for the three Avaritia metals.

Buckets of each molten metal exist as items.

## How to get it

### Smeltery (melting)

| Input | Result |
|---|---|
| Neutronium Block (or equivalent) | Molten Neutronium 810 mB |
| Neutronium Ingot (or equivalent) | Molten Neutronium 90 mB |
| Neutronium Nugget (or equivalent) | Molten Neutronium 10 mB |
| Neutronium Plate | Molten Neutronium 90 mB |

## Step by step

1. Build a Hephaestus Forge smeltery as usual and fuel it.
2. Put an ingot, block or nugget of the metal in the smeltery: it melts into the matching molten fluid (the smeltery must reach the temperature of that metal).
3. Pour the molten metal on a casting table with an ingot cast (or a nugget or plate cast) or into a casting basin for a block.
4. For tools, the Rift Metals also have Hephaestus Tools materials and part-casting recipes (see the Rift Metals chapter).
