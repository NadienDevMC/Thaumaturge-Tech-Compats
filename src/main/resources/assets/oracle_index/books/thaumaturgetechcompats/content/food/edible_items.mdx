Edible copies of tools, weapons, ingots, gems, doors and the Goggles of Revealing. Each is the original item crafted together with sugar, and eating it gives a status effect depending on the kind of item and its tier: haste for pickaxes, health boost and regeneration for weapons, speed for axes, luck for hoes, jump boost for shovels, night vision for goggles and gems, absorption for ingots and resistance for doors.

## Step by step

1. Take the original item (a diamond pickaxe, an iron ingot, an oak door...) and put it in a crafting grid with sugar.
2. Take the edible copy. It no longer works as the tool: it is food.
3. Eat it to get the effect for its class and tier.
