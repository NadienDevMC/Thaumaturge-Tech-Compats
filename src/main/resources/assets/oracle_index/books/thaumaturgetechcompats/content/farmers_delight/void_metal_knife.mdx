> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Farmer's Delight** mod.

## What it is

The Void Metal Knife is a Farmer's Delight knife in every way: it cuts on the Cutting Board and counts as a knife for any recipe that asks for one. It is sturdier, sharper and easier to enchant than the iron knife.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Durability | 1,300 |
| Attack damage (base 0.5 plus) | +4.0 |
| Attack speed | -1.7 |
| Mining speed | 9.0 |
| Enchantability | 24 |
| Flux Taint on hit | 35% chance, 5 seconds |

## How to use it

1. Research the knife of its tier and craft it at the Arcane Workbench (a plate over the knife of the tier before; a plate over a stick for Thaumium).
2. Use it on the Cutting Board of Farmer's Delight like any knife.
3. Enchant it: its high enchantability makes good enchantments easier.
4. The Void Metal Knife is also the base of the Flux Knife.
