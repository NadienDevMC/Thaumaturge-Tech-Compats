## What it is

A Brass Processing Addon for the machines of Industrial Foregoing: it multiplies what the machine processes at once, and is stronger than the diamond original and than the tier before it.

**Used to make (same family):** [Thaumium Processing Addon](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_processing_addon)

## How to get it

The chip has set. Speed, efficiency, processing and range: a brass addon for each, and every one of them stronger than the diamond originals.

They are made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from three brass plates, redstone, a glass pane, the material of each addon and Liquid Death. Place them in the addon slots of a machine, or use them on the machine directly. The values can be changed in the configuration.

### Dissolution Chamber

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Alchemical Brass Plate | 3 |
| Redstone Dust | 2 |
| Glass Pane | 1 |
| Furnace | 1 |
| Crafting Table | 1 |
| Liquid Death | 1000 mB |

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Addons: craft each in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber (Brass, Thaumium, Void Metal tiers) and put it in the addon slots of a machine, or use it on the machine directly.
