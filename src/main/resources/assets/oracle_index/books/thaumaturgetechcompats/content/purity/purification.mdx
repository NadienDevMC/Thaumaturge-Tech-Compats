## What it is

The Purification Splash Potion cures infected creatures in the area where it breaks: pets, livestock and monsters alike return to their natural selves, and it also drives the flux sickness out of the blood.

## How to get it

### Purification Splash Potion

The brew works. Thrown at the ground, the potion shatters into a bright, clean mist, and every infected creature standing in it returns to its natural self, health, temper and all.

It cures pets, livestock and monsters alike, and it also drives the flux sickness out of the blood before it can finish a creature off.

#### Purification Splash Potion

It is brewed in the crucible from Ordo, Victus, Aqua and Lux, with an Ethereal Bloom as the catalyst.

### Biome Purification Potion

The brew is ready. Thrown at the ground, it bursts into a pale green mist that does in one breath the work of an ethereal bloom: the tainted biome returns to what the land was, fibres, crust, soil and growths wither away, flux goo and gas wash out, and any taint seed caught in the mist is destroyed.

It also draws flux out of the aura of the chunks it touches.

#### Biome Purification Potion

It is brewed in the crucible from Ordo, Victus, Aqua, Lux, Auram, Praecantatio, Vitreus and Herba, with a Purification Splash Potion as the catalyst. The area, the flux it draws and what it cleans are set in the configuration.

### Clarity Potion

The draught is clear as spring water and tastes of nothing. Drunk, it takes the temporary warp out of the mind at once.

It does not touch the warp that has settled for good, and the drinker may feel a brief unease afterwards.

#### Clarity Potion

It is brewed in the crucible from Cognitio, Ordo, Spiritus, Aqua, Victus and Alienis, with a Sanity Soap as the catalyst. How much it removes and its side effect are set in the configuration.

### Absolution Potion

It works, though only a little at a time. Each draught pulls a measure of permanent warp out of the drinker and leaves them weakened for a long while.

It will take many to be free, and each costs more than the finest brew I have made.

#### Absolution Potion

It is brewed in the crucible from Cognitio, Ordo, Spiritus, Alienis, Praecantatio, Vacuos, Tenebrae, Victus, Auram and Perditio, with an Eldritch Eye as the catalyst. How much it removes per draught and its side effect are set in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked. (the Purity tab collects them).
2. Fill a crucible with water, heat it, and add the aspects of the recipe, then throw the catalyst item into it. Purification Splash Potion: Ordo, Victus, Aqua and Lux with an Ethereal Bloom. Biome Purification: Ordo, Victus, Aqua, Lux, Auram, Praecantatio, Vitreus and Herba with a Purification Splash Potion. Clarity: Cognitio, Ordo, Spiritus, Aqua, Victus and Alienis with a Sanity Soap. Absolution: ten aspects (listed above) with an Eldritch Eye.
3. Throw the splash potions at the ground: the Purification potion cures every infected creature in the mist (pets, livestock and monsters), the Biome one restores a tainted biome and destroys taint seeds in the mist and draws flux out of the aura.
4. Drink Clarity to remove temporary warp at once (it does not touch permanent warp). Drink Absolution (needs Clarity first) to remove a measure of permanent warp; it weakens you for a long while, so repeat it patiently.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Clarity and Absolution leave a brief side effect. Amounts removed are set in the configuration.
