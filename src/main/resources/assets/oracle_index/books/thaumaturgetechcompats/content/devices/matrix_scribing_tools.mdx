## What it is

Scribing Tools of Crystal Matrix: a quill and an inkwell that hold far more ink than ordinary ones and work on a research table in the same way. They can be refilled with black dye.

**Used to make (same family):** [Neutronium Scribing Tools](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_scribing_tools)

## How to get it

Each set is made on an infusion altar, starting from ordinary scribing tools:

- The Crystal Matrix tools are surrounded by crystal matrix ingots.

- The Neutronium tools need the Crystal Matrix tools and neutron ingots.

- The Infinity tools need the Neutronium tools, infinity ingots and an Extremely Primordial Pearl.

All of them work on a research table exactly like ordinary scribing tools. The Infinity tools restore their ink on their own while I carry them. The Crystal Matrix and Neutronium tools can be refilled with black dye, like the ordinary ones.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is ordinary scribing tools (or the previous tier).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Use them on a research table as ordinary scribing tools. Crystal Matrix and Neutronium tools can be refilled with black dye; the Infinity ones restore their ink on their own while you carry them.
