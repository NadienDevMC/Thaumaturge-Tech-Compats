<Callout variant="info">
    **Requires:** Mekanism
</Callout>

The mod adds a **Native Osmium Cluster** and a **Native Uranium Cluster**, raw chunks in the style of the Thaumaturge ore clusters. They are smelted for the metal or dissolved in the crucible. Mekanism ore-processing routes that this mod adds produce them (Salis Mundus infusing in the Metallurgic Infuser, and a x5 route with dissolution, washing and crystallization; x2, x3 and x4 routes exist but are off by default), and so do the ores of the Industrial Foregoing Laser Drill that this mod adds.

## Step by step

1. Put a Thaumaturge cluster or ore through one of the enabled Mekanism routes (Metallurgic Infuser with Salis Mundus gives 2 clusters; the x5 route gives 5).
2. Smelt the Native Osmium or Uranium Cluster in a furnace to get the metal, or dissolve it in a crucible.
3. Enable the x2, x3 or x4 routes in the configuration only if you accept sharing machine and input with Mekanism own ore recipes.
