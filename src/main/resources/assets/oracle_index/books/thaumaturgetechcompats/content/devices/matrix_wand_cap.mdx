## What it is

The Crystal Matrix Cap is a Crystal Matrix wand part of Thaumaturge. The rod sets how much vis a wand stores and the cap sets what its spells cost; any rod fits any cap. The full Infinity wand (Infinity rod and cap) is built in Avaritia's Extreme Crafting Table and refills every primal aspect on its own.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Spell cost multiplier | 0 (spells are free) |

## How to use it

1. Research Crystal Matrix Wand, then Neutronium Wand, then Infinity Wand.
2. Make the part (see the recipe above); the Neutronium and Infinity parts are infused over the part of the tier before.
3. Assemble a rod and a cap into a wand as with any Thaumaturge wand.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Crystal Matrix Wand
> The Crystal Matrix Rod is crafted from three crystal matrix ingots at an arcane workbench. The Crystal Matrix Cap needs three ingots and two crystal matrix plates, the same plates used to build golems.
> Assembled into a wand, they hold 1000 vis and its spells cost nothing.
