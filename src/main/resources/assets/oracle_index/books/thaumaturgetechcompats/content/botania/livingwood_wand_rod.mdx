## What it is

The Livingwood Rod, a wand rod made of living wood from Botania. Any rod fits any cap.

**Used to make (same family):** [Dreamwood Rod](@thaumaturgetechcompats:dreamwood_wand_rod)

## How to get it

The rod and the cap are separate parts, and any rod fits any cap:

- The Livingwood Rod is crafted from three Livingwood Twigs at an arcane workbench. The Dreamwood Rod is infused from it with four Dreamwood Twigs.

- The Manasteel Cap is crafted from five Manasteel Ingots at an arcane workbench. The Elementium Cap and the Terrasteel Cap are infused from it, with Elementium Ingots and Pixie Dust or with Terrasteel Ingots and a Mana Diamond.

Dreamwood holds more vis than livingwood. The Manasteel and Terrasteel caps make every spell cheaper, and the Elementium Cap makes the primal aspects of air, water, earth and fire cheaper still. The exact values are set in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Craft the Livingwood Rod from three Livingwood Twigs and the Manasteel Cap from five Manasteel Ingots at an arcane workbench.
3. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Dreamwood Rod: infuse from the Livingwood Rod with four Dreamwood Twigs. Elementium Cap: from the Manasteel Cap with Elementium Ingots and Pixie Dust. Terrasteel Cap: from the Manasteel Cap with Terrasteel Ingots and a Mana Diamond.
4. Assemble a wand with any rod and any cap. Dreamwood holds more vis; Manasteel and Terrasteel make every spell cheaper; Elementium makes air, water, earth and fire cheaper still.
