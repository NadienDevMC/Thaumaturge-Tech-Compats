## What it is

The Molten Infinity Bucket holds the molten form of the metal for the Hephaestus Forge smeltery. It is the fluid you get by melting the metal, and the one you pour to cast it again.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Smeltery heat needed | 2000 |

## How to get it

It is not crafted: fill an empty bucket with the molten metal from the Hephaestus smeltery.

## How to use it

1. Research the metal (Smeltery Metals for Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity; Rifting, Fibric, Salis and Crimson for the others).
2. Build a Hephaestus smeltery hot enough (heat in the table above) and melt ingots, plates, nuggets or blocks of the metal into its tank.
3. Fill a bucket from the smeltery, or pour the fluid straight onto a casting table (ingot, plate or nugget cast) or a casting basin (block).
