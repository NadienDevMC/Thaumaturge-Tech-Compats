## What it is

An Alchemical Furnace of Neutronium. It has the structure of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace, but its heart is the matching Avaritia smeltery, so it waits less between items and gives back part of the heat, perditio and aqua it burns.

## How to get it

The structure is exactly that of the Advanced Alchemical Furnace, a three by three base with two layers, but the essentia smeltery at its centre is the matching Avaritia smeltery: Crystal Matrix, Neutronium or Infinity. Salis mundus sprinkled over it brings it to life, and since every smeltery needs the one before it, every furnace needs the one before it too.

- Neutronium: waits a quarter as long and gives back 50%.

If a part is broken the furnace turns back into its smeltery. They draw and release essentia exactly like the Advanced Alchemical Furnace.

### Formed in the world

It is not crafted: it is formed in the world. Build its structure and sprinkle Salis Mundus over it (see the steps below).

| Research | Avaritia Alchemical Furnaces |
|---|---|

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build the Avaritia smeltery of the same tier (see Avaritia Essentia Smelteries) and the structure of an Advanced Alchemical Furnace: a three by three base with two layers, using the essentia smeltery at the centre.
3. Sprinkle the Salis Mundus over the structure to bring it to life. Every furnace needs the one before it.
4. Feed it like an Advanced Alchemical Furnace: it waits half, a quarter or a tenth as long between items, and gives back 25%, 50% or 90% of the heat, perditio and aqua it burns.

## Tips and common mistakes

- If a part is broken, the furnace turns back into its smeltery.
