## What it is

The Absolution Potion is a drinkable potion that pulls **permanent** warp out of the drinker, one measure at a time, and leaves them weakened for a long while.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Removes | 1 point of permanent warp per drink |
| Side effect (default) | Weakness II for 1200 ticks |
| Rest between uses | 100 ticks |

## How to get it

### Crucible

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | Crucible with water, over heat |
| Item to drop in (catalyst) | Eldritch Eye |
| Essentia in the crucible | Cognitio 100, Ordo 100, Spiritus 80, Alienis 80, Praecantatio 80, Vacuos 60, Tenebrae 60, Victus 60, Auram 50, Perditio 40 |
| Research | **Absolution Potion** (Purity tab) |
| Makes | 1 |

## How to use it

1. Research it in the Purity tab (after the Clarity Potion and the Voidseer's Pearl).
2. Brew it in a crucible with an Eldritch Eye as the catalyst.
3. Drink as many as it takes: each one removes one point of permanent warp by default.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Absolution Potion
> It works, though only a little at a time. Each draught pulls a measure of permanent warp out of the drinker and leaves them weakened for a long while.
> It will take many to be free, and each costs more than the finest brew I have made.
> 
> **Absolution Potion**
>
