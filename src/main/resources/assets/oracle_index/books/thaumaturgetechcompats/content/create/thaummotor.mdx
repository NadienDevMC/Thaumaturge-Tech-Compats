## What it is

The Thaummotor is a Create motor with no wires: while a jarred node, a node, a Flux Jar or a flux rift stands within reach, it takes a little power from it every so often and turns at a fixed speed, providing stress to the whole kinetic network. The richer the source, the more stress it gives; with no source, or none left, it stops. A hungry node is never a valid source.

## How to get it

The Thaummotor is made on an arcane workbench from a brass casing, two shafts, two simple mechanisms and four thaumium plates.

While a jarred node, a node, a Flux Jar or a flux rift stands within reach, it takes a little power from it every so often and turns at a fixed speed, providing stress to the whole kinetic network. The richer the source, the more stress it provides, and with no source, or no power left in it, it stops. A hungry node is never a valid source. The goggles of Create tell which source it is using.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Use a brass casing, two shafts, two simple mechanisms and four thaumium plates.
3. Place the Thaummotor and attach shafts or cogwheels as with any Create motor.
4. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source.
5. It takes a little power from the source every so often and turns at a fixed speed, giving stress to the whole kinetic network. Look at it with the Create goggles to see which source it uses.

## Tips and common mistakes

- With no source, or none left, it stops. The richer the source, the more stress it gives.
