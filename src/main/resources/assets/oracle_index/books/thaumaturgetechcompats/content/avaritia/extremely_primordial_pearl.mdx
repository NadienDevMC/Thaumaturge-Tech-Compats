> [!NOTE]

## What it is

The Extremely Primordial Pearl is several primordial pearls folded into one. Used as the central item of an infusion it is **not spent**, so it is an everlasting catalyst; when a recipe lists it on a pedestal (the Infinity jars, stones and devices) it is consumed like any ingredient.

## How to use it

1. Research Primordial Pearl and then Extremely Primordial Pearl (Avaritia tab).
2. Build an altar with plenty of Aqua, Ignis, Terra, Aer, Ordo, Perditio, Alienis, Praecantatio and Terminus around it.
3. Put a Primordial Pearl in the center and seven more on the pedestals, then infuse.
4. Keep it for the Infinity tier: almost every Infinity upgrade asks for one.
