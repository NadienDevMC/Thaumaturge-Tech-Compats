## What it is

The Void Metal Shield Generator raises a bubble around itself while it holds Dimensional Flux. The bubble destroys the projectiles of hostile mobs, pushes hostile mobs out (never bosses) and takes the damage out of explosions inside it (blocks inside are not destroyed).

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Radius | 32 blocks |
| Dimensional Flux stored | 10,000,000 |
| DF per tick while the bubble is up | 160 |

## How to use it

1. Make it (the Thaumium generator at the Arcane Workbench, the others infused around the one before).
2. Supply it with DF (Storage Core, Transmission Link or Rift Extractor touching it).
3. Every tick of the bubble costs DF, and so does every projectile or explosion it absorbs. When the flux runs out it shuts down until it holds enough again.
4. Right click it to read its state.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Void Metal Shield Generator
> The Void Metal Shield Generator is infused from an alchemical brass one, with four void metal plates, a complex mechanism, two vis resonators and a mirrored glass.
> It holds the most flux and projects the widest bubble, at a price in flux per tick to match.
