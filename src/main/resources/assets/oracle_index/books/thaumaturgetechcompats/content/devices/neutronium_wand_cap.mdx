## What it is

The Neutronium Cap, a wand cap that cuts the cost of every spell far more than ordinary caps. Any rod fits any cap.

**Made from (same family):** [Crystal Matrix Cap](@thaumaturgetechcompats:matrix_wand_cap)

## How to get it

Both parts are infused with two neutron ingots and two neutronium plates. The Neutronium Cap uses a Crystal Matrix Cap as its catalyst, and the Neutronium Rod uses a Crystal Matrix Rod.

Assembled into a wand, they hold 5000 vis and cut the cost of every spell by half.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Neutronium: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Use a Crystal Matrix Rod (or Cap) as catalyst with two neutron ingots and two neutronium plates.
3. Assemble a wand from any rod and any cap: any rod fits any cap. Crystal Matrix gives 1000 vis and free spells; Neutronium holds 5000 vis and halves the cost of every spell.
4. For the top tier see the Infinity Wand page.
