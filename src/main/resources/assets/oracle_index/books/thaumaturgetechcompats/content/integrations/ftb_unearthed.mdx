## What it is

A Brass Brush for sand and gravel, used by hand or inside the Unearther of FTB Unearthed. It can unearth primal vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar.

## How to get it

### Brass Brush

The brass holds its edge and the bristles do not fray.

#### Brass Brush

It is made at the arcane workbench from a feather, a brass plate and a stick. Used on sand or gravel, by hand or inside the Unearther of FTB Unearthed, it can unearth vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar. The odds and the durability are the lowest of the three brushes.

### Thaumium Brush

The brush trembles faintly whenever there is something worth finding.

#### Thaumium Brush

It is made at the arcane workbench from a brass brush, three thaumium plates and a feather. It is sturdier and works faster than the brass brush, and it unearths vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar far more often.

### Void Metal Brush

The bristles are dark and weightless, and the sand parts for them.

#### Void Metal Brush

It is made at the arcane workbench from a thaumium brush, three void metal plates and a feather. It is the most durable and the fastest of the three, and it gives the best odds of vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar.

### Thaumium Unearther

The Thaumium Unearther is made at the arcane workbench from an Unearther core, four thaumium plates and two simple mechanisms. It is built with the same frame as the ordinary Unearther.

It has no use for food: while a node, a node jar, a flux jar or a flux rift stands near it, it draws power from it every few moments and works faster. Without power it works at the normal speed, unless the settings say otherwise. A hungry node is never a safe source.

### Alchemical Brass Unearther

The Alchemical Brass Unearther is made at the arcane workbench from a Thaumium Unearther core, four alchemical brass plates and two simple mechanisms.

It looks for power over a wider area and works much faster than the thaumium one, at the price of taking more power each time. A hungry node is never a safe source.

### Void Metal Unearther

The Void Metal Unearther is made at the arcane workbench from an Alchemical Brass Unearther core, four void metal plates and two complex mechanisms.

It is the fastest of the three and the one that reaches the farthest for power, and it is also the one that takes the most. A hungry node is never a safe source.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Brass Brush: a feather, a brass plate and a stick. Thaumium Brush: a brass brush, three thaumium plates and a feather. Void Metal Brush: a thaumium brush, three void metal plates and a feather.
3. Use a brush on sand or gravel, or put it in an Unearther: it can unearth vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar (the higher the brush the better the odds, speed and durability).
4. Magical Unearthers: make a Thaumium Unearther at the arcane workbench from an Unearther core, four thaumium plates and two simple mechanisms (then Alchemical Brass and Void Metal on the previous core).
5. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source. It works without food and faster than the normal Unearther; without power it works at normal speed unless the settings say otherwise.
