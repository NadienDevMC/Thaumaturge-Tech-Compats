## What it is

A Node Transducer of Crystal Matrix. Placed above a stabilized node and powered with redstone, it energizes the node like the Node Transducer, but it charges it many times faster and its charge fades more slowly, for the same single point of aspect per tick.

## How to get it

Each transducer is built around the previous one, with four plates and two ingots of its own material, a comparator and a nitor:

- Crystal Matrix: charges a node 2 times faster and loses charge half as fast.
- Neutronium: 4 times faster, with a charge that fades 4 times slower.
- Infinity: 10 times faster, with a charge that fades 10 times slower.

They are placed above a stabilized node and powered with redstone exactly like the Node Transducer, and they drain the same single point of aspect each tick, so the node is energized for a fraction of the aspects.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Use four plates and two ingots of the new material, a comparator, a nitor and the previous transducer.
3. Make sure a stabilizer holds a node.
4. Place the transducer directly above the stabilized node and power it with redstone.
5. It energizes the node 2, 4 or 10 times faster than the plain Node Transducer (Crystal Matrix, Neutronium, Infinity) and its charge fades 2, 4 or 10 times slower, for the same single aspect point per tick.
