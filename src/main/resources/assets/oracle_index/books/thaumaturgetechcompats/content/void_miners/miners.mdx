## What it is

The Thaumium Miner Controller is a Void Miners miner that takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by (a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar, a node or a flux rift) and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.

## How to get it

### Thaumium Miner

The Thaumium Miner Controller is made on an infusion altar from a Rubetine Miner, thaumium plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from thaumium plates, obsidian and a base frame. The structure is built out of these frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, and it takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Alchemical Brass Miner

The Alchemical Brass Miner Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Thaumium Miner Controller, alchemical brass plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from alchemical brass plates, obsidian and the matching Thaumium frame. The structure is built out of these frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, and it takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Void Metal Miner

The Void Metal Miner Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Alchemical Brass Miner Controller, void metal plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars, a void seed.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from void metal plates, obsidian and the matching Alchemical Brass frame. The structure is built out of these frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, and it takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Thaumium Miner Upgrades

Every Thaumium upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the matching real Void Miners upgrade, a Rubetine miner upgrade, two thaumium mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the miner pays to its power source.

### Alchemical Brass Miner Upgrades

Every Alchemical Brass upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the same upgrade of the Thaumium tier, two alchemical brass mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the miner pays to its power source.

### Void Metal Miner Upgrades

Every Void Metal upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the same upgrade of the Alchemical Brass tier, two void metal mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the miner pays to its power source.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Make the frames from plates of the metal, obsidian and a base frame (or the previous tier frame). Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The controller starts from a Rubetine Miner (Thaumium) or the previous controller, with plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.
3. Build the structure from frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, as in Void Miners.
4. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source.
5. The miner takes no energy: it works only while it can pay units to the source, and stops when it cannot. Add upgrades in free slots: their effects multiply with each other and with the Void Miners modifiers.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Chat messages and tooltips tell you the cost in aura units, the source in use and why the miner stopped (no source, exhausted source or a hungry node).
