## What it is

The Rift Jar, set beside a flux rift, takes the rift apart one point at a time and keeps that as power for any machine that uses magical power. It stops once the rift is at its smallest size. A Rift Jar holding a trapped rift is also what lets a crucible make the Rifting Ingot.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Power capacity | 200 |
| Capture radius | 4 blocks |
| Capture interval | 40 ticks |
| Power per point of rift size | 20 |

## How to use it

1. Research Rift Jar and craft it at the Arcane Workbench.
2. Find or open a flux rift (for example with a Flux Knife) and place the jar within 4 blocks of it.
3. Every 40 ticks the rift shrinks by one point and the jar gains 20 power.
4. Put a magical-power machine next to the jar, or stand the jar next to a crucible to make Rifting Ingots.
5. A broken Rift Jar spills what it holds back into the aura as flux.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Rift Jar
> The Rift Jar is made on an arcane workbench from a warded jar, two void seeds and two void metal plates.
> Set beside a flux rift, it takes the rift apart one point at a time and keeps that as power for any machine that uses magical power. It stops once the rift is down to its smallest size. A broken Rift Jar spills what it still holds back into the aura as flux.
