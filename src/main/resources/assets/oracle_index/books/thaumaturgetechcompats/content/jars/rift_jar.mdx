## What it is

The Rift Jar, set beside a flux rift, takes the rift apart one point at a time and keeps that as power for any machine that uses magical power. It stops once the rift is down to its smallest size.

## How to get it

The Rift Jar is made on an arcane workbench from a warded jar, two void seeds and two void metal plates.

Set beside a flux rift, it takes the rift apart one point at a time and keeps that as power for any machine that uses magical power. It stops once the rift is down to its smallest size. A broken Rift Jar spills what it still holds back into the aura as flux.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Use a Warded Jar, two void seeds and two void metal plates.
3. Place the jar beside a flux rift. It takes the rift apart one point at a time and keeps that as power for any machine that uses magical power. It stops when the rift reaches its smallest size.
4. Also use it for the Rifting Ingot: put a Rift Jar that holds a rift touching a crucible (see the Rift Metals chapter).

## Tips and common mistakes

- A broken Rift Jar spills what it still holds back into the aura as flux.
