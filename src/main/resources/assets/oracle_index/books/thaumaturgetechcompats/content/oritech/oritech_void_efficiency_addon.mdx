> [!NOTE]

## What it is

The Void Metal Efficiency Addon is an addon block for Oritech machines, stronger than Oritech's own. It is placed against the machine like any Oritech addon.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Energy multiplier (lower is cheaper) | 0.2 |

## How to use it

1. Research Brass Addons, then Thaumium Addons, then Void Metal Addons (Tech tab).
2. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench around the addon of the tier before (Oritech's own addon for Brass).
3. Place it against the machine, in an addon slot, like any Oritech addon.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Void Metal Machine Addons
> Each addon is built around the thaumium addon of the same kind, with void metal plates, complex mechanisms and a Heisenberg compensator:
> - Speed: processes in 10% of the time and spends 90% of the energy.
> - Efficiency: spends 20% of the energy.
> - Capacitor: adds 1,000,000,000 of capacity and 1,000,000 of insert rate.
