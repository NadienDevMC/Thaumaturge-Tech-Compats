## What it is

The Portable Void Siphon pulls items and experience orbs towards you while you hold right click, and at the same time draws flux out of the aura of the chunk into its own store, which cleans the aura a little.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensional Flux stored | 2,000,000 |
| Radius | 8 blocks |
| Cost | 2,000 DF per second while held |

## How to use it

1. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench.
2. Hold right click: items and orbs within 8 blocks come to you.
3. It draws up to 0.5 aura flux per second (2,000 DF each) into itself.
4. Recharge it with an Environmental Charger when it runs dry.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Portable Void Siphon
> The Portable Void Siphon is made on an arcane workbench from three brass plates, a void seed and a simple mechanism.
> It is a portable tool that absorbs matter and residual energy from a distance. While right click is held down it pulls the items lying around and the experience orbs towards you, and it uses Dimensional Flux every second to do so. At the same time it draws the flux of the aura of the chunk and turns it into Dimensional Flux of its own, which also cleans the aura a little. Without charge it does nothing, and the Environmental Charger recharges it in your pack.
