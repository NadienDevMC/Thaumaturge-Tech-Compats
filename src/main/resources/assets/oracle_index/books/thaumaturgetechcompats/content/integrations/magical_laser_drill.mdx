## What it is

The Magical Laser Drill works like the Laser Drill of Industrial Foregoing, with the same screen painted purple, but its bar holds vis instead of power. Place it with its emitting face looking at any Laser Base, mundane or magical, inside its working area, and every operation spends 1 vis and gives a step of work to the base. The vis comes from the aura of its chunk and, if the aura cannot pay, from a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or a flux rift within reach; a hungry node never counts.

## How to get it

The Magical Laser Drill is born on an infusion altar from an ordinary Laser Drill. It looks and works like the original, with the same screen painted purple, but its bar holds vis instead of power.

Place it so that its emitting face looks at any Laser Base, mundane or magical. The base must be inside the area of three blocks per side centred two blocks in front of it, and the screen shows the target it found. Every operation spends 1 vis, which is worth the 1000 power of the original, and gives one step of work to the base.

The vis is taken from the aura of its own chunk and, when the aura cannot pay, from a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or a flux rift within reach. A hungry node is never a source. Redstone, addons and the settings of the machine work as in the original. The cost, the speed, the buffer and the sources can all be changed in its configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Laser drill and bases: place the Magical Laser Drill with its emitting face looking at any Laser Base (mundane or magical) inside the area of three blocks per side centred two blocks in front of it, and Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source. Every operation spends 1 vis. Put lenses and an efficiency addon in the base; take the output with hoppers or pipes.
4. Magical Infinity Drill: fill it with a Recharge Pedestal (and Liquid Death in its tank). Right click on air toggles charging; Shift and right click steps the mining area down. The more vis it holds the wider the area, up to a 13x13x13 cube.
