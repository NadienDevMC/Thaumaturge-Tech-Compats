## What it is

The Flight Thruster is a chestplate module that lets you fly in controlled bursts with Dimensional Flux. It cancels fall damage while it holds any DF.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Dimensional Flux stored | 200,000 |
| Cost | 800 DF per second of thrust |

## How to use it

1. Infuse it over a Thaumium Chestplate.
2. Wear it on the chest and keep an Environmental Charger near you to fill it.
3. Hold jump in the air to thrust upward, and forward to push ahead too.
4. Each burst lasts 50 ticks, then it cools down for 10 ticks.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Flight Thruster
> The Flight Thruster is infused from a thaumium chestplate, with three brass plates, two complex mechanisms, a void seed and two vis resonators.
> It is worn on the chest and holds Dimensional Flux, which an environmental charger can refill. While you hold jump in the air it thrusts you upwards, and forward as well if you hold the forward key, in bursts that need a moment to cool down. It also takes the fall out of any fall while it holds flux. Without flux it does nothing.
