## What it is

Tools and field devices powered by Dimensional Flux: the Portable Void Siphon, the Discharge Cannon, the Flight Thruster, the Shield Generator and the Arcane Terraformer.

## How to get it

The Flight Thruster is infused from a thaumium chestplate, with three brass plates, two complex mechanisms, a void seed and two vis resonators.

It is worn on the chest and holds Dimensional Flux, which an environmental charger can refill. While you hold jump in the air it thrusts you upwards, and forward as well if you hold the forward key, in bursts that need a moment to cool down. It also takes the fall out of any fall while it holds flux. Without flux it does nothing.

## Step by step

1. Charge every item with Dimensional Flux (DF): keep an Environmental Charger running near you, or use links and storages (see the Dimensional Flux Network).
2. Flight Thruster: wear it on the chest. Hold jump in the air to thrust upwards (and the forward key to push ahead) in bursts with a cooldown; it also stops falls while it holds flux.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Biomes and dimensions listed in the configuration are left alone by the terraformer. Without DF, none of these devices work. Higher tiers (Alchemical Brass, Void Metal) hold more flux and reach farther, at a bigger flux cost per tick.
