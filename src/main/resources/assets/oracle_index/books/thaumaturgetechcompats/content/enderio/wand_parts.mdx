## What it is

Ten wand rods and ten wand caps made from Ender IO materials, from redstone alloy to end steel, each stronger than the last.

## How to get it

The rod and the cap are separate parts, and any rod fits any cap. The materials, from weakest to strongest, are redstone alloy, conductive alloy, pulsating alloy, energetic alloy, dark steel, soularium, pulsating crystal, vibrant alloy, vibrant crystal and end steel.

- The first three are crafted at an arcane workbench: a rod from three ingots and a cap from five.
- Every other rod and cap is infused from the part of the material before it, with four ingots, or four crystals for the crystal ones.

Stronger materials hold more vis in the rod and make every spell cheaper with the cap, and each cap makes its own aspects cheaper still. The crystals, the vibrant alloy and the end steel glow. The exact values are set in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. From weakest to strongest the materials are: redstone alloy, conductive alloy, pulsating alloy, energetic alloy, dark steel, soularium, pulsating crystal, vibrant alloy, vibrant crystal and end steel.
3. The first three: craft a rod from three ingots and a cap from five at an arcane workbench.
4. Every other part: Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Start from the part of the material before it, with four ingots (or four crystals for the crystal ones).
5. Assemble a wand from any rod and any cap. Stronger materials hold more vis in the rod and cheapen every spell with the cap; each cap makes its own aspects cheaper still.
