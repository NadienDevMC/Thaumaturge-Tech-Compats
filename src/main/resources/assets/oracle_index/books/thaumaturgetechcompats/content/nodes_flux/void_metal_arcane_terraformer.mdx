## What it is

The Void Metal Arcane Terraformer repaints the biome of the area around it, column by column starting at its feet, to the biome of its **bait** (an item that stands for a biome: sand for a desert, snow for the tundra, a sapling for a forest).

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Radius | 64 blocks |
| Dimensional Flux stored | 20,000,000 |
| DF per 4x4 column | 1,000 |
| Columns per cycle | 16 |

## How to use it

1. Make it (the Thaumium one at the Arcane Workbench, the others infused around the one before).
2. Supply it with DF (Storage Core, Transmission Link or Rift Extractor touching it).
3. Right click it with the bait item. Sneak and right click with an empty hand to take the bait back.
4. Right click it again to read its progress, radius and state. Biomes and dimensions blocked in the config are left alone.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Void Metal Arcane Terraformer
> The Void Metal Arcane Terraformer is infused from an alchemical brass one, with four void metal plates, a complex mechanism, two vis resonators and a red mushroom.
> It repaints the widest area, the fastest and for the least flux per column.
