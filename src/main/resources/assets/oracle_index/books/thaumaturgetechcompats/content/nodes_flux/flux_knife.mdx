## What it is

The Flux Knife is a Farmer's Delight knife that bites into the world itself: sneak-use it on a block to open a **flux rift** beside it, sized to the flux it takes from the aura of the chunk. Food cut with it on a Cutting Board turns partly into flux in the air.

## How to use it

1. Research Flux Knife and infuse it over a Void Metal Knife.
2. Go to a chunk with flux in the aura and sneak-use the knife on a block: a rift opens beside it (it costs 10 durability and has a 10 second cooldown).
3. If the chunk does not have enough flux, nothing opens.
4. Put a Rift Jar or a Rift Extractor next to the rift to turn it into power.
5. It is still a normal knife for the Cutting Board and any knife recipe.
