> [!NOTE]
> Needs the **Mekanism** mod.

## What it is

The Energized Alchemical Brass Sword is a tool that runs on Mekanism energy instead of durability: it never loses durability and every action spends energy (a charge bar shows what is left). With no energy it mines like bare hands and deals no attack damage.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Energy capacity (Mekanism joules) | 1,000,000 |
| Energy per block mined | 500 |
| Energy per hit | 800 |
| Energy per strip, flatten or till | 400 |
| Mining speed | 8.0 |
| Attack damage bonus | 3.0 |
| Enchantability | 18 |

## How to use it

1. Research Energized Thaumium Tools, then Energized Alchemical Brass Tools, then Energized Void Metal Tools.
2. Infuse it as shown above. The Alchemical Brass and Void Metal tools also take the energized tool of the tier before, of the same kind, on a pedestal (it is consumed).
3. Charge it in an energy cube, a charge pad, the charging slot of a Mekanism machine or any other energy source.
4. Use it like a normal tool and recharge it when the bar empties.

> The plan is simple enough. A normal iron tool, the energized thaumium tool of the same kind, two energy tablets, an infused alloy, a basic control circuit and two alchemical brass plates, all together on an infusion altar.
> 
> **Energized Alchemical Brass Tools**
> 
> The infusion altar gives me an energized alchemical brass pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe or sword from the matching iron tool, and the energized thaumium tool is consumed in the process. They work like the thaumium ones: they lose no durability, they hold energy and every block mined, every hit and every stripped, flattened or tilled block spends some of it. With no energy left they are as useless as bare hands.
> 
> They hold more energy, spend less of it and work faster than the thaumium ones. The capacity, the cost of every action, the mining speed and the damage can be changed in the configuration.
