## What it is

Tools and field devices powered by Dimensional Flux: the Portable Void Siphon, the Discharge Cannon, the Flight Thruster, the Shield Generator and the Arcane Terraformer.

**Used to make (same family):** [Alchemical Brass Arcane Terraformer](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_arcane_terraformer)

## How to get it

The Thaumium Arcane Terraformer is made on an arcane workbench from four thaumium plates, grass, sand, two simple mechanisms and a vis resonator.

Right-click it with an item that stands for a biome, such as sand for a desert, snow for the tundra or a sapling for a forest, and it takes the flux of the network to repaint the area around it, column by column, starting at its feet. Sneak and right-click it with an empty hand to take the bait back. Biomes and dimensions listed in the configuration are left alone. Right-click it to read its progress.

## Step by step

1. Charge every item with Dimensional Flux (DF): keep an Environmental Charger running near you, or use links and storages (see the Dimensional Flux Network).
2. Arcane Terraformer: place it, give it DF, right-click with an item that stands for a biome (sand for desert, snow for tundra, a sapling for forest). It repaints the area column by column from its feet. Sneak right-click with an empty hand to take the bait back.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Biomes and dimensions listed in the configuration are left alone by the terraformer. Without DF, none of these devices work. Higher tiers (Alchemical Brass, Void Metal) hold more flux and reach farther, at a bigger flux cost per tick.
