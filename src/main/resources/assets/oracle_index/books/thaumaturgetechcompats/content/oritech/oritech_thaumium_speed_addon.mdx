> [!NOTE]

## What it is

The Thaumium Speed Addon is an addon block for Oritech machines, stronger than Oritech's own. It is placed against the machine like any Oritech addon.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Processing time multiplier (lower is faster) | 0.2 |
| Energy multiplier (lower is cheaper) | 1.0 |

## How to use it

1. Research Brass Addons, then Thaumium Addons, then Void Metal Addons (Tech tab).
2. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench around the addon of the tier before (Oritech's own addon for Brass).
3. Place it against the machine, in an addon slot, like any Oritech addon.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Thaumium Machine Addons
> Each addon is built around the brass addon of the same kind, with thaumium plates, complex mechanisms and an overcharged crystal:
> - Speed: processes in 20% of the time and spends 100% of the energy.
> - Efficiency: spends 40% of the energy.
> - Capacitor: adds 100,000,000 of capacity and 100,000 of insert rate.
