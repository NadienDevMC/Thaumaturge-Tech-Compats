## What it is

A Warded Jar of Crystal Matrix that stores far more essentia than the jar before it. It is infused on an infusion altar from an ordinary Warded Jar, with four crystal matrix ingots.

**Used to make (same family):** [Neutronium Jar](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_jar)

## How to get it

Each jar is made on an infusion altar, starting from a plain warded jar:

- The Crystal Matrix Jar is surrounded by crystal matrix ingots.

- The Neutronium Jar needs a Crystal Matrix Jar and neutron ingots.

- The Infinity Jar needs a Neutronium Jar, infinity ingots and an Extremely Primordial Pearl.

They work like any other warded jar, only larger. Labels and braces fit them as usual, and essentia is kept when the jar is picked up.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is a plain Warded Jar (the Crystal Matrix tier), then each tier on the one before it. Surround it with the ingots listed (crystal matrix, neutron, infinity plus an Extremely Primordial Pearl).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Use the jar as any warded jar: label it, brace it and fill it with essentia. Pick it up: the essentia and the aspect filter stay inside.
