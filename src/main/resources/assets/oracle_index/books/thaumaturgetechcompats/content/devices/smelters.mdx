## What it is

An Essentia Smeltery of Crystal Matrix, more efficient and quicker to hand over slurry than the smeltery before it; at Infinity no essentia is lost at all. It works like the other smelteries, with the same alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows.

## How to get it

Each smeltery is built on the one before it, with five plates and two ingots of its own material and an advanced alchemical construct:

- Crystal Matrix: 97% efficiency, hands slurry over every 8 ticks.
- Neutronium: 98.5% efficiency, every 5 ticks.
- Infinity: perfect efficiency, so no essentia is ever lost, every 2 ticks.

They work exactly like the other smelteries, with the same alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. The recipe is a five-plate, two-ingot pattern around an advanced alchemical construct, using the smeltery of the tier before it as base (the Void Metal Essentia Smeltery for Crystal Matrix).
3. Replace the smeltery core of an existing Essentia Smeltery structure with the new one, keeping the same alembics, slurry pumps, venting ports and bellows.
4. Feed it as you feed the others. The higher tiers lose less essentia to flux: 97%, 98.5% and 100% efficiency, handing slurry over every 8, 5 and 2 ticks.
