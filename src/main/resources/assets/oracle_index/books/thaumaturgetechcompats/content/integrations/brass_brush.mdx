## What it is

A Brass Brush for sand and gravel, used by hand or inside the Unearther of FTB Unearthed. It can unearth primal vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar.

**Used to make (same family):** [Thaumium Brush](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_brush)

## How to get it

The brass holds its edge and the bristles do not fray.

It is made at the arcane workbench from a feather, a brass plate and a stick. Used on sand or gravel, by hand or inside the Unearther of FTB Unearthed, it can unearth vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar. The odds and the durability are the lowest of the three brushes.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Brass Brush: a feather, a brass plate and a stick. Thaumium Brush: a brass brush, three thaumium plates and a feather. Void Metal Brush: a thaumium brush, three void metal plates and a feather.
3. Use a brush on sand or gravel, or put it in an Unearther: it can unearth vis crystals, amber and raw cinnabar (the higher the brush the better the odds, speed and durability).
4. Magical Unearthers: make a Thaumium Unearther at the arcane workbench from an Unearther core, four thaumium plates and two simple mechanisms (then Alchemical Brass and Void Metal on the previous core).
5. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source. It works without food and faster than the normal Unearther; without power it works at normal speed unless the settings say otherwise.
