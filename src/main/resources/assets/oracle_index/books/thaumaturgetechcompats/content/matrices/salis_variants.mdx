## What it is

Salis Mundus dusts folded with Avaritia materials: they wake the same structures as Salis Mundus but carry the weight of Blaze Cube, Crystal Matrix, Neutronium or Infinity.

## How to get it

Each variant is infused on an altar, starting from the previous dust, with four units of its material, and gives four dusts:

- The Blaze Cube Salis Mundus needs the Salis Mundus and four blaze cubes.
- The Crystal Matrix Salis Mundus needs the Blaze Cube Salis Mundus and four crystal matrix ingots.
- The Neutronium Salis Mundus needs the Crystal Matrix Salis Mundus and four neutronium ingots.
- The Infinity Salis Mundus needs the Neutronium Salis Mundus and four infinity ingots.

All of them work exactly like the Salis Mundus and wake the same structures.

The Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity ones can only be infused on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of their own tier, with the instability that matrix brings.

These dusts are the ones asked for by the recipes of the planetary pearls and star crucifixes of the blaze cube, crystal matrix, neutronium and infinity levels.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the previous dust (plain Salis Mundus for Blaze Cube), plus four units of the new material.
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item. Each infusion gives four dusts. Crystal Matrix, Neutronium and Infinity ones need an altar whose centre is the Runic Matrix of their own tier.
4. Use them exactly like Salis Mundus to wake the same structures; they are also required by the recipes of the higher planetary pearls and star crucifixes.
