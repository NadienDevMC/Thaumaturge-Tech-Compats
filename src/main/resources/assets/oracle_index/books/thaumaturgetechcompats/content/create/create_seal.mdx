## What it is

The Create Seal sits on a block of Create and lets a golem work it: it inserts the items the golem carries, takes items out, and can also click the block, which turns hand cranks and valve handles. The filter picks the items of every task, and each task can be switched off in the options of the seal.

## How to get it

The seal sits on a block of Create and lets a golem work it.

It inserts the items the golem carries into the block, takes items out of it, and can also click the block, which turns hand cranks and valve handles.

The filter picks the items for every task, and each task can be switched off in the seal's options.

### Create Seal

It is infused over a Use Seal with a cogwheel, a brass sheet, andesite alloy and a simple mechanism.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Use a Use Seal as the starting item, with a cogwheel, a brass sheet, andesite alloy and a simple mechanism.
3. Place the seal on a block of Create (belt, depot, basin, hand crank, valve handle...) like any golem seal.
4. Open the seal options: choose the filter (the items of each task) and switch off the tasks you do not want.
5. Golems then insert items into the block, take items out of it, or click it to turn hand cranks and valve handles.
