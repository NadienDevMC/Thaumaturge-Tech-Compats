## What it is

Tools and field devices powered by Dimensional Flux: the Portable Void Siphon, the Discharge Cannon, the Flight Thruster, the Shield Generator and the Arcane Terraformer.

## How to get it

The Discharge Cannon is made on an infusion altar from a complex mechanism, four void metal plates, two void seeds and two vis resonators.

It is a weapon that concentrates the stored energy to fire high impact projectiles. Hold right click to charge it and release to fire: the shot crosses the air up to its range, hurts what it hits hard and the creatures around the impact a little, and uses a fixed amount of Dimensional Flux. Without charge it will not fire, and it needs a short rest between shots.

## Step by step

1. Charge every item with Dimensional Flux (DF): keep an Environmental Charger running near you, or use links and storages (see the Dimensional Flux Network).
2. Discharge Cannon: hold right click to charge and release to fire; it spends a fixed amount of DF per shot, hurts what it hits hard and creatures around the impact a little, and needs a short rest between shots.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Biomes and dimensions listed in the configuration are left alone by the terraformer. Without DF, none of these devices work. Higher tiers (Alchemical Brass, Void Metal) hold more flux and reach farther, at a bigger flux cost per tick.
