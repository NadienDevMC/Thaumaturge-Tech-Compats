## How to get it

Both artifacts are made on an infusion altar:

- The Creative Node Placer is infused from a Node Jar, with infinity and neutronium plates, neutron ingots and primordial pearls.
- The Cheater's Thaumonomicon is infused from a Thaumonomicon, with the Akashic Records, an Extremely Primordial Pearl, infinity plates and infinity ingots.

Their power is the same as the ones from the creative world, so I should use them with care.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. For the Creative Node Placer the starting item is a Node Jar; for the Cheater's Thaumonomicon it is a Thaumonomicon.
3. Add the ingredients listed above (infinity and neutronium plates, neutron ingots and primordial pearls, or the Akashic Records, an Extremely Primordial Pearl, infinity plates and infinity ingots).
4. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
5. Use the Creative Node Placer to plant whole nodes in the aura, and the Cheater's Thaumonomicon to know every secret at once.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Their power equals the creative-world ones: use them with care in survival worlds.
