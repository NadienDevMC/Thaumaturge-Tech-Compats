## What it is

The tainted version of an ordinary food. It is brewed in a crucible filled with enough vitium, dropping the original food into it as the catalyst.

## How to get it

### Tainted Food

A little flux has soaked into the food.

#### Tainted Food

Fill a crucible with enough vitium and drop the food into it as the catalyst. Bread, apples, carrots, baked potatoes, steak, cooked porkchops, pumpkin pies and mushroom stew each have their own recipe, and so do the Ultimate Stew and the Cosmic Meatballs of Avaritia.

The tainted food fills more hunger and more saturation than the original and keeps its effects, but whoever eats it is struck by Flux Taint for a while.

### Tainted Farmer's Feasts

The soup smells wrong, and yet it smells wonderful.

#### Tainted Farmer's Feasts

Fill a crucible with enough vitium and drop the meal into it as the catalyst. Beef stew, chicken soup, vegetable soup, fish stew, baked cod stew, noodle soup, fried rice, ratatouille, steak and potatoes, hamburgers, bacon sandwiches, cooked bacon, beef patties and dumplings each have their own recipe.

A tainted meal fills more hunger and more saturation than the original and keeps its effects, but whoever eats it is struck by Flux Taint for a while.

#### The Cooking Pot

The Cooking Pot and the Cutting Board of Farmer's Delight accept tainted ingredients too. Tainted cooked beef, carrots and baked potatoes in the pot give a tainted beef stew, steak and potatoes or vegetable soup, and fried rice if there is rice, eggs and onion beside them. A knife on the Cutting Board turns tainted cooked beef into tainted beef patties and tainted cooked porkchops into tainted cooked bacon.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Fill a crucible with water, heat it, and add the aspects of the recipe, then throw the catalyst item into it. Fill it with enough vitium, and use the original food as the catalyst. Each food has its own recipe: bread, apple, carrot, baked potato, steak, cooked porkchop, pumpkin pie, mushroom stew, the Ultimate Stew and Cosmic Meatballs (Avaritia), and the Farmer's Delight meals.
3. Take the tainted food from the crucible.
4. Eat it: it fills more hunger and saturation than the original and keeps its effects, but you receive Flux Taint for a while.
5. With Farmer's Delight you can also use tainted ingredients in the Cooking Pot (tainted cooked beef, carrots and baked potatoes give tainted beef stew, steak and potatoes or vegetable soup; with rice, eggs and onion, fried rice) and a knife on the Cutting Board turns tainted cooked beef into tainted beef patties and tainted cooked porkchops into tainted cooked bacon.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Do not eat tainted food right before a long fight: the Flux Taint lasts a while. Purity potions and Sanity Soap are the remedies (see the Purity chapter).
