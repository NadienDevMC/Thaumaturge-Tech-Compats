## What it is

A Node Stabilizer of Crystal Matrix. Like the Advanced Node Stabilizer, nothing can pull from the node it holds, it can still pull from unprotected nodes nearby and a redstone signal switches it off. Its grip is stronger than the tier before: the node feeds on the aura far more slowly and unstable or fading nodes settle faster.

## How to get it

Each stabilizer is infused around the previous one, using plates and ingots of its own material alternating with nitor and alumentum:

- Crystal Matrix: around an Advanced Node Stabilizer.
- Neutronium: around the Crystal Matrix one.
- Infinity: around the Neutronium one.

All three hold a node the way an Advanced Node Stabilizer does: nothing can pull from it, it can still pull from unprotected nodes nearby, and a redstone signal switches them off. What changes is the grip:

- Crystal Matrix: the node feeds on the aura 50 times slower than normal, and unstable or fading nodes settle 3 times as fast as with the basic stabilizer.
- Neutronium: 200 times slower, and 4 times as fast.
- Infinity: 1000 times slower, and 5 times as fast. The node practically stops feeding.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is the previous stabilizer (an Advanced Node Stabilizer for Crystal Matrix).
3. Add plates and ingots of the new material alternating with nitor and alumentum, as the recipe shows.
4. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
5. Place the stabilizer where you would place an Advanced Node Stabilizer, over a node. Nothing can pull from the node it holds, a redstone signal switches it off, and it can still pull from unprotected nodes nearby.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Grip strength: the node feeds on the aura 50, 200 and 1000 times slower, and unstable or fading nodes settle 3, 4 and 5 times faster (Crystal Matrix, Neutronium, Infinity).
