## What it is

The Akashic Records, a record that holds every thought that has ever been thought. Opening it floods the mind with every aspect there is, hundreds of points of each, and then a little more.

## How to get it

The Akashic Records are complete. Opening them floods the mind with every aspect there is, hundreds of points of each, and then a little more.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it.
3. Use the pure infinity ingots the recipe asks for around the starting item.
4. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
5. Right-click the finished Akashic Records: it is spent and gives you hundreds of points of every aspect in your research knowledge.
