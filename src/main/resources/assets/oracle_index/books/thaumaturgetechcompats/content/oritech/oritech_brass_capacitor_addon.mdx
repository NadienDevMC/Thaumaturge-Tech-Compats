## What it is

The Brass Capacitor Addon is an addon block for Oritech machines, stronger than Oritech's own. It is placed against the machine like any Oritech addon.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Energy capacity added | 10,000,000 |
| Insert rate added | 10,000 |

## How to use it

1. Research Brass Addons, then Thaumium Addons, then Void Metal Addons (Tech tab).
2. Craft it at the Arcane Workbench around the addon of the tier before (Oritech's own addon for Brass).
3. Place it against the machine, in an addon slot, like any Oritech addon.

## Used to make

- [](@thaumaturgetechcompats:oritech_thaumium_capacitor_addon)

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Brass Machine Addons
> Each addon is built at the arcane workbench around the Oritech addon of the same kind, with brass plates, simple mechanisms and a component of Oritech:
> - Speed: processes in 40% of the time (Oritech's addon: 50%) and spends 110% of the energy.
> - Efficiency: spends 60% of the energy (Oritech's addon: 80%).
> - Capacitor: adds 10,000,000 of capacity and 10,000 of insert rate to the machine.
> They are placed against the machine like any other addon. Every value can be changed in the configuration.
