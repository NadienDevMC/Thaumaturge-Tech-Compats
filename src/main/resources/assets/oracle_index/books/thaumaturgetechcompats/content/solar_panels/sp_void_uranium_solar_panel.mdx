## What it is

The panel glows with a light that is not the sun's.

**Made from (same family):** [Void Metal Solar Panel](@solarflux:sp_void_metal)

## How to get it

It is infused over a void metal solar panel using void metal plates, uranium ingots, the finest photovoltaic cells and a complex mechanism. It needs All The Ores, and with Mekanism installed it irradiates whoever carries it.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Variants: Brass Andesite (needs Create), Thaumium Zinc (needs Create or All The Ores), Alchemical Cobalt (needs Hephaestus Forge) and Void Uranium (needs All The Ores) are crafted or infused as listed above.
3. Place the panel in the open sun like any Solar Flux panel and connect it to your energy network.

## Tips and common mistakes

- The Void Uranium panel irradiates whoever carries it when Mekanism is installed.
