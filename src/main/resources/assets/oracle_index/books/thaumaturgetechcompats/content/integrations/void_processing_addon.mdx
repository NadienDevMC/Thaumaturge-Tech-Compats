## What it is

A Void Metal Processing Addon for the machines of Industrial Foregoing: it multiplies what the machine processes at once, and is stronger than the diamond original and than the tier before it.

**Made from (same family):** [Thaumium Processing Addon](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_processing_addon)

## How to get it

The chips are dark, cold and impossibly quick.

Each one is made in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber from the thaumium addon of the same kind, three void metal plates, a complex mechanism, the material of the addon, an eye of ender and a great deal of Liquid Death.

### Dissolution Chamber

| Ingredient | Amount |
|---|---|
| Thaumium Processing Addon | 1 |
| Void Metal Plate | 3 |
| Complex Arcane Mechanism | 1 |
| Furnace | 1 |
| Crafting Table | 1 |
| Eye of Ender | 1 |
| Liquid Death | 4000 mB |

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting item: an ordinary Laser Drill (Magical Laser Drill), Ore Laser Base plus a diamond pickaxe (Ore Base), Fluid Laser Base plus a bucket (Fluid Base) or an Infinity Drill (Magical Infinity Drill).
3. Addons: craft each in an Industrial Foregoing dissolution chamber (Brass, Thaumium, Void Metal tiers) and put it in the addon slots of a machine, or use it on the machine directly.
