## What it is

The Create Seal is a golem seal that lets a golem work a block of Create: insert the items it carries, take items out, and (optionally) click the block to turn hand cranks and valve handles.

## How to use it

1. Research Create Seal (Tech tab) and infuse it over a Use Seal.
2. Place it on the face of a block of Create (by default it cannot go on other blocks).
3. Open the seal: set the filter (9 slots) and the options Insert, Extract, Operate (click) and Sneaky click.
4. Give it a golem with the Deft trait (a Clumsy golem cannot use it).
5. With Extract on it looks every 20 ticks for a matching item; with Operate on the golem clicks every 8 ticks.
6. A redstone signal stops the seal.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Create Seal
> The seal sits on a block of Create and lets a golem work it.
> It inserts the items the golem carries into the block, takes items out of it, and can also click the block, which turns hand cranks and valve handles.
> The filter picks the items for every task, and each task can be switched off in the seal's options.
> 
> **Create Seal**
