## What it is

The Thaumium Mana Spreader holds far more mana and throws its bursts harder than the tier before it. It works like any other spreader, takes lenses and can be bound with the Wand of the Forest.

**Used to make (same family):** [Alchemical Brass Mana Spreader](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_spreader)

## How to get it

Every spreader is made on an infusion altar, starting from the one before it:

- The Thaumium Mana Spreader needs a Gaia Spreader, four thaumium plates and a Simple Arcane Mechanism.

- The Alchemical Brass Mana Spreader needs the Thaumium one, four alchemical brass plates and a Simple Arcane Mechanism.

- The Void Metal Mana Spreader needs the Alchemical Brass one, four void metal plates and a Void Seed.

They work like any other spreader, take lenses and can be bound with the Wand of the Forest. How much mana a burst carries, how much the spreader holds, how far and how fast the burst flies and what colour it has is set in the configuration for each tier.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. Starting items: a Gaia Spreader (Thaumium), then the previous spreader for Alchemical Brass and Void Metal, with four plates of the new metal and a Simple Arcane Mechanism (a Void Seed for Void Metal).
3. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
4. Place the spreader like any Botania spreader, bind it with the Wand of the Forest and add a lens if you want.
