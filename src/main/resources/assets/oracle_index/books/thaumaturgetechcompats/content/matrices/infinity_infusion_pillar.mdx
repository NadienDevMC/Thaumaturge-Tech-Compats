## What it is

An Infusion Pillar of Infinity, raised by sprinkling Salis Mundus over its stones. The altar is built exactly like the plain one: eight stones, a pedestal and a Runic Matrix of any kind. The look of the pillar follows the stone used.

## How to get it

The infusion pillar stones are made at the arcane workbench, four at a time, from ingots and the stones of the tier before: the Crystal Matrix ones start from Eldritch Stone Tiles, the Neutronium ones from the Crystal Matrix ones and the Infinity ones from the Neutronium ones, with an Extremely Primordial Pearl.

The pedestals are infused from the pedestal of the tier before, starting from the Eldritch Pedestal, on an altar whose center is the Runic Matrix of their own tier.

The altar is built exactly like the plain one: eight stones, a pedestal and a Runic Matrix of any kind. Salis Mundus turns the stones into pillars of the same tier, and the look of the pillars follows the stone used.

Each tier lowers the cost and the cycle time of the altar more than the one before, and the pillars also add stability for the plain Runic Matrix. The exact values are set in the configuration.

It has no recipe of its own: the family guide and the steps below explain how to get it.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Craft pillar stones at an arcane workbench, four at a time: Crystal Matrix ones from Eldritch Stone Tiles, Neutronium ones from the Crystal Matrix ones and Infinity ones from the Neutronium ones (with an Extremely Primordial Pearl).
3. Infuse the pedestals from the pedestal of the tier before, on an altar centred on the Runic Matrix of their own tier.
4. Build the altar exactly like the plain one: eight stones, a pedestal and a Runic Matrix of any kind.
5. Sprinkle Salis Mundus over the stones to raise the pillars of the same tier; the look follows the stone used. Each tier lowers the cost and cycle time more than the one before and adds stability for the plain Runic Matrix.
