## What it is

A Alchemical Brass Miner Frame, the block the structure of a miner of this tier is built from, together with glass panels and modifiers.

**Made from (same family):** [Thaumium Miner Frame](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_miner_frame)

**Used to make (same family):** [Void Metal Miner Frame](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_miner_frame)

## How to get it

The Alchemical Brass Miner Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Thaumium Miner Controller, alchemical brass plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from alchemical brass plates, obsidian and the matching Thaumium frame. The structure is built out of these frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, and it takes no energy at all: it works only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, and stops the moment it cannot. A hungry node is never a valid source.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Make the frames from plates of the metal, obsidian and a base frame (or the previous tier frame). Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The controller starts from a Rubetine Miner (Thaumium) or the previous controller, with plates, mechanisms and Flux Jars.
3. Build the structure from frames, glass panels and any miner modifier, as in Void Miners.
4. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source.
5. The miner takes no energy: it works only while it can pay units to the source, and stops when it cannot. Add upgrades in free slots: their effects multiply with each other and with the Void Miners modifiers.

## Tips and common mistakes

- Chat messages and tooltips tell you the cost in aura units, the source in use and why the miner stopped (no source, exhausted source or a hungry node).
