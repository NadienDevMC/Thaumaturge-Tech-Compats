## What it is

A plate of Infinity, pressed from its ingots: three ingots in a crafting grid make three plates, and it also needs a Neutronium Plate. It is the base of a golem of that material in the Golem Press, together with a Complex Arcane Mechanism, and an ingredient of wand caps, wand rods and improved foci.

**Made from (same family):** [Neutronium Plate](@thaumaturgetechcompats:neutronium_plate)

## How to get it

Three ingots pressed in a crafting grid make three plates. Neutronium plates also need a Crystal Matrix Plate, and infinity plates a Neutronium Plate.

- Infinity: the sturdiest of all, brutal, armored, fireproof, blastproof and self-repairing.

Each plate is the base of a golem in the Golem Press, together with a Complex Arcane Mechanism.

The plates are also ingredients for the wand caps and rods and for the improved foci of this chapter.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Craft the plates in an ordinary crafting grid: three ingots make three plates. Neutronium plates also need a Crystal Matrix Plate, and infinity plates a Neutronium Plate (the recipe above is the Infinity one: three infinity ingots over a neutronium plate).
3. Take a plate and a Complex Arcane Mechanism to the Golem Press: it makes a golem of that material.
4. The golems: Crystal Matrix is light, fireproof and blastproof; Neutronium is heavy, armored, fireproof and blastproof; Infinity is brutal, armored, fireproof, blastproof and self-repairing.
5. Keep some plates: they are also ingredients of the wand caps and rods and of the improved foci.
