## What it is

The Thaumium Casing is the next step of the Create machinery: its variants reach nodes from farther away than the alchemical brass ones.

**Made from (same family):** [Alchemical Brass Casing](@thaumaturgetechcompats:alchemical_brass_casing)

**Used to make (same family):** [Thaumium Basin](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_basin), [Thaumium Deployer](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_deployer), [Thaumium Depot](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_depot), [Thaumium Item Drain](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_item_drain), [Thaumium Mechanical Drill](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_mechanical_drill), [Thaumium Mechanical Mixer](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_mechanical_mixer), [Thaumium Mechanical Press](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_mechanical_press), [Thaumium Mechanical Saw](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_mechanical_saw), [Thaumium Millstone](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_millstone), [Thaumium Spout](@thaumaturgetechcompats:thaumium_spout), [Void Metal Casing](@thaumaturgetechcompats:void_metal_casing)

## How to get it

The thaumium variants reach nodes from farther away than the alchemical brass ones.

It is infused over an alchemical brass casing with four thaumium plates and a simple mechanism.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Craft the Alchemical Brass Casing at an arcane workbench from a Brass Casing of Create and four brass plates; infuse the Thaumium Casing over it and the Void Metal Casing over the Thaumium one.
3. Infuse a variant of a Create machine (press, mixer, saw, drill, deployer, millstone, basin, spout, item drain or depot) over the matching Create machine with the casing and plates listed above.
4. Place the variant in your normal Create setup; it works exactly like the original.
5. Put an aura node close by: while a node is near the variant works many times faster (Thaumium reaches nodes from farther than Alchemical Brass, Void Metal from farthest). Away from every node it behaves as the plain machine.
