## What it is

Tainted Carrot is a copy of an ordinary food that fills **more hunger and more saturation** than the original and keeps its effects, but whoever eats it is struck by **Flux Taint** for a while.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Original food | Carrot |
| Hunger and saturation | those of the original × 1.5 (by default) |
| Effects of the original | kept |
| Side effect | Flux Taint I for 10 seconds, every time (by default) |
| Vitium needed in the crucible | 6 |

## How to get it

### Crucible

| Recipe | |
| --- | --- |
| Station | Crucible with water, over heat |
| Item to drop in (catalyst) | Carrot |
| Essentia in the crucible | Vitium 6 |
| Research | **Tainted Food** (Tainted Food tab) |
| Makes | 1 |

## How to use it

1. Research Tainted Food (or Tainted Farmer's Feasts for the Farmer's Delight dishes).
2. Fill a crucible with the Vitium of the recipe (dissolve items that carry Vitium).
3. Drop the original food in as the catalyst and collect the tainted one. Some dishes can also be made from tainted ingredients in the Cooking Pot or on the Cutting Board (recipes above).
4. Eat it when you can afford the Flux Taint.
