## What it is

The Alchemical Brass Casing is the base of the Alchemical Brass variants of the Create machines of this mod. It does nothing on its own: you spend one, together with the machine of the tier before, to infuse a variant that runs many times faster near an aura node.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Node reach (default) | 6 blocks |

## How to use it

1. Research the machinery entry of its tier (Tech tab): Alchemical Brass Machinery, then Thaumium Machinery, then Void Metal Machinery.
2. Make the casing (the Alchemical Brass one at the Arcane Workbench, the others infused over the casing of the tier before).
3. Keep one casing for every machine variant of that tier you want: each variant consumes one.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Alchemical Brass Machinery
> The casing hums beside a node. Built into a machine of Create it makes a variant of that machine: a mechanical press, mixer, saw or drill, a deployer, millstone, basin, spout, item drain or depot that works exactly like the original.
> While an aura node is near, the variant works many times faster. Away from every node it behaves as the plain machine.
> 
> **Alchemical Brass Casing**
> 
> It is made at the arcane workbench from a brass casing of Create and four brass plates.
> 
> **Machine variants**
> 
> Each variant is infused over the matching machine of Create, with an alchemical brass casing and two brass plates.
