## What it is

The Alchemical Brass Transmission Link moves Dimensional Flux between devices that never touch. Two links tuned to each other form a visible beam; flux flows both ways until both are equally full, and each link also takes flux from the storages next to it and gives it to those that can receive it. Links side by side pass flux along, so chains are possible.

## How to use it

1. Make two links of the same tier (the Thaumium one at the Arcane Workbench, two per craft).
2. Use the Transmission Linker on the first link, then on the second: they pair.
3. Keep the pair within range (the shorter range of the two) and both ends loaded.
4. Right click a link to read its flux, partner, distance, rate and status.
5. To break a pair, sneak and use the linker on a link.
