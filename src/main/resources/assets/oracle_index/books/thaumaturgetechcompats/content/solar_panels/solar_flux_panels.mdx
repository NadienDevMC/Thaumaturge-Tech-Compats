## How to get it

### Alchemical Solar Panel

The crucible has dissolved the sun into the panel. The Sol it holds is tame, and the panel now generates far more energy than it ever did.

#### Alchemical Solar Panel

Dropping a tier 8 solar panel into a crucible filled with Sol and Machina turns it into an alchemical solar panel. If Twilight Forest is installed the catalyst is its tier 2 panel (fiery), and if Draconic Evolution is installed it is the chaotic panel.

### Brass Solar Panel

The brass plating channels the light into the panel without losing it.

#### Brass Solar Panel

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from photovoltaic cells, brass plates and an alchemical solar panel.

### Thaumium Solar Panel

The infusion altar has fused the plates and cells into a single, steady source of power.

#### Thaumium Solar Panel

It is infused over a brass solar panel using thaumium plates, advanced photovoltaic cells and a simple mechanism.

### Void Metal Solar Panel

The panel drinks light that would otherwise vanish into nothing.

#### Void Metal Solar Panel

It is infused over a thaumium solar panel using void metal plates, the finest photovoltaic cells and a complex mechanism.

### Brass Andesite Solar Panel

The alloy and the brass have settled into a plain, hardy panel.

#### Brass Andesite Solar Panel

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from photovoltaic cells, andesite alloy, brass ingots and an alchemical solar panel. It needs Create.

### Thaumium Zinc Solar Panel

Zinc and thaumium have been drawn into a single, even plating.

#### Thaumium Zinc Solar Panel

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from advanced photovoltaic cells, thaumium plates, zinc ingots and an alchemical solar panel. It needs Create or All The Ores.

### Alchemical Cobalt Solar Panel

The cobalt casing has sealed the alchemy inside.

#### Alchemical Cobalt Solar Panel

It is crafted at the arcane workbench from photovoltaic cells, cobalt ingots and an alchemical solar panel. It needs Hephaestus Forge.

### Void Uranium Solar Panel

The panel glows with a light that is not the sun's.

#### Void Uranium Solar Panel

It is infused over a void metal solar panel using void metal plates, uranium ingots, the finest photovoltaic cells and a complex mechanism. It needs All The Ores, and with Mekanism installed it irradiates whoever carries it.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Alchemical: drop a tier 8 Solar Flux panel into a crucible filled with Sol and Machina (with Twilight Forest the catalyst is its tier 2 panel, with Draconic Evolution the chaotic one).
3. Brass: at the arcane workbench craft photovoltaic cells, brass plates and an alchemical panel. Thaumium and Void Metal: infuse over the previous panel with plates, advanced or finest photovoltaic cells and mechanisms.
4. Variants: Brass Andesite (needs Create), Thaumium Zinc (needs Create or All The Ores), Alchemical Cobalt (needs Hephaestus Forge) and Void Uranium (needs All The Ores) are crafted or infused as listed above.
5. Place the panel in the open sun like any Solar Flux panel and connect it to your energy network.

## Tips and common mistakes

- The Void Uranium panel irradiates whoever carries it when Mekanism is installed.
