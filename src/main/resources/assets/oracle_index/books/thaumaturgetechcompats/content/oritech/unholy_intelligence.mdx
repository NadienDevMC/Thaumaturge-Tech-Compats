## How to get it

At the arcane workbench, a Brain in a Jar, a clockwork mind, two complex mechanisms, four thaumium plates and a Dubious Container of Oritech become an Unholy Intelligence, the same logic core that Oritech obtains by capturing an Allay or from the centrifuge.

The workbench needs the vis and the order and entropy crystals shown in the recipe. The recipe can be turned off in the configuration.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Use a Brain in a Jar, a clockwork mind, two complex mechanisms, four thaumium plates and a Dubious Container of Oritech, with the vis and the order and entropy crystals shown in the recipe.
3. Take the Unholy Intelligence: it is the same logic core Oritech gets by capturing an Allay or from the centrifuge.

## Tips and common mistakes

- The recipe can be turned off in the configuration.
