## What it is

A Blank Crystal Matrix Focus, an improved focus that holds a bigger spell than any focus before it.

## How to get it

All three are infused on top of a Greater Focus or another improved focus.

- Crystal Matrix Focus: a Greater Focus, two crystal matrix ingots and two crystal matrix plates. Holds a bigger spell than any focus before it.
- Infinity Focus: a Crystal Matrix Focus, two infinity ingots, two infinity plates and two neutronium plates. The most capable focus there is.
- Blaze Cube Focus: a Greater Focus, two blaze cubes and two crystal matrix plates. Not as deep as the matrix focus, but it fans out into many more branches and repeats, which suits a barrage of fire.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The starting item is a Greater Focus (or the previous improved focus).
3. Add the plates, ingots or blaze cubes shown above; the Infinity Focus needs a Crystal Matrix Focus underneath.
4. Supply the essentia the recipe asks for (the recipe screen in JEI or the Thaumonomicon lists every aspect), start the infusion and wait for the cycle to end; the result replaces the starting item.
5. Open the blank focus in a focal manipulator as you would any focus and weave your spell into it: the Crystal Matrix one holds a bigger spell, the Infinity one is the most capable, the Blaze Cube one fans out into many more branches and repeats.
