## What it is

The Void Metal Solar Panel Controller is the controller of a Void Miners solar panel that makes FE **without sun or open sky**: it generates only while it can pay units of power to a magical source nearby.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| FE per tick | 6,000 |
| Energy stored (FE) | 500,000,000 |
| Units paid every 20 ticks | 4 |
| Power source radius | 16 blocks |
| Modifier slots of its structure | 6 |

## How to use it

1. Research the Magical Solar Panel entry of its tier and infuse it over the controller of the tier before (the Rubetine Solar Panel for Thaumium).
2. Craft the Void Metal solar frames and build the solar structure of the tier around the controller.
3. Put a power source within the radius (jarred node, Flux Jar, Rift Jar, world node or flux rift; never a hungry node).
4. Connect a cable or an FE consumer as with any Void Miners solar panel. Every 20 ticks it pays and generates.

> [!NOTE]- Thaumonomicon: Void Metal Solar Panel
> The Void Metal Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller, void metal plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes, a void seed.
> Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from void metal plates, obsidian and the matching Alchemical Brass frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.
