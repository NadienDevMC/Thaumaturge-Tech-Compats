## What it is

The Thaumium Solar Panel Controller generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.

## How to get it

### Thaumium Solar Panel

The Thaumium Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from a Rubetine Solar Panel, thaumium plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from thaumium plates, obsidian and a base frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Alchemical Brass Solar Panel

The Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Thaumium Solar Panel Controller, alchemical brass plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from alchemical brass plates, obsidian and the matching Thaumium frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Void Metal Solar Panel

The Void Metal Solar Panel Controller is made on an infusion altar from the Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Controller, void metal plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes, a void seed.

Its frames are made on an arcane workbench from void metal plates, obsidian and the matching Alchemical Brass frame. It generates energy only while it can pay units to a magical power source close by, with no need for sun or open sky. A hungry node is never a valid source.

### Thaumium Solar Panel Upgrades

Every Thaumium upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the matching real Void Miners upgrade, a Rubetine solar upgrade, two thaumium mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the solar panel pays to its power source.

### Alchemical Brass Solar Panel Upgrades

Every Alchemical Brass upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the same upgrade of the Thaumium tier, two alchemical brass mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the solar panel pays to its power source.

### Void Metal Solar Panel Upgrades

Every Void Metal upgrade is made on an arcane workbench from the same upgrade of the Alchemical Brass tier, two void metal mechanisms and two plates.

Place them in the free slots of the structure. Their effects multiply with each other and with the real Void Miners modifiers, and the cost ones lower the aura units the solar panel pays to its power source.

## Step by step

1. Complete the matching research entry in the Thaumonomicon (Avaritia, Tech or Purity tab); without it the recipe stays locked.
2. Open an arcane workbench, place the ingredients in the pattern of the recipe, and charge it with vis (a wand, or a workbench charger) and with the crystals the recipe asks for. Make the frames from plates of the metal, obsidian and a base frame (or the previous frame). Build an infusion altar: a Runic Matrix, a central pedestal and the eight pillar stones around it. Put the starting item on the central pedestal and the other ingredients on the pedestals around it. The controller starts from a Rubetine Solar Panel (Thaumium) or the previous controller, with plates, mechanisms and jarred nodes.
3. Build the structure from frames, glass panels and any solar modifier.
4. Place a magical power source within reach of the machine: a node, a jarred node, a Flux Jar, a Rift Jar or an open flux rift. A hungry node is never accepted as a source.
5. It generates energy only while it can pay units to the source, with no need for sun or open sky. Upgrades go in free slots and multiply with the others.
